# LizOn Education — SEO and AI discovery project
Baseline date: 2026-10-27
Status: research/staging branch, not production.

## Objective
Become a credible, useful answer source for Bangladesh IELTS, PTE and Study Abroad student questions, then improve qualified consultations and enrollments. No ranking guarantees.

## Initial baseline
Search Console (last 3 months selected): 188 impressions, 5 clicks, approximately 2.7% CTR. Data volume too low for stable average ranking conclusions.

## Architecture audit
Repository includes 105 top-level HTML files. Audit active, retired, administrative and duplicate pages before publishing more. Retain working free class portals and approved design.
Current sitemap has 8 listed URLs. Review actual active canonical URLs.

## Research categories
- IELTS Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking (each question type, worked example, mistakes, teacher explainer).
- IELTS decisions (Academic/GT/UKVI, Bangladesh booking, score and preparation).
- PTE Speaking & Writing, Reading, Listening (all 22 question types including Summarize Group Discussion and Respond to a Situation).
- Study Abroad country choice, costs, eligibility, visas, scholarships, university/program matching, SOP/LOR, student profiles and arrival.

## Safeguards
- No hidden keyword stuffing or cloaking.
- Claims, testimonials and results only if verified.
- Structured data must reflect real public content.
- Verify all visa/fees/scholarship information from official sources and show reviewed date.
- No automatic restoration of archived pages.
- Production changes require testing, with existing UX preserved.
