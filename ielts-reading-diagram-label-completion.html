IELTS Reading Diagram Label Completion Guide

Diagram label completion requires words from a reading passage to fill labels on a diagram. The diagram may show a mechanism, building, process or another illustrated subject.

ডায়াগ্রাম লেবেলিং-এ passage থেকে সঠিক শব্দ নিয়ে ছবির অংশে বসাতে হয়। Word limit অবশ্যই মানতে হবে।

Banglish question: ielts reading diagram labelling kivabe korbo — learn the method below.

Step-by-step strategy

  1. Read the word-limit instruction before answering.
  2. Identify the relevant section by matching the diagram’s features to ideas in the passage.
  3. Read carefully for relationships between components; don't rely on the diagram alone.
  4. Copy the exact required words and check spelling and word limits.

Original worked example

Text: 'The filter sits immediately above the water tank and captures larger particles.' Diagram label: A component directly above the water tank.

Reveal answer and explanation

filter. The passage identifies the component and its location explicitly.

Common mistakes

Do not guess from a matching keyword alone. Read or listen for meaning, obey task instructions and check the context before finalizing the answer. A strategy is not a score guarantee.
