CZECH REPUBLIC · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Czechia
from Bangladesh.

Czechia offers strong public universities, English-taught degrees, free tuition at public and state universities when studying in Czech, direct student access to the labour market, and a 9-month post-graduation transition route. For Bangladeshi students, long-term study applications are handled through the Czech Embassy in New Delhi using a scheduled registration system.

Czechia in one view

Strong fit for students who want high-quality Central European education, especially if they value work access, lower-cost public options and a meaningful graduate transition period.

Bangladesh study appointments are handled by the Czech Embassy in New Delhi

The Embassy’s 2026 system requires Bangladeshi study applicants to register by email only during specified appointment windows. The Embassy published a quota of 360 study applications for Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal and Sri Lanka combined in 2026.

ADMISSION

Admission combines university selection with recognition of previous education.

Universities set their own entrance requirements, but foreign qualifications may also need Czech recognition or nostrification depending on the institution and level.

Bachelor

Completed secondary education

Bangladeshi applicants normally submit SSC and HSC documents, subject to recognition, translation and faculty-specific admission rules.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Programme relevance, academic results, entrance exams, portfolio or interview requirements vary by faculty.

PhD

Graduate degree + research fit

Doctoral applicants usually need a relevant Master degree, research proposal and faculty or supervisor approval.

Some universities with institutional accreditation can recognise foreign secondary education directly within their own admission procedure. That recognition may be valid only for that university and faculty.

INTAKES

September is the main intake, but application deadlines vary widely.

Many programmes start in September or October. Competitive universities and faculties often close applications much earlier than the semester start.

MAIN INTAKE

September / October

Broadest choice across Bachelor, Master and PhD study.

SECONDARY

February, selected

Available at some universities and graduate programmes, but not a universal national intake.

Bangladeshi students should plan earlier than the final university deadline because study appointment registration in New Delhi is capacity-controlled and the long-term study decision can take up to 60 days.
CZECH · ENGLISH

Language choice completely changes the tuition strategy.

Public and state higher education in Czech is tuition-free for all nationalities, while foreign-language programmes usually charge tuition.

Czech-taught public studyTuition-freeAdmission and administrative fees may still apply.
English-taught programmesUniversity-specific proofIELTS, TOEFL or faculty tests are common.
Preparatory routeCzech language coursesUseful for students targeting tuition-free Czech study.
Exact levelProgramme decidesNo one national IELTS score for all degrees.
Studying in Czech can reduce tuition dramatically, but reaching the required academic language level usually takes significant preparation.
LIVING COST + FUNDS

Living costs are moderate for Central Europe, but Prague is not the same as smaller cities.

Official Study in Czechia materials show student dormitory and shared-flat costs well below many Western European capitals, while the immigration proof-of-funds formula is calculated separately in CZK.

TUITION

Czechia has one of Europe’s clearest language-based tuition models.

At public and state higher education institutions, Czech-language degree study is free for students of all nationalities. Foreign-language programmes set their own tuition.

Czech-language public study

No tuition fee

Students still need to budget for admission fees, recognition, accommodation and living expenses.

Most foreign-language programmes

0–€6,000 / year

Official Study in Czechia material says 94% of listed foreign-language tuition fees fell in this range in the referenced national dataset.

Arts / medicine

~€8,500–19,000 / year

Selected arts and medical programmes can sit substantially above the general English-taught range.

Exact 2026 fees must always be checked on the programme page. The national ranges are useful for planning, not a substitute for an offer letter.

STUDY VISA / RESIDENCE · BANGLADESH

Bangladeshi applicants submit long-term study applications through New Delhi.

For stays over 90 days, third-country students use a Czech long-term visa or long-term residence permit for study, depending on the programme and intended duration.

01

Secure university admission

Obtain the official confirmation of study or enrolment required for the immigration application.

02

Prepare recognition + documents

Complete any required nostrification, legalisation and Czech translation before submission.

03

Watch the New Delhi registration window

The Embassy publishes specific registration dates for Bangladeshi study applicants.

04

Register by email correctly

The 2026 system required passport number as the subject plus passport and proof-of-study PDFs.

05

Attend in person in New Delhi

Long-term applications are submitted personally at the Embassy.

06

Ministry of Interior decision

Study long-term visa and residence applications are generally processed within up to 60 days, subject to suspension or complications.

07

Travel to Czechia

Enter with the issued long-term visa or residence authorization.

08

Complete local residence formalities

Follow the university, Foreign Police and Ministry of Interior requirements after arrival.

Bangladeshi civil and academic documents can require legalisation and Czech translation. Build document preparation into the timeline rather than waiting until the appointment window opens.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Family reunification exists, but it is a separate residence process.

Eligible family members use the Czech long-term family reunification route. The New Delhi Embassy operates a separate appointment system for family applications.

Separate application

Family does not automatically inherit student status

Residence eligibility, accommodation, relationship evidence and household income are assessed under family rules.

Timing

Can take much longer

The Embassy lists a statutory processing deadline of up to 270 days for long-term residence applications for family reunification.

New Delhi family reunification appointments ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Accredited full-time university students have free access to the labour market.

Third-country students studying full-time in an accredited Czech university programme generally do not need a separate work permit for employment.

Work permit

Not required for qualifying students

Your valid study residence plus accredited full-time university status provides free labour-market access.

No simple 20-hour cap

Employment is not defined by a national weekly student limit

The key condition is lawful student residence and qualifying accredited study, not a UK-style fixed weekly allowance.

Academic priority

Study purpose must remain genuine

Even with labour-market access, you must continue fulfilling the purpose of your study residence.

Non-accredited courses or residence statuses classified as “other educational activities” can follow different work rules. Check your exact programme status before accepting employment.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Comprehensive medical insurance is part of the residence process.

Third-country students must maintain health coverage meeting Czech immigration requirements unless they fall under a qualifying public-insurance arrangement.

Before arrival

Immigration-compliant insurance

Use the policy type required for the long-term visa or residence stage.

During study

Keep coverage valid

Insurance can be requested again during residence processes or extensions.

AFTER GRADUATION

Czechia gives eligible graduates up to 9 months to find work or start a business.

Graduates of accredited Czech university programmes can apply for a long-term residence permit specifically for seeking employment or starting a business.

Validity

Up to 9 months

The graduate job-search or business residence permit is issued for up to nine months and cannot be extended.

Labour market

Free access continues

Qualifying graduates on this permit can work, seek employment or start a business without a separate job-specific work permit.

You must apply before your current study residence expires. The nine-month permit is a transition route, not permanent residence.

WHERE TO STUDY

Czechia has several strong student cities beyond Prague.

Housing costs, employer access and campus experience vary significantly by city.

Prague

Largest university + employer market

Charles University, CTU, VŠE and major international employers, with the highest housing pressure.

Brno

Technology + student city

Masaryk University and Brno University of Technology with strong IT and engineering links.

Olomouc

Historic academic environment

Palacký University with lower-scale city living than Prague.

Ostrava

Engineering + industry

Industrial-region opportunities and generally lower housing costs.

Plzeň

Engineering + manufacturing

University study with strong industrial links in western Czechia.

Zlín

Applied + business study

Smaller university city with technology, design and management options.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Complete residence registration immediately.

After entering Czechia, students should follow Foreign Police, Ministry of Interior and university instructions for registration and residence-card formalities.

01

University enrolment

Complete registration and provide original academic documents.

02

Address + accommodation

Keep proof of your Czech accommodation current.

03

Foreign Police registration

Follow the registration deadline and university guidance applicable to your accommodation setup.

04

Residence card biometrics

Long-term residence holders complete Ministry of Interior biometric formalities where required.

05

Keep study status active

Your legal stay depends on continuing to fulfil the study purpose.
