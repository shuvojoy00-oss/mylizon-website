UNITED ARAB EMIRATES · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in the UAE
from Bangladesh.

The UAE offers English-medium universities, international branch campuses, strong business and technology ecosystems, and globally connected cities. But students should understand the residence model clearly: the university or resident parent normally sponsors study residence, employment needs separate authorization, and graduation does not automatically create open work rights.

UAE in one view

Strong fit for students who value English-medium education, global employers, regional proximity and modern infrastructure, but who can support a comparatively high living-cost environment.

A student residence permit is issued without work rights

ADMISSION

Admission is institution-specific and often straightforward to understand.

UAE universities set their own academic, subject and English requirements. There is no one national GPA or HSC percentage for all international students.

Bachelor

HSC or equivalent

Bangladeshi students normally apply with SSC and HSC records. Mathematics, science or subject prerequisites depend on the programme.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Minimum GPA, subject relevance, English proof and professional experience vary by programme.

PhD

Graduate qualification + research fit

Research proposal, supervisor fit, interview and prior research evidence are common at research universities.

Before paying a deposit, confirm that both the institution and programme are licensed or accredited by the appropriate UAE education authority for the emirate and qualification type.
HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

The UAE mixes national universities, private universities and international branch campuses.

That creates a very broad market. A Dubai branch campus can offer a completely different tuition and academic model from a federal research university in Abu Dhabi or Al Ain.

INTAKES

September is the main start, but January is common at many UAE institutions.

The UAE is more flexible than many European systems. Private and branch campuses often use multiple intakes, while national research universities can be more structured.

MAIN INTAKE

September

Usually the widest programme choice for Bachelor, Master and PhD study.

SECONDARY

January, often available

Common across many private and international branch campuses, with some May or summer starts depending on the provider.

Do not treat UAE intakes as nationally fixed. Check the exact course calendar, visa-processing lead time and scholarship deadline together.
IELTS · TOEFL · ENGLISH

English-medium study is a major UAE advantage.

Many universities teach fully in English, especially private universities, research institutions and international branch campuses. Minimum scores remain programme-specific.

BachelorOften IELTS 5.5–6.5Programme and institution dependent.
MasterOften IELTS 6.0–6.5+Research or professional programmes may ask for more.
Alternative testsTOEFL / EmSAT / internalDepends on the university.
MOISometimes consideredNever assume it replaces a test without written confirmation.
Example: UAE University’s elite undergraduate scholarship currently requires IELTS 7.5 or TOEFL iBT 105, which is much higher than ordinary admission at many institutions.

LIVING COST

The UAE can be academically accessible but financially demanding.

Housing is usually the largest variable. Dubai and Abu Dhabi can be expensive, while Sharjah, Ajman and Ras Al Khaimah can offer lower-cost options depending on campus and commute.

Do not use a single agency-created “UAE monthly cost” for every student. Accommodation model, emirate, transport and campus location can change the budget dramatically.
TUITION

Tuition ranges from subsidised national options to premium international campuses.

There is no meaningful UAE-wide tuition average for decision-making. Compare the exact programme and the net fee after scholarships or discounts.

National / research

Competitive + scholarship-heavy

Institutions such as Khalifa University and UAEU can offer substantial scholarships to high-performing students.

Private UAE universities

Mid to high fee bands

Business, architecture, engineering and health programmes vary substantially by provider.

Branch campuses

Premium international pricing

UK and US branch campuses can charge fees closer to international private-university levels.

SCHOLARSHIPS

The strongest UAE scholarships are merit-driven and institution-specific.

Unlike countries with one dominant national scholarship portal for all international students, UAE funding is heavily university-led.

UAEU elite scholarshipUp to 100% tuitionVery high academic and English thresholds.
Khalifa UniversityMerit-based fundingEspecially important for graduate and research study.
Private universitiesPartial discounts commonAcademic merit and early confirmation can matter.
Branch campusesUniversity-specific awardsCheck renewal conditions carefully.
UAEU example

100% tuition for elite students

From 2025 to 26 onward, UAEU lists a Chancellor’s Scholarship covering full tuition for qualifying international undergraduates with exceptionally high Grade 12 and English scores.

Scholarship strategy

Net tuition matters more than sticker tuition

Compare the actual award after admission, its renewal GPA and whether housing or living costs are included.

STUDENT RESIDENCE · BANGLADESH

The institution usually handles the residence process.

A student can reside in the UAE under sponsorship of an accredited university or college, or under a resident parent where applicable. Bangladesh passport holders require a visa or residence route before normal entry for study.

01

Secure admission

Receive an offer from a licensed or recognised UAE institution.

02

Confirm sponsor

The university or resident parent normally sponsors the student residence process.

03

Submit passport + student documents

The institution guides entry permit and residence paperwork.

04

Enter the UAE

Complete entry under the appropriate sponsored route.

05

Medical examination

Residence issuance requires the approved medical examination where applicable.

06

Health insurance

Valid health insurance is required for student residence issuance.

07

Residence permit + Emirates ID

Residence issuance is followed by Emirates ID procedures.

08

Renew with study status

The residence duration follows the study programme and sponsorship conditions.

Dubai service fee example

AED 200 residence issuance

GDRFA Dubai lists AED 200 as the residence permit fee, plus knowledge, innovation, delivery and other applicable charges.

After completion

180-day stay provision

ICP service guidance says a student may remain in the UAE for 180 days after completion of the study period, subject to the applicable residence conditions.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Do not assume every student can sponsor dependants.

The UAE has family residence routes, but eligibility depends on the sponsor’s residence category, income, accommodation and other conditions. A student residence by itself should not be treated as an automatic spouse-and-children sponsorship entitlement.

If parent is already resident

Student may be sponsored by parent

The official UAE portal states that a student may study under sponsorship of a resident parent.

If student wants to sponsor family

Check exact residence category first

Family sponsorship should be assessed separately through ICP or GDRFA before making a relocation plan.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Student residence does not equal work permission.

The student residence is issued without work. Employment or part-time work must follow UAE labour authorization rules, usually through the employer and the relevant labour authority.

Residence

Study purpose only

The university-sponsored student residence allows legal study and residence, not unrestricted employment.

Employment

Separate permit required

Any lawful part-time or employment arrangement must use the appropriate work-permit process.

Budget

Do not depend on assumed wages

Plan tuition and living costs without assuming that a student job will automatically be available or permitted.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Health insurance is part of residence compliance.

Both ICP and GDRFA student-residence guidance require valid health insurance during residence. The exact policy and provider process can depend on the emirate and university.

Residence requirement

Valid insurance

Do not treat health insurance as an optional extra when calculating first-year setup costs.

University plans

Check what is included

Some universities package insurance into student services while others charge it separately.

AFTER GRADUATION

The UAE offers multiple next-step routes, but not one automatic graduate work visa for everyone.

Graduates can use the post-study grace period to move into employment, another residence category or leave the UAE. Longer-term routes depend on meeting their own eligibility criteria.

Employment

Employer-sponsored work residence

The most common next step is securing eligible employment and moving into the appropriate work and residence route.

Green Residence

Five-year route for qualifying skilled workers

Current ICP rules require a valid UAE employment contract, professional level 1 to 3, at least a Bachelor degree and minimum salary of AED 15,000 per month.

Golden Residence

Outstanding graduates may qualify

Separate Golden Residence routes exist for qualifying graduates, exceptional students and other talent categories. These are selective, not automatic after graduation.

Think of UAE post-study planning as “move into another qualifying residence category” rather than “receive an open graduate visa.”

WHERE TO STUDY

The emirate matters almost as much as the university.

Housing, transport, industry access and campus style differ sharply between Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the northern emirates.

Dubai

Business + branch campuses

Largest concentration of international university branches and private-sector opportunities, with high housing costs.

Abu Dhabi

Research + national institutions

Strong research, energy, technology and government ecosystem with institutions such as Khalifa University and NYU Abu Dhabi.

Sharjah

University city + lower housing potential

Major academic cluster with AUS and University of Sharjah, often chosen by students who commute or live outside Dubai.

Al Ain

UAEU + quieter student environment

Home to UAE University with a more campus-oriented setting.

Ras Al Khaimah

Smaller-city alternative

Growing education and industry environment with potentially lower living costs.

Ajman

Private-university access

Can provide lower housing costs while remaining connected to Sharjah and Dubai.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Residence formalities continue after entry.

The university or sponsor normally coordinates the medical, insurance, residence and Emirates ID steps that turn entry status into legal student residence.

03

Health insurance

04

Residence permit

Your institution or sponsor completes the residence process through the relevant immigration authority.

05

Emirates ID
