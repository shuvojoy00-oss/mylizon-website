STUDY METHOD

Explain the most important visual features

Identify the overall trends or stages, group comparable details and avoid adding reasons that the visual does not show.

Focus on overall movement, turning points and comparisons across time.

Apply this method

  1. Identify the specific question, instruction or task demonstrated in the video.
  2. Observe how Joy Sir reasons through an example instead of copying only the final response.
  3. Try a different IELTS-style example from your practice material without watching the solution.
  4. Compare your attempt with reliable model material and explain any mistakes.

Self-check after the lesson

These review prompts help you practise the method. They are not official IELTS test questions.

Can you explain the lesson's main technique?

Write the steps yourself. If you cannot explain a step, replay the corresponding demonstration.

Can you apply the technique to a fresh example?

Choose a new example of the same type, work independently and compare your reasoning with a trusted explanation.

What should you review next?

Note whether your difficulty is understanding instructions, finding evidence, organising an answer, speaking fluently or managing time. Practise that specific weakness.
