BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

France does not have one national HSC percentage or CGPA cut-off.

Academic admission depends on the institution, degree level and application route. Bangladesh now also has its own Campus France pathway.

Bachelor from Bangladesh

Campus France Bangladesh currently says SSC + HSC + at least 2 years of Bachelor study.

This is the current Bangladesh-specific guidance published by Campus France for applicants seeking Bachelor-level study in France.

Licence 1

DAP applies to first-year university Licence in many cases.

Foreign applicants holding a foreign secondary-school diploma who apply to first-year Licence at a public university normally follow the DAP route through Études en France.

Masters

A completed Bachelor in a relevant field is the usual starting point.

Campus France Bangladesh states that Master applicants should have completed a Bachelor in the field they intend to continue. Individual institutions still decide equivalency and prerequisites.

Field change

Major subject changes can be difficult.

France often expects academic progression. A large change of discipline needs a strong institutional and career rationale.

French

French-taught study can require TCF, DELF or DALF.

Required level depends on the programme. DAP applicants to French-taught first-year Licence can face specific French-language testing rules.

English

English-taught programmes set their own IELTS/TOEFL requirements.

France has thousands of English-taught options, especially at Master level, but there is no single national IELTS threshold.

ÉTUDES EN FRANCE · 2026 CHANGE

Bangladesh now uses France’s central international application platform.

Launch in Bangladesh

The Études en France procedure opened for Bangladesh on 1 October 2026.

One connected workflow

The platform supports programme search, applications, institution communication and visa-related academic procedures for participating programmes.

2027/28 Licence 1 calendar

For DAP-type first-year Licence applications, the 2027/28 procedure opened 1 October 2026 and the file deadline is 15 December 2026.

FRENCH · IELTS · ENGLISH

Language is one of the biggest differences between Bachelor and Master study in France.

French-taught programmes

B2 French is a common institutional benchmark, though some programmes require higher. DAP applicants may need TCF-DAP unless exempt.

English-taught programmes

More common at Master and engineering/business-school level. Institutions decide IELTS, TOEFL or other accepted proof.

Career reality

Even when the degree is fully English-taught, French can materially improve internship, part-time work and graduate-job options.

SCHOLARSHIPS

France has national, embassy, university and Erasmus funding.

Eiffel Excellence

Highly competitive scholarship programme for selected Master and doctoral candidates, normally nominated by French institutions.

Campus France catalogue

CampusBourses lists scholarships from government, regions, universities, foundations and international organisations.

Institutional exemptions

Some public institutions exempt selected international students from differentiated tuition, which can be as valuable as a tuition scholarship.

VLS-TS STUDENT VISA · BANGLADESH

Bangladesh students go through Campus France first, then the Embassy visa process.

01

Études en France / institution process

Complete the appropriate Campus France academic procedure for Bangladesh and secure admission.

02

Campus France academic review

France-Visas Bangladesh states that long-stay student applicants have a first interview with the Embassy’s cultural attaché to review academic background.

03

France-Visas

Create the long-stay student visa application and prepare the country-specific supporting-document list.

04

Resources & accommodation

Show sufficient financial resources and provide at least a temporary address for the first three months or permanent housing where already arranged.

05

Visa section interview

Bangladesh long-stay student applicants have a second interview with the Embassy visa section.

06

Submission

Student appointments are not booked through the ordinary online VFS appointment route; the Embassy/Campus France process contacts students directly with appointment instructions.

07

VLS-TS decision

For study longer than three months, France normally issues a long-stay student visa serving as a residence permit once validated.

08

Validate after arrival

Validate the VLS-TS online within three months of arriving in France and pay the student validation tax.

VISA & RESIDENCE COSTS

Études en France countries receive a lower indicative student visa fee.

Student visa

France-Visas currently indicates €50 for applicants in Études en France countries, subject to special cases and local-currency collection.

VLS-TS validation

The student VLS-TS validation tax is currently €50.

Later student residence card

The student residence-card tax and stamp currently total €75 when renewing after the first VLS-TS period.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Keep the Campus France file and visa file consistent.

Academic documents

SSC, HSC, Bachelor/Master certificates and transcripts as applicable, plus current enrolment/employment evidence and programme-specific documents.

Études en France

Passport, academic history, programme choices, language proof, motivation and the supporting documents requested by the Bangladesh Campus France process.

Visa

France-Visas form, passport, admission, Campus France process evidence, resources, accommodation, photos and the other country-specific documents generated by the visa assistant.

Financial support

Bank statements, scholarship or sponsor evidence should clearly show that the minimum living resources and education costs are credible.

Translations

Use certified translations where required by Campus France, the institution or France-Visas. Keep spellings and dates consistent across every document.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

An ordinary student permit does not create an automatic “student dependant” route.

Separate immigration basis

A spouse or child may need their own visa/residence basis. Do not assume France follows the dependant model used by the UK, Australia or Finland.

Family reunification later

A non-EU resident can become eligible for the ordinary regroupement familial process after meeting residence, income, housing and other statutory conditions. The general rule normally requires at least 18 months of regular residence before applying.

Talent / researcher routes differ

Students who later move into certain talent or researcher permits can have access to simplified accompanying-family rules that are different from ordinary student status.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

International students can work up to 964 hours per year.

964 hours / year

A VLS-TS or student residence card allows salaried work up to 60% of normal annual working time, equal to 964 hours.

No ordinary work permit within the limit

Students can work within the permitted annual limit without a separate work authorisation, though the employer has declaration obligations.

Do not fund the whole degree from work

France-Visas explicitly describes student employment as supplementary income, not a way to cover all student expenses.

HEALTHCARE & SOCIAL SECURITY

International students register into the French health system after arrival.

Student social security

Eligible international students register with Assurance Maladie after arrival. Registration itself is free.

Mutuelle

French social security does not reimburse every healthcare cost in full, so many students purchase supplementary health insurance.

AFTER STUDY

Eligible French graduates can receive one year to search for work or create a business.

Recherche d'emploi / création d'entreprise

Length1 year
RenewableNo
Typical qualificationMaster-equivalent or listed eligible diploma
PurposeJob search / business

The permit can include employment while you search.

Employment related to your training can be taken without a separate work authorisation where the conditions are met. The current salary benchmark for this facilitated employment route is above €2,800.53 gross per month.

WORK & LONG-TERM STAY

The graduate permit is a bridge into a professional residence status.

Employee permit

Graduates with suitable employment can move to the salarié or travailleur temporaire route depending on the contract and conditions.

Talent permit

Highly qualified graduates can qualify for multi-year talent permits where salary, qualification and role requirements are met.

EU Blue Card

The current 2026 gross annual salary threshold published by Service Public is €59,373 for the EU Blue Card route.

Long-term residence

Permanent or long-term residence depends on qualifying lawful residence, integration and other statutory conditions. A French degree alone does not guarantee it.

CITIES

France’s student budget changes dramatically outside Paris.

Paris / Île-de-France

Largest concentration of institutions and employers, but housing and daily costs are much higher.

Lyon

Major university, business, biotech and engineering city with lower housing costs than Paris.

Toulouse

Aerospace, engineering and technology hub with a strong student population.

Grenoble

Science, engineering, semiconductors and mountain-region research ecosystem.

Strasbourg

International/EU city with strong research, law, politics and science programmes.

Lille / Marseille / Bordeaux

Large regional student centres with different housing, climate and employment markets.

ARRIVAL

Validate your VLS-TS before the first three months are over.

Before departure

Passport, VLS-TS, admission, accommodation, funding evidence and copies of the Campus France/visa file.

University registration

Complete enrolment and obtain the CVEC certificate where required before final university registration.

Within 3 months

Validate the VLS-TS online and pay the €50 student validation tax.

Practical setup

Register for health coverage, arrange bank/phone/transport, CAF housing support where eligible and understand the 964-hour work limit.
