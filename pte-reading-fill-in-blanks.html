Practical method

  1. Read the sentence and surrounding text for the overall topic.
  2. Identify the grammatical form required by the blank.
  3. Compare available words for meaning and natural collocation.
  4. Recheck the full sentence after filling each blank.

How to practise effectively

Try the task with several different examples. Record your answers or working process, review mistakes, and compare your performance over multiple attempts. One shortcut or template cannot guarantee a test score.
