LATVIA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Latvia
from Bangladesh.

Latvia can offer comparatively affordable European tuition, English-taught programmes, Schengen-area access and useful student work rights. For Bangladeshi applicants, however, the immigration route is more specific: residence permit processing is handled through the Latvian Embassy in India, and Bangladesh is subject to additional examination.

Latvia in one view

Strong fit for students looking for lower-cost European study, especially in business, engineering and computing, with clearer work rights than some destinations but a more involved Bangladesh immigration process.

Bangladeshi students must apply for the residence permit in India

The Embassy of Latvia in India states that Bangladeshi nationals studying in Latvia must submit the residence permit application in person at the Embassy in New Delhi. After OCMA grants residence rights, the student can apply for the long-stay visa.

ADMISSION

Latvia combines national recognition rules with university-specific admission standards.

Your previous qualification is checked for recognition, while the university decides programme-specific grades, subject prerequisites, interviews and other requirements.

Bachelor

Completed secondary education

Bangladeshi applicants normally submit SSC and HSC documents for recognition and programme assessment.

Master

Relevant Bachelor degree

Academic relevance, GPA and programme-specific prerequisites depend on the university.

PhD

Graduate qualification + research fit

Doctoral admission commonly includes research proposal, academic background and supervisor or faculty assessment.

Study in Latvia’s official application sequence includes qualification recognition before final admission. Do not assume a foreign diploma is accepted automatically without that step.

INTAKES

September is the main intake, with selected February starts.

Study in Latvia states that autumn applications commonly open in March and run until July or August, while spring intake often opens in November and December for February study. Citizenship-based deadlines may be earlier.

MAIN INTAKE

September

Broadest programme choice across Bachelor, Master and PhD study.

SECONDARY

February, selected programmes

Available at some institutions, but not all programmes or universities.

Bangladesh applicants should apply earlier than the published final deadline because residence-permit processing requires an in-person New Delhi step.

LIVING COST + FUNDS

Latvia can be one of the more affordable EU study destinations.

Study in Latvia’s current survival guide places typical monthly expenses around €700 to €800 including accommodation, although lifestyle and housing choice matter.

Official planning examples

Typical monthly total~€700–800
Student dormitory / hostel~€70–120
Shared flat~€100–200
Solo flat~€250–350
Student public transport~€5–15 / month
OCMA’s study residence page currently lists €620 per month as the required subsistence evidence for the student, plus €620 per accompanying adult and €150 per minor child. This is an immigration minimum, not the same thing as realistic spending.

TUITION

Tuition varies widely by discipline, but many programmes remain competitively priced for Europe.

Study in Latvia publishes broad national ranges and stresses that each institution sets the actual fee.

Business

~€2,000–6,000 / year

Typical national guide range for business administration and management.

Engineering

~€2,700–3,500 / year

Typical national guide range for engineering programmes.

Computer Science

~€1,800–3,800 / year

Typical national guide range for computing programmes.

Health / medicine

~€8,000–15,000 / year

Medical and health programmes can be significantly more expensive.

The official portal says some Bachelor programmes cost below €1,600 annually, while dentistry can reach around €15,000 and Executive MBA programmes much higher.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Bangladeshi applicants should not rely on the Latvian State Scholarship.

The 2026 to 27 official eligibility list does not include Bangladesh. Students should focus on university-level scholarships, tuition discounts and programme-specific funding instead.

State scholarship

Bangladesh not eligible in 2026–27

The official VIAA eligible-country list includes selected partner countries, but Bangladesh is not among them for the current cycle.

University funding

Still worth checking

Institutions may offer merit awards, discounts or fee waivers independently from the national scholarship programme.

The next Latvian State Scholarship call is scheduled for 1 February to 1 April 2027, but eligibility countries should be rechecked before any Bangladesh applicant plans around it.

RESIDENCE PERMIT · BANGLADESH

The Bangladesh route is residence permit first, then long-stay visa.

Bangladeshi nationals intending to study in Latvia must apply for the residence permit in person through the Embassy of Latvia in India. After OCMA grants residence rights, the student applies for the long-stay visa for entry.

01

Receive admission

Secure an offer and study agreement from an accredited Latvian institution.

02

University invitation / call

The inviting institution completes the required immigration invitation process.

03

Prepare residence documents

Passport, application, photo, criminal-record evidence, funds and study agreement are core documents.

04

Travel to New Delhi

Bangladeshi applicants submit the residence permit application in person at the Embassy of Latvia in India.

05

OCMA decision

The Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs decides the residence application.

06

Apply for long-stay visa

After a positive OCMA decision, submit the D visa application for travel to Latvia.

07

Enter Latvia

Travel with the visa and immigration decision documents.

08

Collect / activate residence status

Follow OCMA and university instructions after arrival.

Bangladeshi nationals are also subject to additional examination under Latvia’s designated-country rules, so allow more time than a straightforward EU admissions timeline.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Family accompaniment is possible, but financial evidence rises.

OCMA’s student residence guidance explicitly accounts for accompanying family members when calculating necessary subsistence.

Adult dependant

€620 / month evidence

The current study residence guidance lists the same monthly subsistence amount for an accompanying adult.

Minor child

€150 / month evidence

Relationship documents and, where relevant, consent from the non-travelling parent are required.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Latvia’s work rights differ sharply by study level.

The official Study in Latvia FAQ gives Bachelor students a limited work allowance, while full-time Master and doctoral students have no study-level hour restriction.

Bachelor

Up to 20 hours / week

Full-time Bachelor students may work up to 20 hours per week during studies.

Bachelor holidays

Up to 40 hours / week

During holidays, Bachelor students may work full-time.

Master / PhD

No study-level hour restriction

Study in Latvia states that full-time Master and doctoral students may work without restrictions.

Immigration status and employment formalities still matter. “No study-level hour restriction” does not mean every job or employment arrangement is automatically lawful without the correct employer and tax compliance.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Insurance belongs in the immigration budget.

Long-stay and residence processes require appropriate health-insurance coverage. Check the exact minimum cover and policy period required for your visa and residence stage.

Before travel

Visa-compliant insurance

Use a policy accepted for the long-stay visa and Schengen-area travel requirements.

During study

Maintain valid coverage

Do not let insurance lapse while residence status is active.

AFTER GRADUATION

Latvia offers time to transition, but the route depends on your study level.

The official Study in Latvia FAQ distinguishes Bachelor graduates from Master and PhD graduates.

Bachelor graduate

4 additional months

The residence permit includes four months after study completion to apply for Master study or find a job.

Master / PhD graduate

4 months + possible 9-month extension

Master and doctoral graduates can request an additional nine-month extension for job search, but approval is case-by-case and not guaranteed.

The dedicated post-graduation residence permit itself does not include employment rights. A graduate who gets a job still needs the appropriate work authorization with the employer.
Study in Latvia graduate residence FAQ ↗
WHERE TO STUDY

Riga is the centre of the international student market.

Most major English-taught programmes and employers are concentrated in the capital, while other cities can offer smaller communities and lower housing pressure.

Riga

Universities + jobs + transport

Largest choice of institutions, internships and international employers.

Jelgava

Applied sciences + agriculture

Home to Latvia University of Life Sciences and Technologies.

Daugavpils

Regional university environment

Smaller city with selected academic options and lower-cost potential.

Liepāja

Coastal regional option

Smaller-city study with selected programmes and a different lifestyle from Riga.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Finish the residence process and register your local life quickly.

Your university’s international office is an important practical partner after arrival.

02

Residence formalities

03

Declare address where required

Keep accommodation information current for immigration and university purposes.

04

Set up banking, transport and healthcare

Use student discounts and university support to reduce first-month costs.
