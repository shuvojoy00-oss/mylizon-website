SERBIA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Serbia
from Bangladesh.

Serbia offers relatively affordable tuition and living costs, a growing catalogue of English-taught programmes, legal student work, and a straightforward Visa D to temporary-residence structure. For Bangladeshi students, ordinary passports require a visa, so immigration planning must start before travel.

Updated: 7 October 2026For Bangladeshi applicantsOfficial sources linked throughout

Serbia in one view

Strong fit for students who want a lower-cost European option with English study, legal work rights and a simpler residence framework than many larger destinations.

!

Bangladeshi ordinary passport holders need a visa for Serbia

The Serbian Ministry of Foreign Affairs confirms that ordinary Bangladeshi passport holders require a visa. Students staying longer than 90 days use the long-stay Visa D route for education and then temporary residence.

ADMISSION

Admission is university-led, with recognition of foreign education where required.

Serbian institutions set programme-specific requirements. Foreign qualifications may need recognition or nostrification before continuing education.

Bachelor

Completed secondary education

Bangladeshi applicants normally submit SSC and HSC documents, translations and any entrance-exam evidence requested by the faculty.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Academic relevance, prior credits, GPA and entrance criteria vary by programme.

PhD

Master degree + research fit

Doctoral admission commonly considers academic background, proposal and supervisor or faculty approval.

Serbia’s official foreigner portal notes that nostrification is often required when a foreign citizen wants to continue education in Serbia.

HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

Serbia has public universities, private universities and specialist academies.

Most international students focus on Belgrade, Novi Sad, Niš and Kragujevac, with a growing number of accredited English-taught programmes.

INTAKES

October is the normal academic-year start.

Serbia’s academic year generally starts on 1 October. University application and entrance-exam dates are institution-specific and often run during spring and summer.

MAIN INTAKE

October

Broadest programme choice across Bachelor, Master and PhD study.

OTHER STARTS

Programme-specific

Selected private or graduate programmes may have different starts, but October is the standard planning anchor.

The Serbian Ministry of Education states that the academic year begins on 1 October and ends on 30 September the following year.

LIVING COST

Serbia remains relatively affordable compared with many European destinations.

The current Study in Serbia catalogue places average living costs, including accommodation, food and other expenses, around €500 to €850 per month.

Official national planning range

Use €500 to €850 as a planning range, not a visa-funds figure. Visa D also requires proof of financial means for the intended stay.
TUITION

International tuition spans a wide range, but many programmes remain comparatively affordable.

The current Study in Serbia FAQ places annual international tuition generally between €750 and €20,000 depending on institution and programme.

Lower-cost programmes

From ~€750 / year

Some public and specialised programmes sit near the low end of the national range.

Typical programmes

Several thousand euro / year

Business, engineering, computing and sciences commonly sit in the middle.

Medicine / premium programmes

Up to ~€20,000 / year

Medical and selected private or specialised programmes can be much higher.

Study in Serbia says tuition generally covers 60 ECTS per year and access to university facilities, while books and study materials are normally separate.

WORLD IN SERBIA

Serbia has a government scholarship route for candidates from Non Aligned Movement countries.

The World in Serbia project offers scholarships to candidates nominated from member and observer states of the Non Aligned Movement. Bangladesh is a NAM member state, so Bangladeshi candidates can be relevant to this route subject to nomination and current call rules.

2026 call18 May – 1 JunInitial diplomatic-submission window.
Updated online window10 – 26 Jun 2026Current Ministry scholarship page.
Monthly scholarshipRSD 18,000Under the current project description.
Language phaseSerbian course firstSeptember to June unless already proficient.
Coverage

Accommodation + meals + health support

The 2026 Ministry announcement states that scholarship holders receive accommodation and meals through the student-support system, health insurance and the monthly scholarship.

Selection

Government nomination matters

This is not a simple direct university discount. Candidates are proposed through competent national or diplomatic channels under the project rules.

The 2026 deadline has passed. Treat World in Serbia as a future-cycle scholarship opportunity, not as currently open funding.

VISA D + TEMPORARY RESIDENCE

Bangladeshi students need the long-stay education visa before the residence stage.

Visa D permits entry and stay for between 90 and 180 days. Students who will stay longer then apply for temporary residence on the same education basis.

01

Secure admission

Obtain enrolment or an education-purpose confirmation from the Serbian institution.

02

Prepare Visa D file

Passport, invitation, insurance, funds and institution documents form the core education file.

03

Apply for Visa D

Use the official Serbian visa route for long-term education.

04

Travel to Serbia

Enter within the validity of the long-stay education visa.

05

Register address

Complete local accommodation registration after arrival.

06

Apply for temporary residence

Submit on the same education basis before the Visa D stay expires.

07

Biometrics + permit card

Attend the competent authority when scheduled for biometric data and card issuance.

08

Renew as needed

Temporary residence can be issued for up to three years depending on the basis and circumstances.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Family reunification can be based on the student’s temporary residence.

Serbia permits spouses and qualifying family members of a foreign citizen with approved temporary residence to use the family-reunification route.

Spouse / family

Separate Visa D or residence application

Relationship evidence, funds, insurance and invitation documents apply.

Adult family member work

Automatic right to work after family residence

Welcome to Serbia states that adults with family-reunification residence based on an adult foreign resident automatically obtain the right to work.

Welcome to Serbia · Family reunification ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Students with regulated residence based on study can work up to 16 hours per week.

Serbia’s official foreigner portal gives a clear weekly limit for foreign students whose residence is regulated on the basis of studies.

Current limit

16 hours / week

This is the current official student work allowance under study residence.

Student cooperative

Common temporary-work route

Students under 26 may work through student or youth cooperatives, with Serbian tax and social-contribution rules applying.

Beyond student work

Employment route changes

For full employment after study, move to the appropriate employment-based residence and work status.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Health insurance is required in both visa and residence planning.

The education Visa D checklist requires adequate valid health insurance, and residence applications continue to require lawful coverage.

Visa D

Valid health insurance

Include compliant insurance in the education visa file.

During study

Maintain valid coverage

Keep insurance active during residence renewals and status changes.

AFTER GRADUATION

Serbia does not provide one automatic open post-study work visa for every graduate.

The practical route is to move from study residence into employment, self-employment or another qualifying residence basis. Serbia’s residence system can still support a longer-term pathway.

Employment

Switch to work-based status

A qualifying job normally moves the graduate into the employment-based Visa D or single residence-and-work permit framework.

Self-employment

Separate residence and work route

Business or self-employment uses its own single-permit basis.

Permanent residence

Three-year continuous-residence framework

Serbia allows permanent-residence applications after three years of qualifying continuous residence, but only half of time spent under study or schooling counts toward that three-year calculation after changing to another basis.

Study alone should not be marketed as a direct permanent-residence shortcut. The current law explicitly discounts study time by half for the permanent-residence calculation.
Welcome to Serbia · Permanent residence ↗
WHERE TO STUDY

Serbia has several genuine student cities.

Belgrade offers the broadest academic and employer market, while Novi Sad, Niš and Kragujevac can reduce living costs.

Belgrade

Largest university + job market

Novi Sad

IT + engineering + student life

Strong public university and a major technology ecosystem.

Niš

Engineering + medicine

Large regional university with lower-cost potential.

Kragujevac

Industry + regional university

Engineering, medicine and business linked to a smaller-city cost base.

Choose Serbia with tuition, city, Visa D, work rights and long-term plan together.
