2026 Canada rule changes students should know first

From 1 January 2026, Master’s and doctoral students in degree-granting programs at public DLIs are exempt from PAL/TAL. From 1 September 2026, the single-applicant living-funds requirement outside Quebec is CAD 23,448, excluding tuition and transportation. The 2026 international-student cap also remains in force.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Canada does not use one national HSC or CGPA cut-off.

Admission is set by each university or college. Bangladesh HSC can be a direct undergraduate credential at some Canadian universities, while graduate admission depends on degree recognition, GPA, prerequisites and program competitiveness.

HSC

Direct university entry is possible.

University of Toronto and University of Manitoba currently list the Bangladesh Higher Secondary Certificate as a recognized secondary-school qualification for undergraduate admission consideration.

Diploma

College and university routes differ.

A Bangladesh diploma may lead to college, advanced diploma, degree-entry or transfer pathways depending on credential level, subjects and institution.

Masters

Your Bachelor is assessed by the program.

Canadian universities assess degree equivalence, GPA, prerequisites, research/professional background and sometimes GRE/GMAT or work experience.

Low CGPA

Provider choice can change the route.

Graduate certificate, qualifying study, a different university or college program may be more realistic than forcing a competitive direct Masters route.

Study Gap

Gap is not an automatic refusal rule.

Your study plan, previous education, employment, finances and reasons for returning to study should make sense together.

Research

Master’s and PhD now have a 2026 advantage.

Public-DLI degree-granting Master’s and doctoral applicants are PAL/TAL-exempt from 1 January 2026.

DLI · PGWP · PROGRAM CHECK

“The college is a DLI” is no longer enough.

Students need to verify three separate things: the school is a DLI, the exact program is eligible for a PGWP if post-study work matters, and any field-of-study requirement is met.

1. DLI

A post-secondary school must be on IRCC’s Designated Learning Institution list for a study permit application.

2. PGWP-eligible program

Not every program at a DLI is PGWP eligible. IRCC’s DLI list identifies institutions and eligible programs.

3. Field of study

Many non-degree programs must have an eligible 6-digit CIP code. For 2026, IRCC has frozen the eligible field-of-study list.

IELTS · PTE · ENGLISH

Study-permit English and PGWP English are different issues.

Canada does not set one universal IELTS score for every study permit. The DLI sets admission English requirements. PGWP applicants, however, may need formal language results depending on credential type.

Degree graduates

Bachelor, Master’s and doctoral PGWP applicants generally need CLB/NCLC 7 in all four skills. Degree graduates do not have a PGWP field-of-study requirement.

College / non-university graduates

PGWP applicants generally need CLB/NCLC 5 in all four skills, and students whose study permit was applied for on or after 1 November 2024 may also need an eligible field of study.

TUITION

Canada has one of the widest tuition gaps by province and program.

Statistics Canada’s 2025/26 national averages for international students were about CAD 41,746 for undergraduate degree study and CAD 24,028 for graduate degree study, but province and field can change the figure dramatically.

Public college and vocational tuition varies by program length, credential and province. Use the exact program page and EduCanada cost tools rather than applying university averages to college programs.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Government awards and university scholarships are different things.

University entrance awards

Many Canadian universities offer automatic or competitive merit awards to international students. Values and renewal conditions vary significantly.

Graduate funding

Research Master’s and PhD students should compare supervisor funding, assistantships, fellowships and university graduate awards separately from undergraduate scholarships.

Government scholarship search

Global Affairs Canada maintains international scholarship information. Some federal programs are short-term exchange awards rather than full degree scholarships.

ACCOMMODATION & REAL LIVING COST

Toronto and Vancouver should not be budgeted like Winnipeg or Atlantic Canada.

The immigration proof-of-funds number is a visa benchmark, not a guarantee that it will cover your actual lifestyle in every city.

Campus residence

Often convenient for first-year students and may include meal plans, but prices vary widely by institution and room type.

Shared rental

Common among international students. Rent, utilities, transit and deposits should be checked city by city.

Homestay

Can include meals and utilities and may suit younger students or those wanting a family environment.

For serious budgeting, compare the exact school’s residence fees and live rental listings. Canada’s immigration living-funds figure excludes tuition and transportation.
PAL / TAL

Most study-permit applicants still need provincial or territorial attestation — but not everyone.

Usually required

Many post-secondary study-permit applicants need a PAL/TAL issued through their province/territory and DLI.

2026 graduate exemption

From 1 January 2026, Master’s and doctoral students in degree-granting programs at public DLIs do not need a PAL/TAL.

Quebec is different

Quebec has its own CAQ process. A federal PAL/TAL exemption does not mean Quebec provincial requirements disappear.

QUEBEC

Do not treat Quebec as just another province.

Quebec uses a separate provincial study authorization process through the CAQ. Financial calculations, documentation and immigration pathways can also differ from the rest of Canada.

Before the study permit

Most international students studying in Quebec need an attestation of issuance of a Quebec Acceptance Certificate (CAQ), subject to exemptions.

After graduation

Quebec has its own selection programs and should be researched separately from federal Express Entry/PNP planning.

STUDY PERMIT

From school application to Canadian port of entry.

01

Apply to a DLI

Choose the exact program and verify DLI, PGWP and field-of-study status before paying.

02

Letter of Acceptance

Receive an LOA. DLIs must validate letters of acceptance with IRCC.

03

PAL/TAL or exemption

Get the provincial/territorial attestation if required, or document why you are exempt.

04

Financial & study plan

Prepare tuition, living funds, source of funds, academic progression, employment and family/economic evidence.

05

Study permit application

Apply online. The federal study-permit fee is CAD 150.

06

Biometrics

If required, complete biometrics after receiving the biometric instruction letter. A separate biometrics fee can apply.

07

Medical & additional documents

Bangladesh is currently on IRCC’s list where an immigration medical exam can be required for stays over 6 months under the temporary-resident medical rules.

08

Decision & entry

Approval normally gives a port-of-entry letter of introduction and, where applicable, a temporary resident visa. The study permit itself is issued at entry.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Canada’s documents should be organised by purpose.

Academic & admission

Passport, HSC/SSC, diploma/Bachelor/Masters certificates and transcripts as relevant, English evidence, CV, references, portfolio/research proposal and institution-specific application documents.

Study plan / SOP

Explain why this program, why this institution/province, how it connects to previous education/work, how it improves your career and why the finances make sense. Avoid generic “Canada is a developed country” writing.

Employment evidence

Job certificate, experience letter, appointment letter, salary slips, salary bank statement, business documents and other evidence that supports the declared education-to-career timeline.

Financial & sponsor evidence

Bank statements, GIC where used, education loan, scholarship, tuition payment, sponsor income/tax/business evidence, source-of-funds documents and relationship evidence where someone else funds the study.

Police certificate

IRCC requires a police certificate if requested. The country-specific visa office instructions determine whether it should be included upfront.

Medical

Use an IRCC-approved panel physician when an immigration medical exam is required. Your own doctor cannot perform the immigration medical.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Spouse open-work eligibility became much narrower.

Eligible Masters

Master’s must normally be 16 months or longer.

From 21 January 2025, spouses/common-law partners can qualify for the student-spouse open work permit when the student is in a Master’s degree program of 16 months or longer.

Doctoral

Doctoral students can qualify.

Spouses/common-law partners of doctoral students can qualify under the current student-family open-work rules.

Professional degrees

Selected university degrees qualify.

Current eligible examples include medicine, dentistry, law/JD, pharmacy, nursing, education, engineering, optometry and veterinary medicine.

Usually not eligible

Most college and ordinary undergraduate programs.

Spouse open-work eligibility is no longer automatic for most students outside the qualifying categories.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Eligible students can work 24 hours per week off campus during study periods.

24 hours / week

Eligible full-time students can work off campus up to 24 hours per week during regular academic terms.

Unlimited during scheduled breaks

Eligible students can work unlimited off-campus hours during scheduled breaks such as summer/winter holidays and reading weeks.

SIN required

You need a Social Insurance Number and the correct work condition on your study permit before working.

Co-op changed in 2026

From 1 April 2026, post-secondary international students no longer need a separate co-op work permit for qualifying student work placements such as co-op or internships.

WAGES & EARNINGS

Canada does not have one student minimum wage.

Most jobs are governed by provincial/territorial minimum wages. The federal minimum wage applies only to federally regulated private-sector workplaces.

Weekly gross
Monthly gross estimate

Example only. Current general minimum wages include Ontario CAD 17.95, BC CAD 18.25, Manitoba CAD 16.40 and Alberta CAD 15.00. Province, job and special rules can change the actual minimum.

SIN · TAX

Work permission is not enough — you also need the right SIN and tax setup.

Social Insurance Number

You need a SIN to work in Canada. Your study permit must contain the appropriate work authorization wording before Service Canada can issue one for off-campus work.

Income tax

International students may need to file a Canadian tax return. Tax treatment depends on residency status and residential ties, not simply on having a study permit.

PROVINCES & CITIES

Canada is not one study market.

Ontario · Toronto / Ottawa / Waterloo

Largest concentration of universities and colleges, broad job market, but housing costs can be high.

British Columbia · Vancouver / Victoria

Strong universities and applied providers, high housing costs, technology and service-sector opportunities.

Alberta · Calgary / Edmonton

Major universities, energy/engineering/business ecosystem and different cost structure from Toronto/Vancouver.

Manitoba / Saskatchewan

Smaller-city study markets with universities, colleges and province-specific nominee pathways.

Atlantic Canada

Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and PEI have smaller markets and distinct provincial/Atlantic immigration options.

Quebec · Montreal

Separate CAQ and provincial immigration system. French can matter significantly for long-term planning.

PERMANENT RESIDENCE REALITY

Study is not PR. Canadian education can become one part of a later immigration profile.

Express Entry

Federal economic immigration system. Canadian education and skilled work may contribute to eligibility/ranking depending on the program and current rules.

Canadian Experience Class

A common route for eligible skilled workers with qualifying Canadian work experience. Student work while studying does not automatically count the same way as post-graduation skilled work.

Provincial Nominee Programs

Each province/territory designs its own streams. International-graduate options can change frequently and are not guaranteed by studying in that province.

Quebec

Quebec selects many economic immigrants under its own provincial system, separate from ordinary PNP planning.

ARRIVAL

The Canadian study permit is finalized at the border.

Before departure

Passport, visa/TRV if applicable, port-of-entry letter, LOA, funds, tuition evidence, accommodation, health documents and DLI contact.

At the airport

Present your documents to the border services officer. Check the printed study permit carefully before leaving the airport.

First week

Complete enrolment, get student ID, apply for SIN if eligible to work, set up bank/phone/transport and understand provincial health-insurance arrangements.

First month

Understand work-hour limits, academic standing requirements, housing/tenancy rules, winter preparation and campus support.

FLIGHT PLANNING

Dhaka to Canada is a long-haul route — destination matters.

Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal and other Canadian destinations can have very different fares, transit routes and baggage rules. Use live fares rather than publishing one permanent number.

Before booking

Compare total travel time, baggage allowance, transit-visa requirements where relevant, arrival airport, university move-in date and the start date on your immigration documents.
