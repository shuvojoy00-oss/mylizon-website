SINGAPORE · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Singapore
from Bangladesh.

Singapore offers globally competitive universities, English-medium education, strong employer access and a tightly regulated student immigration system. For Bangladeshi students, the main pathway is university admission followed by an ICA Student's Pass, with part-time work allowed only for qualifying students at approved institutions.

ADMISSION

Singapore admissions are highly competitive and qualification-specific.

Each university publishes its own international qualification rules. Bangladeshi HSC applicants should never assume that completing HSC alone automatically satisfies every university's entry standard.

Bachelor

Bangladesh HSC can require additional evidence

NTU currently lists an HSC GPA of at least 4.0 plus an additional qualification for Bangladesh HSC applicants. NUS assesses applicants under its international qualification framework and programme prerequisites.

Master

Relevant recognised Bachelor degree

Admission depends on academic results, programme fit and sometimes GRE, GMAT, portfolio or professional experience.

PhD

Strong academic + research profile

Doctoral admission commonly includes research fit, supervisor or school review and funding assessment.

HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

Singapore's strongest value is concentrated in a small number of highly selective institutions.

Students should compare autonomous universities, polytechnics and approved international campuses carefully because immigration and work rights depend on institution status.

INTAKES

August is the main undergraduate start.

Most autonomous universities organise their main undergraduate intake around August, while some graduate programmes also admit in January.

MAIN INTAKE

August

Primary undergraduate intake across NUS, NTU, SMU and other autonomous universities.

SECONDARY

January, selected graduate programmes

Programme-specific at Master and research level rather than a universal undergraduate intake.

For NTU's next international qualification cycle, Bangladesh HSC applicants can apply from 15 October 2026 to 20 January 2027 for the following academic year.

ENGLISH

Singapore's university system is overwhelmingly English-medium.

English requirements depend on your school qualification and programme. Some international qualifications require IELTS, TOEFL or another recognised test, while others may be exempt.

LIVING COST

Singapore is expensive, especially when on-campus housing is unavailable.

NUS currently estimates a single student's monthly essentials at about S$1,200 to S$2,100 on campus and S$1,550 to S$3,400 off campus.

NUS planning examples

On-campus total~S$1,200–2,100 / month
Off-campus total~S$1,550–3,400 / month
On-campus accommodation~S$500–900 / month
Off-campus accommodation~S$650–1,800 / month
Food~S$300–800 / month
Housing availability matters. Graduate coursework students may need to prioritise off-campus accommodation because campus housing demand can exceed supply.

TUITION

Singapore tuition has two very different price tracks: subsidised and non-subsidised.

International undergraduates may receive the MOE Tuition Grant at eligible autonomous universities. Students who do not receive or accept it pay substantially higher fees.

NTU 2026 other international students

~S$21,400 / year with Tuition Grant

Most standard programmes. Non-subsidised fees are about S$36,350 to S$40,600 depending on lab status.

SMU 2026 standard degrees

S$26,200 / year with Tuition Grant

Other international students pay S$47,700 without the Tuition Grant for most standard undergraduate degrees.

Premium programmes

Medicine and law cost more

NTU medicine is listed at S$91,250 for other international students with subsidy; SMU law is S$30,450 subsidised and S$56,150 non-subsidised.

Use the exact university fee table for your cohort. Singapore's cohort-based fee system means the entry-year rate is usually fixed across the normal candidature.

TUITION GRANT + SCHOLARSHIPS

Do not confuse the MOE Tuition Grant with a normal merit scholarship.

The Tuition Grant is a government subsidy for eligible students at participating institutions. Universities also offer separate competitive scholarships to outstanding international applicants.

MOE Tuition Grant

Subsidises a large part of undergraduate tuition

International students must apply and accept the grant to pay subsidised tuition at eligible universities.

University scholarships

Competitive merit awards

NUS, NTU, SMU and other universities offer selected scholarships with different tuition and living benefits.

Research funding

Strong PhD support

Research degrees can have scholarships, stipends, research assistantships and project funding depending on school and discipline.

International applicants should read the Tuition Grant agreement carefully before accepting it. Subsidised tuition and scholarship funding are separate decisions with separate conditions.
MOE Tuition Grant portal ↗
ICA STUDENT'S PASS

The Student's Pass is the core immigration document for full-time study.

Foreigners accepted as full-time matriculated students at Singapore's Institutes of Higher Learning apply online through ICA.

01

Accept university offer

Confirm the full-time place and receive the school's Registration Acknowledgement Letter details.

02

Prepare ICA information

Passport, photograph, education history, financial support and family information are part of the Student's Pass application.

03

Apply 2 to 3 months before course start

ICA requires IHL students to apply at least two months and no more than three months before the course begins.

04

Pay S$45 processing fee

The application fee is non-refundable.

05

Receive In-Principle Approval

If a Singapore entry visa is required, ICA includes it automatically in the IPA letter.

06

Travel to Singapore

Carry the IPA and complete immigration entry formalities.

07

Medical + Student's Pass issuance

Complete the required medical report and ICA formalities before the pass is issued.

08

Maintain full-time status

Your Student's Pass depends on continuing to meet the study conditions.

ICA currently states that IHL Student's Pass applications are generally processed within one week, or within two weeks where a visa is required, though some cases take longer.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Singapore does not give every international student a general dependant entitlement.

Family eligibility depends on the family member's own immigration basis. Students should not assume a spouse or child automatically receives a dependant pass because the principal applicant holds a Student's Pass.

Planning

Check family before accepting the offer

Use ICA's current Long-Term Visit Pass and other residence categories to confirm whether a particular family member qualifies.

Work

Family work rights require the correct pass

A family member's ability to work depends on their own immigration and MOM work-pass status.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Qualifying students can work up to 16 hours per week during term without a work pass.

The exemption applies only if you are a full-time registered student at an institution on MOM's approved list and hold a valid ICA Student's Pass.

During term

Maximum 16 hours / week

No separate work pass is required when all MOM conditions are met.

Internship

Can exceed the weekly rule when part of graduation requirements

An industrial attachment or internship can qualify when conducted by the institution and contributing toward graduation.

After studies

Student work exemption ends

Once studies are completed and the Student's Pass expires, an employer must apply for the appropriate work pass before you can work.

HEALTH + INSURANCE

University health coverage and ICA medical requirements should be checked separately.

Student's Pass issuance may require a medical examination, while universities can impose their own health-service or insurance fees.

ICA

Medical report may be required

Complete the examination according to the IPA and ICA issuance instructions.

University

Student health fees vary

Check the exact insurance or health service fee on your university's student-fee page.

AFTER GRADUATION

Eligible graduates can stay to seek work, but working still requires a work pass.

ICA allows graduates from listed Institutes of Higher Learning to apply for a Long-Term Visit Pass for the purpose of seeking employment in Singapore.

Graduate LTVP

Job-search stay

Eligible graduates may apply directly to ICA without a Singpass sponsor under the IHL graduate category.

Employment Pass / S Pass

Employer application required

Getting a job does not convert the LTVP into work permission. The employer must obtain the relevant MOM work pass.

No generic PSW

Do not market Singapore like the UK or Australia

The route is job search plus employer-sponsored work status, not an automatic open graduate work visa.

WHERE TO LIVE

Singapore is one city, but campus location still affects housing and commute.

Instead of comparing cities, students should compare campus access, accommodation availability and internship proximity.

Kent Ridge

Major research, healthcare and university cluster in western Singapore.

Jurong West

NTU campus

Large residential campus with engineering, science and research facilities.

City centre

SMU + business access

Excellent employer proximity but potentially higher private-rental costs.

Punggol

SIT new campus

Applied-learning environment linked to the Punggol Digital District.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Complete Student's Pass issuance before relying on student work rights.

Your IPA gets you to the final immigration stage. The digital Student's Pass confirms the study status used for employment exemption and university administration.

01

Enter with IPA

If an entry visa is required, it is included in the approved IPA letter.

02

Complete medical requirements

Submit the ICA medical report if required.

03

Complete ICA formalities

Attend in person if ICA instructs you to do so.

04

Receive digital Student's Pass

Keep the pass valid throughout full-time study.

05

Track graduation transition

Plan LTVP or employer-sponsored work status before the Student's Pass ends.

MAKE IT PERSONAL

Choose Singapore with admission competitiveness, tuition subsidy, housing and post-study work sponsorship together.

Singapore can be exceptional for the right Bangladeshi student, but it is not a low-barrier destination. Academic profile, exact tuition after subsidy, housing and employer-sponsored work pathways should all be evaluated before applying.
