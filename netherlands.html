BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Bangladesh HSC is not equivalent to a Dutch VWO diploma.

Nuffic’s official credential comparisons are crucial because Dutch institutions use them when judging whether a Bangladeshi qualification fits HBO or WO entry.

HSC Science

Comparable to 4 years of HAVO.

Nuffic currently compares Bangladesh HSC Science to four years of Dutch HAVO. This means direct research-university WO Bachelor entry is not automatically established by HSC alone.

HSC Humanities

Also comparable to 4 years of HAVO.

The same comparison applies to the humanities group under Nuffic’s current Bangladesh framework.

HSC Business

Business Studies is also compared to 4 years of HAVO.

Institutions can therefore use foundation, prior tertiary study or HBO-oriented routes depending on the programme.

Diploma in Engineering

Comparable to MBO level 3 or 4.

This does not mean automatic Bachelor admission. The receiving institution decides how the credential fits the programme.

Honours Bachelor

Comparable to an HBO Bachelor or 2 years of WO.

This can support Master’s assessment, but Dutch universities still decide whether your degree, subject and academic level fit the intended programme.

Professional Bachelor

A 4-year professional Bachelor can compare to an HBO Bachelor or 2 years of WO.

A 5-year professional Bachelor is usually compared to a WO Bachelor, sometimes an HBO Bachelor.

WO VS HBO

The Netherlands has two major higher-education tracks.

WO

Research universities

WO focuses on academic and research-oriented study. Bachelor programmes are commonly 3 years, while Master’s length depends on the field.

HBO

Universities of Applied Sciences

HBO is professionally oriented, often includes practical projects and internships, and Bachelor programmes commonly last 4 years.

A “university of applied sciences” is a recognised Dutch higher-education institution, but it is not the same academic track as a research university. Students should choose based on career and academic goals, not just the word university.

IELTS · TOEFL · ENGLISH

Dutch institutions set their own English admission scores.

English-taught programmes are widespread, especially at Master’s level and across HBO. The exact accepted test and component minimums depend on the institution/programme.

Academic English

IELTS and TOEFL are widely accepted, while many institutions also accept Cambridge and sometimes other tests. Never assume one Dutch national IELTS score.

Preparatory period

The Dutch student residence permit can also cover a preparatory period, usually up to one year, where the institution requires extra courses, language or a pre-Master before full admission.

MONEY & STUDY NORM

The 2026 higher-education living-funds norm is €1,130.77 per month.

Your institution checks the funds.

IND requires sufficient money for 12 months. The educational institution normally asks you to prove the funds as part of the immigration process. The study norm excludes tuition.

HOUSING

Housing is one of the biggest practical risks in the Netherlands.

Severe housing shortage

Study in NL explicitly warns that there is not enough student housing for everyone and recommends arranging accommodation before arrival.

Students often live off-campus

Dutch students commonly live in shared student houses across the city rather than on a university campus.

Scam risk

Do not transfer large amounts without verifying the landlord, contract, address and whether registration is possible.

SCHOLARSHIPS

NL Scholarship is useful — but it is not full tuition.

NL Scholarship

For eligible non-EEA Bachelor or Master students at participating institutions. The scholarship is €5,000 in the first year and is not a full-tuition award.

University scholarships

Research universities and HBO institutions offer their own merit awards, faculty scholarships and tuition waivers.

Research funding

PhD positions in the Netherlands are often employment contracts rather than ordinary tuition-paying student places.

MVV + STUDENT RESIDENCE PERMIT

Your Dutch institution normally runs the immigration application.

01

Choose an accredited programme

The programme must meet the accreditation requirement and the institution must be an IND recognised sponsor for study.

02

Receive admission / registration

Obtain proof of registration or temporary registration and complete the institution’s immigration instructions.

03

Tuition & financial evidence

Provide the institution with the required tuition/payment information and 12-month living-fund evidence.

04

Institution applies to IND

The recognised sponsor submits the residence application and, where required, applies for the MVV at the same time.

05

Pay application fee

The 2026 study application costs €254 and is paid through the educational institution.

06

IND decision

The legal decision period is 60 days. Complete applications from recognised sponsors have a target decision period of about 2 weeks.

07

MVV / biometrics

If an MVV is required, follow the Dutch representation’s instructions for the entry visa sticker, passport and biometrics.

08

Travel & residence card

After arrival, collect the residence document, register locally and complete any TB-test requirement indicated in the application.

IMMIGRATION COSTS

The recognised sponsor simplifies the process, but the permit still has a fee.

Study residence application

€254 in 2026 for a first study application or change of purpose.

Orientation year

€254 in 2026 for the orientation-year residence application.

Other costs

University application fees, tuition, translations/legalisation, travel and insurance are separate.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Dutch admission and Dutch immigration are linked through the university.

Academic admission

SSC/HSC or prior-degree certificates and transcripts, English evidence, CV, motivation/SOP, references and course-specific prerequisites as required by the institution.

Diploma equivalency

The institution can use Nuffic’s Bangladesh framework to evaluate the level of your qualification. A Nuffic comparison is not itself an admission offer.

Financial evidence

Proof that you meet the IND study norm for 12 months, plus tuition and institution-specific payment/deposit requirements.

Residence permit documents

Passport and institution/IND forms, antecedents information, financial evidence and other documents requested by the recognised sponsor.

Legalisation & translation

Official foreign documents may need legalisation and translation into Dutch, English, French or German depending on the document and procedure.

TB test after arrival

If the institution indicated that a TB test is required, make the GGD appointment within three months after receiving the residence permit.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Family can join a student, but student-family work rights are restrictive.

Partner and minor children

Family residence applications can be made alongside the student application where the requirements are met.

Family member cannot work

IND states that a family member staying with a person whose residence purpose is study is not allowed to work in the Netherlands.

No civic integration exam abroad

Where the family member needs an MVV as the family member of a student, the normal civic integration exam abroad is not required.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Student employment needs an employer TWV.

Option 1 · 16 hours/week

Your employer needs a work permit (TWV). You can work up to 16 hours per week throughout the year.

Option 2 · full-time summer

Instead of the weekly option, you can work full-time in June, July and August, again with the required employer TWV.

Self-employed work

IND allows study-permit holders to work as self-employed while continuing to meet student-permit conditions. Registration and tax obligations apply.

Study progress matters

You normally need to obtain at least 50% of the credits for each study year. Insufficient progress without a valid reason can be reported to IND.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Your insurance changes when you start working.

Study only

If you are in the Netherlands only for study, you are generally not allowed to take Dutch public health insurance. You need adequate home-country or private student coverage.

Study + part-time job

Once you have a part-time job, you are generally required to take out Dutch public health insurance. Self-employed students should request an SVB assessment.

You normally apply after graduating.

Dutch accredited Bachelor and Master graduates, certain post-Master graduates, researchers and qualifying graduates from designated foreign institutions can access the orientation-year route under the applicable criteria.

HIGHLY SKILLED MIGRANT

The orientation year can make employer sponsorship easier.

Reduced salary criterion

For 2026, the reduced highly skilled migrant salary criterion is €3,122 gross per month excluding holiday allowance when the eligibility conditions are met.

Employer must be recognised

For the highly skilled migrant route, the Dutch employer must be an IND-recognised sponsor and the salary must also be market-conform.

Other work routes

European Blue Card, start-up, self-employed and other residence purposes can be relevant depending on the graduate’s job or business plan.

CITIES

Housing availability can matter more than a small ranking difference.

Amsterdam

Large international university and employer market with extreme housing pressure.

Rotterdam

Business, logistics, architecture and Erasmus University ecosystem.

Delft / The Hague

Engineering and international-organisations corridor with strong university and applied-sciences options.

Eindhoven

High-tech region anchored by TU/e and major semiconductor/engineering employers.

Groningen

Major student city with a strong international university community.

Enschede

Technology-oriented student city around the University of Twente with a different cost profile from the Randstad.

ARRIVAL

Housing, municipality registration and your residence card should be planned before landing.

Before departure

Passport, MVV if required, admission, university immigration instructions, housing, insurance and financial documentation.

After arrival

Collect the residence permit when instructed and register with the municipality if required for your stay.

BSN & practical setup

Municipal registration gives access to your Citizen Service Number (BSN), which is central to tax, employment and many Dutch services.

TB / insurance / work

Complete any required GGD TB test, make sure your insurance matches your situation and never start employee work before the employer has the required TWV.
