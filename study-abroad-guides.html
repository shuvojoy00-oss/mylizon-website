Start with your question

  • Which country fits my budget? Compare verified tuition, housing, visa and living costs for a specific city and programme.
  • Am I eligible? Check your qualification, grades, English-language evidence, course prerequisites and study history against an individual university's official requirements.
  • Can I get a scholarship? Compare eligibility, coverage, restrictions and application deadlines from scholarship providers.
  • What documents are required? Check official visa and passport rules before arranging finances or submitting an application.
  • What if I have a study gap or a previous refusal? Identify what the institution and immigration authority actually require; avoid claims of guaranteed approval.
