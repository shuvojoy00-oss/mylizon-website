IELTS vs PTE in Bangladesh: Which Exam Should You Choose?

Neither IELTS nor PTE is universally easier. Choose based on who accepts the result, the required test version, your strengths, test-day preferences, costs and deadlines.

Check acceptance first

Ask your university, professional body or immigration authority exactly which test and test version it accepts, and what scores are required. General acceptance statements are not enough.

Compare how you perform

IELTS has a live Speaking interview with an examiner. PTE Academic uses a computer-based integrated test with microphone responses. Practise official samples before deciding which experience suits you.

Compare preparation requirements

Both assess English ability. Individual strengths in speaking, listening, typing and exam familiarization affect preparation needs. Switching tests does not automatically solve weak skills.

Check current prices and availability

Fees, approved centres, result timelines and rules can change. Use current official providers and booking portals rather than relying on old social-media posts.
