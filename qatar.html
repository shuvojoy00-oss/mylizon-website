QATAR · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Qatar
from Bangladesh.

Qatar offers strong public and branch-campus universities, generous undergraduate scholarships, English-medium programmes, approved part-time work and a graduate residence-extension route. The key difference is sponsorship: international students normally study under university sponsorship, and employment after graduation requires a separate work-status transition.

ADMISSION

Admission is highly institution-specific, and meeting the minimum does not guarantee a seat.

Qatar University and Education City institutions use competitive admissions. Bangladeshi applicants should prepare attested academic records and programme-specific English, mathematics or subject evidence.

Bachelor

12 years of formal education

Qatar University requires completion of 12 years of schooling. Minimum college thresholds differ by programme, and stronger applicant pools can raise the effective standard.

Master

Relevant recognised Bachelor degree

Graduate admission depends on programme prerequisites, GPA, language and sometimes professional or research experience.

PhD

Graduate qualification + research fit

Doctoral programmes assess academic background, research capability, faculty fit and programme-specific testing.

For Qatar University applicants until Spring 2027, many colleges currently list a 70% minimum high-school threshold, but this is only the minimum and does not guarantee admission.

INTAKES

Fall is the main intake, with Spring available for selected programmes.

Application calendars vary by institution. Qatar University’s 2026 international undergraduate and scholarship windows opened earlier than its general resident admission cycle.

MAIN INTAKE

August / September

Broadest choice across undergraduate and graduate programmes.

SECONDARY

January, selected programmes

Spring entry exists at some institutions and programmes but should not be assumed universally.

For Fall 2026, Qatar University’s international first-year undergraduate window ran 1 March to 25 March. Graduate international applications opened 1 December 2025 and closed 25 February 2026.

STUDENT VISA + UNIVERSITY SPONSORSHIP

International students normally enter through university-sponsored student status.

At Qatar University, self-funded international students must apply for the student visa during the same admission period. Scholarship recipients do not complete a separate visa application because QU arranges the visa after scholarship award.

01

Submit admission application

Apply to the chosen university within the international applicant timeline.

02

Send certified academic documents

Qatar University requires international applicants to submit original or certified academic records according to its application checklist.

03

Apply for student visa if self-funded

At QU, the admission application is not considered complete without the visa application or scholarship application.

04

Provide financial sponsor evidence

QU’s student-visa application requires a third-party financial sponsor and supporting financial evidence.

05

University coordinates visa issuance

Once admitted and eligible, the university’s international office works with the relevant Qatari authorities.

06

Travel to Qatar

Use the issued student visa and university arrival instructions.

07

Complete residence formalities

Medical, identity and Qatar ID processes are completed under the relevant sponsorship arrangement.

08

Maintain sponsorship

Keep enrolment and university sponsorship valid throughout study.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Family planning depends heavily on your sponsorship and residence category.

Student sponsorship does not automatically create the same family rights as a work residence permit. Married graduate students should confirm the university’s sponsorship and family rules before assuming dependants can accompany them.

Graduate family housing

Limited QU option

Qatar University provides family housing to selected married international graduate students, including some GA, RA and externally funded students, subject to availability.

Children

Schooling rules apply

Children aged 6 to 18 on qualifying family visas must meet Qatar’s compulsory-education registration or verification requirements.

QU graduate family housing ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Part-time student work exists through an approved national programme.

Jusour’s Sahem programme supports students enrolled at accredited Qatar-based higher education institutions and uses Ministry of Labour-approved training contracts.

During semester

Up to 20 hours / week

Eligible students can work within the Sahem programme during the academic term.

Summer / winter breaks

Up to 40 hours / week

The programme allows a higher limit during official academic breaks.

Eligibility

Qatar-based enrolled students

Applicants must be at least 17, actively enrolled in a Qatar-based university or higher education institution and hold a valid Qatari ID.

This is not unrestricted casual employment. Use the approved Sahem or university employment route rather than assuming a student residence automatically allows any off-campus job.
Jusour Sahem student work ↗
HEALTH + RESIDENCE

Health and identity formalities follow the residence process.

International students complete the medical and Qatar ID steps required under their sponsorship. Once resident, students can access Qatar’s health-card system subject to eligibility.

Residence process

Medical + identity steps

Complete the university and immigration instructions linked to residence sponsorship.

Health card

Available to residents

Qatar University notes that citizens and residents may apply for an HMC health card through the relevant PHCC process.

AFTER GRADUATION

Qatar offers a job-search residence extension, but work still requires employer sponsorship.

Jusour’s Graduate Residency Extension service gives eligible recent graduates of Qatar-based higher education institutions more time to remain in Qatar while seeking employment.

Graduate extension

1 year

Eligible recent graduates can extend residence for one year while looking for a suitable job.

After securing a job

Move to employer-sponsored work status

Qatar’s normal employment framework requires the employer to arrange the appropriate work and residence permit. The graduate extension is not itself an open work permit.

Current Jusour guidance targets recent graduates from Qatar-based universities who hold legal Qatar residence under university or higher-education sponsorship.
Jusour graduate extension FAQ ↗
WHERE TO STUDY

Most international higher education is concentrated in Greater Doha.

The city question in Qatar is less about choosing between multiple university cities and more about campus ecosystem, transport and housing location.

Doha

Largest academic + employer market

Qatar University, government, finance, healthcare, technology and major employers.

Education City

International campus cluster

HBKU and several international branch campuses within one research and education ecosystem.

Al Rayyan

Education City area

Housing and campus planning should consider commute and university transport.

Doha outskirts

Potential rent trade-off

Lower rent may mean longer commute, so calculate transport alongside housing.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Complete university registration and Qatar ID formalities quickly.

The university international office will normally guide sponsored students through residence and onboarding steps.

01

Complete enrolment

02

Complete residence medical steps

Follow the sponsor’s instructions for the required medical process.

03

Qatar ID

04

Housing + bank + phone

Finish daily-life setup after your residence status is activated.

05

Track sponsorship status

Before graduation, understand whether you will leave, extend residence or transition into employer sponsorship.
