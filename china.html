CHINA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in China
from Bangladesh.

China offers globally ranked universities, major government scholarships, English-taught and Chinese-taught degrees, and comparatively affordable study at many public institutions. But the 2026 route is more structured than before: undergraduate applicants increasingly need CSCA, long-term students use X1 visas and convert to residence permits, and off-campus work is permission-based rather than automatic.

2026 Bachelor applicants need to understand CSCA

The China Scholastic Competency Assessment is now widely used for international undergraduate admission. Universities including Zhejiang University explicitly state that from the 2026 to 27 academic year international Bachelor applicants must provide a valid CSCA report.

ADMISSION

Academic level is familiar. The 2026 undergraduate test layer is new.

Universities set their own grade, age and subject requirements, but the broad academic sequence remains high school for Bachelor, Bachelor degree for Master, and Master degree for PhD.

Bachelor

HSC or equivalent + CSCA

Bangladeshi applicants generally apply with SSC and HSC records. From the 2026 to 27 cycle, many universities require a valid CSCA report for international undergraduate admission.

Master

Bachelor degree

Subject relevance, GPA, references, study plan and language evidence depend on the university and programme.

PhD

Master degree + research fit

Research proposal, supervisor or faculty fit, recommendation letters and interview requirements are common.

CSCA is not one universal pass/fail cut-off. Its scores are used by participating universities, and each university sets its own admission standard and required subjects.

China is not one university market.

Students can choose elite research universities, national public institutions, specialised technology and finance universities, medical universities, provincial universities and joint-venture institutions with foreign partners.

INTAKES

September is the main degree intake.

Many international degree programmes begin in September. Application windows commonly run from late in the previous year through spring or early summer, while scholarships often close earlier.

MAIN INTAKE

September

The widest choice for Bachelor, Master and PhD programmes. Many 2026 university deadlines fell between April and June.

SPRING

February / March, selected

Available at some institutions and for some language or postgraduate programmes, but far less universal than the autumn intake.

For Bachelor applicants, plan the CSCA date before the university deadline. For scholarship applicants, build around the scholarship deadline rather than the class-start month.
HSK · IELTS · TOEFL

China offers both Chinese-medium and English-medium routes.

Language requirements are university-specific. HSK is used for Chinese-taught programmes, while English-taught programmes commonly accept IELTS or TOEFL and sometimes previous English-medium education.

Chinese BachelorOften HSK 4+Some humanities, medicine and competitive programmes require HSK 5.
English BachelorOften IELTS 5.5–6.0University-specific, not a national score.
English graduateOften IELTS 6.0+Some programmes use higher standards.
MOISometimes acceptedSome universities accept proof of previous English-medium study.
Examples: Chongqing University lists IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 80 for English-taught undergraduate programmes, while Donghua University lists IELTS 5.5 / TOEFL iBT 72 for its 2026 English-taught Bachelor routes.

LIVING COST + FINANCIAL EVIDENCE

Do not confuse the Bangladesh visa bank threshold with your real annual budget.

The Chinese Embassy in Bangladesh currently asks self-funded X1 applicants to show a bank statement proving at least US$2,500 for each study year. That is a visa-document threshold, not a realistic total cost of tuition and living in China.

TUITION

Public-university tuition can be affordable, but China has a very wide fee range.

Low-cost provincial public universities, elite institutions, medical degrees, English-taught programmes and international joint-venture universities should not be compared as one category.

Lower-cost public examples

~RMB 14,000–30,000 / year

2026 guides from universities such as Chongqing Normal and Chengdu University show many Bachelor and graduate programmes in this band.

English / specialist examples

~RMB 30,000–55,000+

English-taught Master and Doctoral programmes at larger research universities can move into higher bands.

International joint venture

Can exceed RMB 100,000

University of Nottingham Ningbo China lists 2026 tuition around RMB 120,000 for Bachelor and RMB 130,000 for many postgraduate programmes.

Always use the exact international-student fee table for your programme. Domestic tuition shown on a Chinese university page is not automatically the international fee.

SCHOLARSHIPS

China has one of the strongest government-scholarship ecosystems in Asia.

For Bangladeshi students, the Chinese Government Scholarship Type A route through the China Scholarship Council and Chinese Embassy is particularly important, alongside university, provincial and municipal scholarships.

Bangladesh CGSType AEmbassy-announced route for Bangladeshi students.
LevelsBachelor · Master · PhDPlus one-year Chinese language in the 2026 Bangladesh announcement.
Living allowance~RMB 2,500–3,500 / monthDepends on study level under the 2026 Bangladesh CGS announcement.
Other routesUniversity + local governmentProvincial, municipal and president scholarships also exist.
CGS coverage

Comprehensive funding

The Chinese Embassy in Bangladesh’s 2026 Type A announcement lists full tuition, on-campus accommodation, comprehensive medical insurance, monthly allowance and international round-trip airfare.

University route

Type B and institution scholarships

Many universities also administer Chinese Government Scholarship Type B or university and city scholarships. Eligibility and deadlines differ by institution.

The Bangladesh Type A deadline for the 2026 cycle was 10 January 2026. Future applicants should watch the Chinese Embassy in Bangladesh and Campus China for the next cycle.

X1 · X2 · BANGLADESH

The visa category depends on the length of study.

X1 is for study longer than 180 days. X2 is for study up to 180 days. Long-term students then complete residence formalities after arrival.

01

Secure admission

Receive the university admission notice and study-visa documentation.

02

Get JW201 / JW202

For X1 higher-education applications, the Bangladesh Embassy list requires the Confirmation Form for Study in China plus the original admission letter.

03

Prepare funding evidence

Scholarship students submit scholarship proof. Self-funded applicants submit a bank statement meeting the Bangladesh Embassy threshold.

04

Apply for X1 or X2

Use the current China visa application procedure applicable in Bangladesh.

05

Travel with originals

Carry passport, visa, admission and study forms, plus physical-exam evidence where required.

06

Register with the university

Your institution guides the local residence-permit process.

07

Residence permit

X1 students required to reside in China must apply to the local exit-entry authority within 30 days after entry.

08

Keep status current

Extensions, changes, internships and work permissions depend on the residence permit and local immigration authority.

X1

More than 180 days

Long-term degree study normally uses X1, followed by a study-type residence permit after arrival.

X2

Up to 180 days

Short study uses X2. Residence-permit requirements depend on the actual visa and intended stay.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN · S1 / S2

Family can visit or reside, but they do not inherit student work rights.

China uses S1 and S2 private-affairs visas for eligible family members of foreigners who are studying or working in China. The category depends mainly on intended duration.

S1

More than 180 days

For spouses, parents, children under 18 and parents-in-law visiting a foreigner residing in China for study, work or other qualifying purposes.

S2

Up to 180 days

For short family visits and private affairs, with a broader listed family-member definition.

A spouse or dependant visa is not a work permit. Family members who intend to work must independently qualify for the appropriate work authorization.
China visa categories · Official ↗
WORK + INTERNSHIPS

There is no simple national “20 hours per week” rule you can rely on.

A student-type residence permit does not automatically authorize off-campus work. The National Immigration Administration requires school approval and an immigration remark stating the location and duration of the work-study activity or internship.

Step 1

University approval

The school must approve the off-campus work-study activity or internship.

Step 2

Residence permit annotation

The local exit-entry authority must add the permitted location and duration to the study residence permit.

Without both

Do not work

The official rule says students without the required residence-permit remark may not undertake off-campus work-study or internship.

Do not build your China budget around assumed part-time income. Treat any permitted work-study or internship as conditional until the university and local immigration authority approve it.
National Immigration Administration · Official ↗
HEALTH + INSURANCE

Insurance and health checks are part of the real arrival cost.

Many universities require international students to purchase comprehensive medical insurance, often around RMB 800 per academic year in current university guides. Long-term residence permits can also require a health certificate.

Insurance

Often mandatory through the university

2026 university guides commonly list comprehensive medical insurance as a separate annual charge.

Physical exam

Long-term study may require verification

Residence-permit applications valid for more than one year can require a health certificate under NIA guidance.

NIA residence permit guidance ↗
AFTER GRADUATION

There is no single automatic national post-study work visa.

Graduation does not by itself convert a study residence permit into open work permission. To work, graduates generally need an employer-supported work permit and work-type residence status, or must qualify for a specific entrepreneurship or talent route.

Employment

Separate work route

If a graduate meets work-permit requirements and has an eligible employer, the person may move into work authorization under the applicable rules.

Entrepreneurship

Special pathways exist

NIA guidance allows certain outstanding foreign graduates from key Chinese universities to apply for 2 to 5 year private-affairs residence permits for innovation or entrepreneurship.

This is not equivalent to Canada PGWP, UK Graduate Route or New Zealand PSWV. China’s post-study stay is pathway-specific, not an automatic open work period for every graduate.

WHERE TO STUDY

Your Chinese city changes cost, language exposure and industry access.

University fit comes first, but the city around it shapes living cost, internships, transport, climate and professional networks.

Beijing

Universities + research + policy

Home to Tsinghua, Peking and many national institutions. Strong academic ecosystem with higher living costs.

Shanghai

Finance + business + global links

Fudan, SJTU and major multinational industry access, with premium housing costs.

Hangzhou

Technology + entrepreneurship

Zhejiang University and a strong digital-economy environment.

Nanjing

Large university city

Strong public universities, engineering and research with a large student population.

Wuhan

Education + engineering

Major central-China university hub with potentially lower costs than Beijing or Shanghai.

Shenzhen / Guangzhou

Technology + trade

Strong southern-China industry access, especially technology, manufacturing and business.

FIRST 30 DAYS

The X1 visa is only the entry stage.

Long-term students need to complete university registration and the local residence-permit process quickly after entering China.

01

University registration

Present original academic, passport and admission documents.

02

Accommodation registration

Complete local residence registration through the dormitory, hotel or local police process as applicable.

03

Health formalities

Complete or verify the foreigner physical examination where required for long-term residence.

04

Residence permit within 30 days

X1 students who need to reside in China must complete the study residence-permit process with the local exit-entry authority.
