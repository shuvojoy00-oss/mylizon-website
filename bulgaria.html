BULGARIA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Bulgaria
from Bangladesh.

Bulgaria offers comparatively affordable public-university study, strong medical and technical education, growing English-taught options, legal student work and a 9 month post-study job-search window. Bangladeshi ordinary passport holders need a visa, and the Bulgarian Embassy in New Delhi is accredited for Bangladesh.

Bulgaria in one view

Strong fit for students who want lower-cost EU study, especially in medicine, engineering, computing, business and selected English-taught programmes.

Bangladeshi ordinary passport holders need a Bulgarian visa

ADMISSION

Admission is university-specific, with foreign qualification recognition built into the process.

Bulgarian universities set subject, grade, language and entrance-exam requirements. Foreign school and degree documents may need recognition before final enrolment.

Bachelor

Completed secondary education

Bangladeshi students normally submit SSC and HSC documents, legalised and translated as required.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Programme relevance, credits, GPA and faculty-specific requirements apply.

PhD

Graduate qualification + research fit

Doctoral admission commonly includes prior research, academic background and faculty or supervisor selection.

For many third-country applicants, recognised secondary-education documentation from Bulgaria’s competent recognition authority is part of admission or enrolment. Always follow the chosen university’s current checklist.

Bulgaria combines comprehensive public universities, technical universities and major medical schools.

International students are especially visible in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, engineering, computing and business.

INTAKES

September and October are the main academic starts.

Application deadlines vary considerably by university, especially for medicine and programmes with entrance exams.

MAIN INTAKE

September / October

Broadest programme availability across Bachelor, Master and PhD study.

OTHER STARTS

Institution-specific

Selected programmes may start later, but Bulgaria should generally be planned around the autumn academic year.

Bangladesh applicants should leave additional time for legalisation, translation, Type D visa and residence processing rather than relying on the final university deadline.
BULGARIAN · ENGLISH

English is common in medicine and selected technical or business programmes.

Bulgarian-language study offers the widest programme choice, while English-medium programmes often have separate tuition and entrance requirements.

LIVING COST

Bulgaria is generally one of the lower-cost EU study destinations.

Housing is usually the biggest variable. Sofia is typically more expensive than Plovdiv, Varna and smaller university cities.

Build the budget from real components

Do not use one agency-created national monthly figure as if it were an immigration requirement. Bulgaria’s visa file separately requires evidence of sufficient financial resources.
TUITION

International tuition varies sharply by field and university.

Current 2026 university examples show lower technical and humanities fees, while English-taught medicine and dentistry sit much higher.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Do not assume there is one Bulgarian government scholarship automatically open to Bangladeshi degree applicants.

Current Ministry scholarship pages are heavily focused on bilateral agreements, Bulgarian citizens studying abroad and specific categories. Bangladesh eligibility should be verified for each call rather than marketed generically.

University funding

Merit and faculty awards

Some institutions offer scholarships, tuition support or performance-based awards.

Erasmus+

Mobility funding

Relevant to eligible exchange, mobility and joint-programme participation rather than every full-degree student.

Bilateral schemes

Check nationality eligibility

Government or diplomatic scholarships can be country-specific and should be verified when a new call opens.

For Bangladesh applicants, the safest strategy is to treat university-level funding and programme-specific scholarships as the primary route unless a current official bilateral call explicitly names Bangladesh.
TYPE D VISA + RESIDENCE

Long-term study requires a Bulgarian Type D visa and then a residence permit.

Non-EU students admitted to full-time higher education must obtain the long-stay visa before travel and complete residence procedures after entering Bulgaria.

01

Secure university admission

Obtain the official education documents required for long-term study.

02

Legalise + translate documents

Required foreign documents must follow the applicable legalisation procedure and Bulgarian translation rules.

03

Prepare Type D file

Passport, admission, accommodation, insurance, financial resources, medical certificate and other required documents.

04

Submit through New Delhi route

The Bulgarian Embassy in New Delhi is accredited to Bangladesh and handles long-stay visa services.

05

Attend mandatory personal interview

Bulgarian MFA guidance states that no exception is made to the personal interview requirement for Type D applications.

06

Travel to Bulgaria

Enter within the validity of the issued Type D visa.

07

Apply for residence permit

Complete the residence process with the Migration Directorate after arrival.

08

Renew during study

Residence is generally aligned to the study programme and renewed as required.

Type D applications require medical insurance covering at least €30,000 for urgent care, hospital treatment and repatriation. The passport and supporting-document rules should be rechecked immediately before submission.
Bulgarian MFA visa guidance ↗
SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Family members use a separate residence route.

Student status does not automatically give dependants the same immigration or work rights. Family reunification conditions should be checked separately before travel.

Separate application

Plan dependants before accepting the offer

Relationship evidence, accommodation, resources, insurance and residence status can all matter.

Work

Dependant work rights are not identical to student rights

Confirm the exact labour-access route for the family member’s residence category.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Full-time students can work up to 20 hours per week without a separate work permit.

The EU Immigration Portal’s current Bulgaria guidance confirms that third-country students may work 20 hours weekly during the academic year and also work during official holidays.

Academic year

20 hours / week

This is the current official student employment allowance.

Official holidays

Employment allowed

The current guidance explicitly permits student work during official holidays as well.

Budget

Do not depend on guaranteed employment

Work rights do not guarantee a job, so tuition and living costs should remain viable without assumed wages.

EU Immigration Portal · Bulgaria student rights ↗
HEALTH INSURANCE

Insurance is part of the long-stay visa file.

Type D applicants must hold medical insurance valid for EU member states and covering the required emergency treatment and repatriation risks.

Minimum coverage

€30,000

Bulgarian MFA currently specifies at least €30,000 insurance coverage for visa applications.

During residence

Keep coverage valid

Maintain compliant insurance throughout study and residence renewal.

AFTER GRADUATION

Graduates can use up to 9 months to seek and start work.

The current EU Immigration Portal states that after finishing education, a graduate may use a 9 month period to seek and start employment, provided they register with the Employment Agency within the required deadline.

Graduate period

Up to 9 months

A transition window exists after study for job search and starting work.

Registration

Within 7 business days

The official guidance requires an application for Employment Agency registration within seven business days to use the graduate job-search framework.

Once the graduate moves into employment, the relevant work and residence rules for non-EU workers apply. The 9 month period should not be described as automatic permanent work status.
EU Immigration Portal · post-study rules ↗
WHERE TO STUDY

City choice changes both budget and career access.

Sofia gives the widest employer market, while Plovdiv, Varna and other regional cities can lower housing costs.

Sofia

Largest university + employer market

Technology, business, public institutions, universities and major international employers.

Plovdiv

Medicine + lower-cost city

Large international medical community and lower housing pressure than Sofia in many cases.

Varna

Coastal + business + medicine

Tourism, economics, medical and maritime study options.

Pleven

Medical study

Known for international medicine programmes and a smaller-city environment.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Type D gets you in. Residence formalities keep you legal for long-term study.

Complete the local registration and Migration Directorate process quickly after arrival.

01

Complete university enrolment

Submit originals and activate student status.

02

Notify address

Current EU guidance says students should notify their residence address within five days after arrival.

03

Apply for residence permit

Complete the Migration Directorate process linked to the study purpose.

04

Maintain insurance + funds

Keep the conditions supporting the residence permit valid.

05

Renew before expiry

Track residence expiry and programme dates carefully.
