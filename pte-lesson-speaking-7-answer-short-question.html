Understanding Answer Short Question

Listen for the exact information requested: object, person, place, measurement or concept. Resist guessing before the question ends.

Try an original practice example

Original prompt: “What instrument measures temperature?”

Show the reasoning

A concise response is “A thermometer.” The task calls for the relevant short answer; extra unrelated explanation does not improve accuracy.

How to practise

Practise with varied simple factual prompts and compare the exact answer to what was asked.

Common questions

Can I rely on one memorised template?

Templates can help organise a response, but they should never replace listening, understanding and accurate task completion.
