IELTS Reading Time Management: 60-Minute Practice

Practise reading with a 60-minute limit, recognise difficult question types, and review where time is lost.

Practical method

  1. Practise all three sections within the official 60-minute Reading test time.
  2. Skim for structure before focusing on specific answers.
  3. If a question is taking too long, flag it and return after easier items.
  4. Review wrong answers and time spent on every task, then adjust your pacing.

How to practise effectively

Try the task with several different examples. Record your answers or working process, review mistakes, and compare your performance over multiple attempts. One shortcut or template cannot guarantee a test score.
