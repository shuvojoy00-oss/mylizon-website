BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Hungary has no single national HSC percentage or Bachelor CGPA requirement.

Hungarian universities assess foreign qualifications and set their own academic and language thresholds.

HSC

Bangladesh HSC can support Bachelor applications where the university accepts it.

The institution decides whether the school-leaving qualification gives sufficient access to the chosen degree and can require entrance tests, interviews or preparatory study.

Recognition

Recognition is institution-specific for continued study.

Hungarian recognition decisions can be made by universities or other competent authorities depending on the purpose. There is no automatic EU-wide diploma recognition.

Masters

A relevant recognised Bachelor is the normal starting point.

Universities examine degree level, credits, subject match, grades and language. Exact requirements differ by programme.

Entrance exams

Interviews and tests are common.

Medicine, engineering, business and other competitive programmes frequently use subject tests or interviews in addition to academic documents.

English

The university decides accepted English proof.

IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge, prior English-medium study or an institutional interview may be accepted depending on the programme.

Preparatory

Foundation routes are widely available.

Hungarian universities offer preparatory programmes for medicine, engineering, business and language development where direct entry is not yet appropriate.

HUNGARIAN HIGHER EDUCATION

Public, church-maintained, foundation-run and private universities all operate in Hungary.

Universities

Bachelor, Master, one-tier Master and doctoral degrees with academic and professional programmes.

One-tier Master

Medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, veterinary science, architecture and selected regulated fields can use long-cycle integrated programmes.

Practice-oriented study

Many institutions offer professionally oriented Bachelor and Master programmes with industry placements.

English-taught study

Hungary has a large international English programme portfolio, especially in medicine, engineering, business and sciences.

IELTS · TOEFL · ENGLISH

Hungary does not impose one national IELTS score.

Programme-level language rules

Universities set accepted tests, minimum scores and exemptions. Some programmes conduct their own English interview instead.

Residence permit requirement

Immigration requires proof that you possess the linguistic knowledge necessary for the studies, normally evidenced through the institution’s admission process.

MONEY & FINANCIAL EVIDENCE

Hungary does not publish one universal fixed monthly student-funds amount in the current OIF study factsheet.

What you must prove

Living costsSufficient
AccommodationCovered
Return / onward travelCovered
HealthcareCovered

Use credible, traceable funds.

OIF accepts bank statements, savings, a notarised family-support declaration with proof of the sponsor’s capacity, regular income evidence or a scholarship certificate. If relying on savings, transaction history is recommended in addition to the balance statement.

Do not rely on agency websites that invent one Hungary-wide “required bank balance.” The immigration authority assesses whether the full study stay is financially credible.
TUITION

Hungarian tuition is highly programme-specific.

Humanities / social sciences

For 2026/27, ELTE humanities examples range from about €2,000–€4,300 per semester for many Bachelor programmes and around €2,500–€3,500 per semester for many Master programmes.

Business / economics

English business and social-science programmes at major universities commonly sit around several thousand euros per semester.

Medicine

Medical study is much higher. University of Debrecen lists 2026/27 Medicine at US$16,900/year, while Szeged lists Medicine at €15,800/year.

Bangladesh payment warning

University of Debrecen states that Bangladeshi students on programmes under US$10,000 tuition must pay the yearly tuition plus the required deposit before visa-supporting documents are issued.

STIPENDIUM HUNGARICUM · BANGLADESH

Bangladesh is an official sending partner and can apply across all major degree levels.

Bangladesh eligibility

The current partner agreement allows Bangladesh applicants to apply for full-degree Bachelor, Master, one-tier Master and doctoral study in any field of interest, including Medicine and Health Science in the one-tier Master category.

2026/27 deadline

The latest completed application cycle closed on 15 January 2026 at 2 PM CET. The Bangladesh sending partner is the Secondary and Higher Education Division, Ministry of Education.

BA / MA scholarship package

Tuition-free study, HUF 43,700/month stipend, dormitory place or HUF 40,000/month accommodation contribution, plus medical insurance.

Funding is a contribution, not a full living budget

The scholarship programme explicitly warns that its stipend and housing allowance may not cover all living costs, especially in Budapest.

RESIDENCE PERMIT FOR STUDIES · BANGLADESH

Bangladesh has a local Hungarian consular office for D-visa residence cases.

01

Admission

Receive admission to full-time study at a state-recognised Hungarian higher-education institution or qualifying preparatory programme.

02

Tuition / scholarship evidence

Provide paid tuition where required by the university, or the official scholarship documentation.

03

Financial evidence

Show credible funds for living costs, housing, healthcare and return travel using the OIF-accepted evidence types.

04

Accommodation & insurance

Declare a real Hungarian accommodation address and provide comprehensive healthcare coverage or proof you can cover healthcare costs.

05

Book the Dhaka Office

The Dhaka Office of the Hungarian Embassy in New Delhi receives D-visa / residence cases from Bangladesh. Appointment is arranged by email with the Dhaka consular office.

06

Submit in person

D-type residence applications require biometric data and personal submission.

07

Residence decision + D visa

If approved, the D visa permits entry to Hungary so you can receive the residence permit. Entry must occur within the validity conditions attached to the decision.

08

Collect residence card

After arrival, complete the Hungarian address, university and residence-card procedures required for your case.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Your university file and immigration file must support the same study story.

Academic documents

SSC/HSC or previous university degrees and transcripts, programme application, language evidence and any entrance-exam or interview results required by the institution.

Study residence application

Passport, application form and study appendix, admission/student-status evidence, finance, healthcare coverage, accommodation address and travel/exit evidence.

Financial support

Applicant bank statements/savings, sponsor declaration plus evidence of sponsor capacity, regular income records or official scholarship evidence.

Minor applicants

Parental/legal representative consent must be provided in a document with full evidential force and translated into Hungarian or English.

SPOUSE & FAMILY · IMPORTANT RESTRICTION

Ordinary study status does not permit family reunification.

Spouse

A family-reunification residence permit may not be granted when the sponsor holds a residence permit for studies.

Existing children

The same restriction means students should not plan Hungary as a standard spouse-and-children dependant destination under ordinary study status.

Child born in Hungary

There is a specific exception where a third-country-national child is born in Hungary while the parent’s study residence permit is valid.

Current family-reunification restriction ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Students can work up to 30 hours per week during the study period.

30 hours/week

Third-country nationals with a study residence permit can work up to 30 hours per week during the study period.

90 full-time days

Outside the study period, students can work full-time for up to 90 days in a year.

Employer notification

Hungarian employers have immigration notification obligations when employing third-country nationals, including students.

Study progress still matters

The study residence permit can be refused or revoked if the student fails to complete the degree within one and a half times the official programme duration.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Comprehensive healthcare coverage is part of the residence requirement.

Self-funded students

You must show that you have access to comprehensive healthcare services or sufficient resources to cover healthcare costs.

Stipendium Hungaricum

Scholarship holders receive Hungarian healthcare entitlement under the programme plus supplementary medical insurance within the scholarship limits.

AFTER STUDY

Hungarian graduates can receive up to 9 months to seek work or start a business.

The work or business should match your study level.

The graduate permit requires successful completion of studies and a prospective job or business activity appropriate to the level of the completed qualification.

WORK & LONG-TERM STAY

The graduate permit is only a bridge into an eligible work or business residence category.

Hungarian Card

During the graduate permit, eligible graduates can move into a Hungarian Card where the occupation and qualification conditions are met.

EU Blue Card

Highly qualified employment can use the EU Blue Card route where current salary and professional conditions are satisfied.

Guest self-employment

A qualifying graduate business project can potentially move into the guest self-employment route.

No direct national residence card from study

Study residence holders are not eligible for a Hungarian national residence card merely on the basis of student status. Long-term settlement requires a qualifying later residence basis.

CITIES

Budapest has the largest job market, but regional university cities are substantially cheaper.

Budapest

Largest concentration of international programmes, multinationals, finance, technology and graduate jobs. Housing is the most expensive.

Debrecen

Major international university city with medicine, engineering, manufacturing and automotive investment.

Szeged

Large student city with medicine, science and research, generally cheaper than Budapest.

Pécs

Historic international student city with broad study options and lower average housing costs.

Győr

Engineering and automotive-focused regional economy linked strongly to Széchenyi István University.

Miskolc

Lower-cost regional city with engineering, business and industry-oriented study.

Study in Hungary’s current calculator shows private-flat averages of about €566/month in Budapest, versus roughly €415 in Debrecen, €340 in Szeged and €440 in Pécs.
ARRIVAL

Complete the residence, university and address setup immediately after entry.

Before departure

Passport, D visa/residence decision, admission, finance evidence, accommodation and healthcare documents.

University registration

Complete enrolment, Neptun/student-system activation, student card and orientation.

Residence card & address

Complete the immigration authority’s delivery/address procedures and report any required changes.

Work

Stay within 30 hours/week during study periods and maintain academic progress so employment does not put the residence permit at risk.
