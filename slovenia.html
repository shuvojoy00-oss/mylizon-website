SLOVENIA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Slovenia
from Bangladesh.

Slovenia offers comparatively moderate tuition, smaller student cities, strong public universities, access to the Schengen area, legal student work and a 9 month graduate job search route. For Bangladeshi students, the residence pathway is linked to the Slovenian Embassy in New Delhi, which is accredited to Bangladesh.

Bangladeshi applicants should plan around New Delhi for Slovenian residence services

The Embassy of Slovenia in New Delhi is accredited to Bangladesh and handles residence permit services. The Embassy currently encourages residence applications to be filed through authorised persons or institutions in Slovenia where possible, with card collection arranged through New Delhi.

ADMISSION

Slovenian universities combine programme admission with recognition of foreign education.

For first-cycle study, foreign secondary qualifications are assessed alongside the programme’s entry requirements. Graduate admissions depend on prior degree level and subject relevance.

Bachelor

HSC or equivalent

Bangladeshi applicants normally submit SSC and HSC records plus certified translations and recognition documents requested through the eVŠ admission process.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Universities assess academic relevance, GPA and programme prerequisites.

PhD

Graduate degree + research fit

Doctoral selection commonly includes prior research, proposal and supervisor or faculty approval.

For the 2026 to 27 first-cycle and integrated Master call, the first national application window ran from 18 February to 18 March 2026, followed by later rounds for remaining places.

HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

Slovenia is a small system with several strong public universities.

Most international interest concentrates around Ljubljana, Maribor, Primorska and Nova Gorica, with selected private and specialised institutions.

INTAKES

October is the main academic start.

The national enrolment calendar is structured around autumn entry, with first, second and remaining-place application rounds.

MAIN INTAKE

October

Broadest Bachelor, Master and PhD choice across Slovenian institutions.

SPRING

Selected programmes only

Second-semester entry is not a broad national intake and should be checked programme by programme.

For 2026 to 27 first-cycle study, the official call used 18 February to 18 March as the first round, 20 to 27 August as the second, and 23 to 24 September for remaining places.
SLOVENIAN · ENGLISH

English-taught study exists, but programme availability is narrower than in some larger EU destinations.

Universities decide the language proof. Many international Master and PhD programmes use English, while Slovenian expands the available Bachelor and professional-study options.

LIVING COST

Slovenia can be moderate by Western European standards, especially outside Ljubljana.

Official Study in Slovenia figures show meaningful differences between Ljubljana, Maribor, Primorska and Nova Gorica.

Maribor student dormitory~€100–170 / month

Maribor single room in private flat~€120–150 / month

These are average student accommodation examples, not guaranteed market prices. Add food, transport, insurance, residence fees and setup costs separately.

TUITION

Bangladeshi students normally pay tuition at Slovenian public universities.

Non-EU students without a qualifying exemption generally pay tuition for full-time study. The official national guide gives useful programme-level planning ranges.

Bachelor

~€2,000–11,000 / year

Varies by university and programme.

Master

~€2,000–15,000 / year

Graduate tuition can vary substantially by field.

PhD

~€2,000–6,000 / year

Doctoral students generally pay unless covered by funding or another exemption.

The tuition-free exemptions listed by Study in Slovenia include EU citizens and certain Western Balkan countries, but Bangladesh is not among the standard exemption groups.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Bangladeshi students should focus on university, Erasmus and research funding rather than the main Slovenian state call.

The current 2026 national foreign-student scholarship call published on Study in Slovenia is targeted at selected Western Balkan citizens for postgraduate Master study, so it should not be advertised to Bangladesh applicants as a general scholarship.

Current national call

Western Balkans focused

The 2026 Study in Slovenia funding page does not list Bangladesh among the standard beneficiaries of the main foreign-citizen state scholarship call.

Other funding

University + Erasmus + research

Check faculty awards, Erasmus Mundus or Erasmus mobility, doctoral research positions and project-funded study.

STUDENT RESIDENCE · BANGLADESH

Long-term study is built around a Slovenian temporary residence permit.

Third-country nationals need the appropriate visa or residence permission before long-term study. The Slovenian Embassy in New Delhi is accredited to Bangladesh for consular and residence services.

01

Secure admission

Receive the official enrolment or admission document from the Slovenian institution.

02

Prepare residence file

Passport, admission, funds, health insurance, accommodation and criminal-record documents are core parts of the temporary-residence process.

03

Coordinate with the university or authorised person

The New Delhi Embassy currently encourages residence applications to be filed directly with the competent Slovenian administrative unit where possible.

04

Administrative-unit decision

The competent administrative unit in Slovenia decides the temporary residence permit.

05

Embassy card collection

When the residence card reaches New Delhi, the Embassy schedules collection by email.

06

Travel to Slovenia

Enter using the valid residence documentation and any required entry visa.

07

Register address

Complete local residence registration after arrival.

08

Renew during study

Keep enrolment, insurance, funds and accommodation valid.

Current New Delhi fees list €102 for a first temporary residence permit application, €15 for the permit card and €13 for effective service of the card outside Europe. Use the Embassy’s current calculation at submission time.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Family reunification exists, but it is a separate residence route.

Family members do not simply inherit the student’s permit. Eligibility, relationship evidence, funds, insurance and housing must be assessed under Slovenia’s family-reunification rules.

Separate permit

Plan family before departure

Confirm the family route before paying major non-refundable costs if your spouse or children will accompany you.

Work

Family employment has its own access rules

Family members with residence may still need the appropriate labour-market process unless they fall into a free-access category.

Slovenia entry and residence ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Slovenia has a distinctive student-work system, plus a separate route for normal employment.

Foreign students in formal education can use Slovenia’s Student Work system for temporary and part-time jobs. A student who wants ordinary employment uses the Employment Service information-sheet procedure linked to the residence card.

Student Work

Temporary + part-time jobs

Student Services agencies issue referrals and handle payment. Foreign students need enrolment, identification, a Slovenian bank account and tax number.

Normal employment

Employer + information sheet

The employer starts a labour-market procedure. The Employment Service can then issue an information sheet and the residence card is updated with access to work.

Budget

Do not assume automatic full employment

Student Work and ordinary employment are legally different. Your study residence alone should not be treated as unrestricted labour-market access.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Health insurance is a core residence condition.

Temporary residence requires appropriate health insurance. Make sure the policy covers the full stage of your immigration process and confirm what additional coverage your university expects.

Residence

Insurance required

Health coverage forms part of the general temporary residence conditions.

During study

Keep coverage active

Do not allow insurance to lapse during residence renewals or employment changes.

AFTER GRADUATION

Slovenia offers graduates up to 9 months to seek employment or self-employment.

Graduates who meet the conditions can obtain a temporary residence permit for job search or self-employment preparation.

Validity

Maximum 9 months

The graduate job-seeking permit is issued for up to nine months and cannot be extended.

Important limitation

The job-seeking permit itself does not allow work

If you find a job or become self-employed, you must switch to the appropriate employment or self-employment residence before the graduate permit expires.

This is a genuine post-study transition window, but it is not an open work visa. The permit allows job search, not employment itself.

WHERE TO STUDY

Slovenia’s smaller size makes city choice especially important.

Ljubljana offers the widest academic and employment market, while Maribor and coastal cities can reduce accommodation costs.

Ljubljana

Largest university + employer market

University of Ljubljana, strongest job market and highest housing pressure.

Maribor

Lower-cost major student city

Large public university and generally cheaper accommodation than Ljubljana.

Koper / Primorska

Coastal international environment

University of Primorska and proximity to Italy and Croatia.

Nova Gorica

Research + cross-border setting

Small research city on the Italian border.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Finish local registration and student setup quickly.

Once in Slovenia, complete university enrolment, residence registration, insurance, banking and tax steps relevant to study and work.

01

Complete university enrolment

Submit originals and activate student status.

02

Register residence

Follow the local police or administrative-unit requirements for your accommodation.

03

Set up bank + tax number

These are especially important if you intend to use Student Work.

04

Maintain insurance

Keep compliant coverage during the residence period.

05

Track renewal dates

Do not let the study residence expire while waiting for extension or a post-study switch.
