IELTS Self-Study vs Coaching in Bangladesh: How to Decide

Both self-study and coaching can be useful. The right choice depends on your starting level, target score, ability to diagnose errors, routine and access to feedback.

When self-study makes sense

You understand the question types, follow a regular routine, can evaluate your Listening and Reading mistakes accurately and have trustworthy feedback for Speaking and Writing.

When coaching may help

You are struggling to identify why answers are wrong, need structured guidance, have repeated Writing or Speaking difficulties, or benefit from external accountability.

How to compare courses

Ask to see the actual lesson plan, teacher credentials, feedback method, class size, practice resources and total price. Avoid assuming that a guaranteed score is possible.

Free starting point

Try the LizOn beginner roadmap and free lessons before deciding. Keep a record of your weakest skill and whether you can make progress independently.
