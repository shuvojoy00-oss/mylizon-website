How can I choose a summary that represents the whole recording?

A practical response to a common preparation difficulty, connected to the original free lesson.

Why this task is challenging

Choosing a summary because it contains several familiar words but misses the main relationship. Before attempting the question, identify exactly what the instructions require.

Original practice example

Original lecture: “Digital libraries improve access to journals but require stable internet and training.”

Show explanation and answer

The best summary mentions improved access together with the requirements or limitations, not just one example. Reject options that introduce conclusions absent from the audio.

How to practise the method

Try another item without looking at an answer first. Compare your response against the text or audio and note the specific word, relationship or instruction you misunderstood. Repeat with varied material rather than relying on one shortcut.
