BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Malta uses qualification level and institution recognition, not one national Bangladesh HSC percentage.

MQRIC

Foreign qualifications can be compared against Malta’s MQF.

MQRIC, Malta’s official ENIC-NARIC centre, evaluates foreign academic and vocational qualifications and issues recognition statements where needed.

HSC

Bachelor admission remains institution-specific.

The receiving Maltese institution decides whether Bangladesh HSC and supporting academic records meet its programme requirements. MQRIC recognition can be requested where necessary.

Masters

A recognised relevant Bachelor is normally required.

The institution assesses degree level, subject relevance, grades, credits and English competence. There is no Malta-wide Bangladesh CGPA threshold.

MQF Level 5+

This is the key higher-education immigration threshold.

For the higher-education study residence route, the course must lead to a recognised qualification at MQF Level 5 or above and be delivered by an MFHEA-licensed institution.

English

Malta is English-speaking, but universities still set proof requirements.

IELTS, TOEFL, previous English-medium study or other evidence can be accepted depending on the institution and programme.

Verify before paying

Check the licence and qualification database.

Before paying a private provider, verify both the institution and the exact qualification in MFHEA’s official register.

MALTESE HIGHER EDUCATION

University, vocational and private higher education all coexist in Malta.

University of Malta

Malta’s main public university with Bachelor, Master and doctoral study across a wide range of disciplines.

MCAST

Malta College of Arts, Science and Technology focuses strongly on technical, vocational and applied higher education.

Institute of Tourism Studies

Public specialist institution for tourism, hospitality and related management education.

Private licensed institutions

Malta has a large private sector. Quality and immigration eligibility depend on MFHEA licensing and the specific accredited programme.

IELTS · ENGLISH

English is a major advantage of Malta, but proof remains programme-specific.

University programmes

Universities and higher-education providers decide accepted IELTS, TOEFL and other proof. Competitive programmes can set higher standards.

English-language courses are different

Language schools and sub-degree study can fall below MQF Level 5 and follow different immigration and work rules from higher-education students.

MONEY & FINANCIAL EVIDENCE

Malta uses percentage-based financial tests rather than one universal student bank-balance figure.

Residence permit standard

FormulaAt least 60%
ReferenceNational equivalised income threshold
EvidenceRecent 3 months
Tuition / housingAdditional

Do not market one fake fixed amount.

Identità says the exact required residence-permit amount cannot be confirmed in advance because it is calculated against the applicable household threshold and assessed case by case. Applicants should show consistent funds and legal access to them in Malta.

For D-visa and visa-extension documentation, Maltese guidance also uses a percentage of the national minimum wage. Always use the latest Identità/VFS checklist at the actual submission date.
TUITION

Malta has no single national international tuition fee.

University of Malta Bachelor example

Many non-EU humanities-style Bachelor programmes for 2026/27 are around €8,500 per academic year, though programme fees vary substantially.

University of Malta Master examples

Many taught postgraduate programmes currently show totals around €10,800, while ICT and specialist programmes can be higher.

Private institutions

Private tuition varies widely by provider and programme. Always verify the exact MFHEA-accredited qualification and full course fee.

MCAST / applied study

International programme fees differ by award level and course. Use the current MCAST international fee schedule for the exact intake.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Funding is mainly institution and programme-specific.

University scholarships

University of Malta faculties and research programmes can offer fee waivers, research funding or scholarships for selected international students.

Research funding

Doctoral and research routes can have funded positions tied to projects, departments or external grants.

Erasmus+

Mobility and joint-programme funding can be relevant for selected international students.

STUDENT D VISA · VFS DHAKA

Bangladesh students can submit Malta long-stay study visas locally in Dhaka.

01

Admission

Obtain a formal acceptance letter from the Maltese educational institution showing programme, dates, fees and study details.

02

Verify course level

For higher-education residence, confirm the programme is licensed and recognised at MQF Level 5 or above.

03

Tuition payment

Prepare proof of tuition payment according to the institution and current student D-visa checklist.

04

Finance & insurance

Prepare recent bank evidence, sponsor evidence where used, travel medical insurance and accommodation documentation.

05

Book VFS Dhaka

Dhaka is an official Identità Extended Service branch for Malta D visas. The Malta VFS centre is in Banani.

06

Submit biometrics & documents

Submit the national D-visa application through the authorised VFS service. Identità’s Central Visa Unit processes Malta D visas.

07

Travel

Students on courses of 105 days or more require the national D visa before travel.

08

Residence permit after arrival

Students on recognised higher-education courses at MQF Level 5 or above should apply for the Maltese study residence permit through Identità after arrival.

VISA & RESIDENCE COSTS

Visa and residence fees are separate.

Study D visa

The current national student D-visa checklist lists €100 for applicants aged 12+ under the standard appointment option and €150 under the extended appointment option.

Study residence permit

Identità’s current non-EU study permit page lists an application fee of €50 for study residence permits under S.L. 217.22.

VFS charges

VFS administration, courier and optional-service charges are separate and can change.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Use the latest Identità checklist together with the institution’s academic file.

Academic admission

SSC/HSC or prior degree certificates and transcripts, English evidence, passport, programme application and any MQRIC recognition statement requested by the institution.

D visa

Application form, passport, photo, acceptance letter, tuition-payment evidence, accommodation, insurance, flight itinerary and financial documents.

Bangladesh financial evidence

The Bangladesh VFS study checklist has historically asked for detailed bank history and sponsor documents. Use the newest Malta/Identità checklist generated for your appointment because requirements can be updated.

Residence permit

Tuition payment, recent financial statements, access to funds in Malta, accommodation, insurance, health-screening clearance where applicable and the institution’s enrolment evidence.

Translations / legalisation

Documents must meet Maltese translation/legalisation requirements where applicable. MQRIC can also require certificate, transcript and apostilled translation materials for recognition.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Malta is not an immediate automatic dependant destination for ordinary students.

Family reunification is a separate route

Current Identità guidance requires the sponsor to satisfy residence, income, accommodation and insurance conditions before ordinary family reunification can be approved.

Residence period matters

Identità’s current family-reunification FAQ states that the sponsor normally needs to have lived legally and continuously in Malta for at least 2 years.

Do not assume spouse work rights

Family permits issued under Malta’s alternative family-member policy do not automatically grant work rights. A separate Single Permit may be required.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Higher-education students can work up to 20 hours per week.

20 hours/week

Students following recognised higher-education courses at MQF Level 5 or above can work up to 20 hours per week.

Jobsplus licence required

The student cannot simply start working. The employer must obtain the required Jobsplus employment licence before employment begins.

Study remains primary

Attendance and academic progress must remain satisfactory. Poor performance or attendance can affect residence-permit renewal.

Lower-level courses differ

Students below MQF Level 5 can face different rules, including access to employment only after the first 12 weeks of study and labour-market conditions.

HEALTH INSURANCE & SCREENING

Insurance begins before travel and health screening can continue after arrival.

D visa insurance

Students need travel medical insurance meeting the current national-visa requirements for the initial entry period.

Residence / health screening

Higher-education residence applicants need suitable health cover, and students on courses over three months can be required to complete Malta’s health-screening process after arrival.

AFTER STUDY

Eligible graduates can remain for 9 months to seek work or set up a business.

Post-study residence

Length9 months
PurposeJob search / business
ExtendableNo
Next stepSingle Permit / other work status

The 9-month permit is not itself a full work permit.

You can stay in Malta and arrange employment, but once a job is secured the employer must obtain the appropriate Single Permit or employment authorisation before full-time work begins.

Identità graduate route ↗
WORK & LONG-TERM STAY

The graduate period is a bridge into Malta’s employment-permit system.

Single Permit

The normal long-term employment route combines work and residence permission. Employer sponsorship and current labour-market rules apply.

Change of status

Malta allows qualifying graduates to change immigration status in-country where the relevant conditions are satisfied.

Key Employee / specialist routes

Higher-skilled employment can use specialised schemes where salary and occupational requirements are met.

Long-term residence

Long-term status is separate and depends on qualifying residence history, income, integration and other legal conditions. A Maltese degree alone does not guarantee it.

PLACES TO LIVE

Malta is small, so commute and rent matter more than city boundaries.

Msida / Gżira

Popular with University of Malta students because of campus access and central transport.

Sliema / St Julian’s

Large employment, hospitality and services market, but among the most expensive rental areas.

Valletta / Floriana

Central government and cultural hub with strong public-transport links.

Paola / southern Malta

Useful for MCAST access and often cheaper than the main central coastal rental zones.

Gozo

Quieter island living with specialist study options, but commuting to Malta island is not practical for everyday classes.

ARRIVAL

MQF Level 5+ students should move quickly from D visa to residence status.

Before departure

Passport, national D visa, admission, tuition receipt, insurance, accommodation and financial documents.

Institution registration

Complete final enrolment and obtain attendance/student-status evidence.

Study residence permit

Apply through Identità’s Expatriates process for the MQF Level 5+ study residence card.

Work

Do not start student employment until the employer has obtained the required Jobsplus authorisation. Keep total work within 20 hours/week.
