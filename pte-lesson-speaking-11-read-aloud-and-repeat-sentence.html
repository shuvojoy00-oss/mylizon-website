Understanding Read Aloud and Repeat Sentence

Do not assume that a single memorised speaking template works for both tasks. Work on reading clarity and auditory recall separately.

Try an original practice example

Original sentence: “The department will announce the new timetable next Monday.”

Show the reasoning

For Read Aloud, the written text is visible and you should practise phrasing and accurate reading. For Repeat Sentence, listen carefully and reproduce the sentence you heard without the visible text.

How to practise

Practise each task under its own conditions and record which words or phrase boundaries you lose.

Common questions

Can I rely on one memorised template?

Templates can help organise a response, but they should never replace listening, understanding and accurate task completion.
