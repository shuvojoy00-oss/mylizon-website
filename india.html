INDIA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in India
from Bangladesh.

India can combine lower living costs, English-medium higher education, strong STEM and management institutions, and dedicated scholarship access for Bangladeshi students. But the route is process-driven: Study in India registration, a valid offer, the correct Student Visa and FRRO registration all matter.

India in one view

Good fit for students who value affordability, proximity, scholarship access and broad course choice more than a built-in post-study work visa.

Your Study in India ID is not optional

The Government of India’s Study in India portal states that the SII ID is mandatory for foreign students seeking higher education in India, and no foreign student will be granted a student visa without that ID.

ADMISSION

India has national entry baselines, but universities still decide the real threshold.

Study in India gives the broad academic starting point. The institution then decides subject prerequisites, marks, entrance tests, English evidence and programme-specific selection.

Bachelor

10+2 is the baseline

The official SII eligibility page says undergraduate study requires 10+2 years of schooling. For Bangladesh, SSC + HSC normally forms the core school record.

Master

A completed Bachelor is required

Master’s admission begins with a Bachelor’s degree in the relevant discipline. Individual institutions decide minimum grades and subject fit.

PhD

Postgraduate qualification

The SII baseline for doctoral study is a postgraduate degree. Research proposal, supervisor fit, interview and test requirements vary by institution.

India does not publish one national HSC percentage or Bachelor CGPA cut-off for all international students. Always verify the exact course page.

HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

Do not judge India by one university category.

India’s system includes central and state universities, Institutes of National Importance, IITs, NITs, research institutes, management institutes and private universities. Recognition and programme quality must be checked institution by institution.

Study in India partner institutions are screened using criteria such as NIRF listing, NAAC score, Institute of National Importance / Eminence status, QS Asia listing or recognised autonomous status.

INTAKES

July and August are common starts, but India does not use one universal deadline.

Application dates depend on the university, programme and scholarship route. Research-intensive programmes and scholarship applications can follow their own calendar.

MAIN ACADEMIC CYCLE

Mid-year start

Many universities begin the academic year around July or August. Confirm the exact orientation and class-start date with the institution.

OTHER STARTS

Programme-specific

Some institutions and postgraduate programmes use January or other entry points. Never assume a second intake exists without checking the course page.

For scholarship routes such as ICCR, follow the scholarship portal calendar separately from self-funded university admissions.
IELTS · TOEFL · ENGLISH

English is widely used, but proof is still institution-specific.

Study in India says English is the medium of instruction at its partner institutes. IELTS and TOEFL are commonly considered, while some institutions may use their own aptitude test or other accepted proof.

LIVING COST

India’s official planning estimate is about US$400 per month, excluding tuition.

That figure is a broad national guide. Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and private accommodation can cost more, while smaller cities and university hostels can cost less.

Study in India monthly example

Accommodation~US$150
Food + housekeeping~US$100
Transport~US$45
Personal expenses~US$50
Stationery + laundry + mobile~US$55
Indicative total~US$400 / month
This is a living-cost estimate, not a universal student-visa bank-balance requirement. The Bangladesh visa file asks for financial soundness and guarantor solvency rather than one national fixed maintenance amount.

TUITION

There is no useful single “India tuition fee.”

Fees vary sharply by institution, course and level. Elite public institutions, private engineering, management, medicine and professional courses can have completely different international fee structures.

Public

Check international fee tables

Foreign-student tuition may differ from domestic fees, even at public institutions.

Private

Large variation

International-friendly private universities can offer simpler admissions but often charge more.

Fee waiver

10% to 100%

SII partner institutions may offer merit-based tuition fee waivers from 10% to 100%, depending on the institution.

A tuition waiver normally does not mean living costs, accommodation or canteen costs are covered.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Bangladesh has one of India’s most important dedicated scholarship routes.

The ICCR Suborno Jayanti Scholarship Scheme is specifically for Bangladeshi nationals and is separate from institute-driven Study in India fee waivers.

2026–27 slots500Dedicated to Bangladesh under Suborno Jayanti.
LevelsUG · PG · PhDAcross approved fields.
Study mix50% STEMAnd 50% non-STEM.
ExcludedMedical · Law · IntegratedUnder the 2026–27 scheme rules.
What ICCR supports

More than tuition

The current scheme lists tuition/other compulsory fees, stipend, HRA, contingency, thesis/dissertation allowance and return economy airfare under its scholarship norms.

What can remain

Some student-paid charges

Hostel/private accommodation charges, mess, deposits, certificate fees, immigration registration charges and items outside scholarship norms can still fall to the student.

The 2026–27 ICCR registration cycle is closed. Future applicants should watch the ICCR A2A portal and Bangladesh-specific Suborno Jayanti instructions for the next round.

STUDENT VISA · BANGLADESH

The visa route starts before the visa application.

Foreign students should first register on Study in India and obtain the mandatory SII ID, then secure admission and apply through the official Indian visa system for Bangladesh.

01

Register on Study in India

Create your SII profile and obtain the mandatory Student ID.

02

Get admission

Receive a formal admission / bonafide letter from a recognised Indian institution.

03

Build the Bangladesh file

Passport, address, profession/student evidence, finances, guarantor solvency and admission documents.

04

Apply online

Use the official India Visa Application system for Bangladesh.

05

Submit through IVAC / Mission

Submit the signed form, passport and supporting documents as instructed.

06

Receive Student Visa

Student visas can cover the course period, subject to the issued visa and current rules.

07

Travel to India

Carry admission, visa, financial and accommodation documents.

08

FRRO registration

Student Visa holders requiring registration should complete it within 14 days of first arrival.

Bangladesh visa file

Financial soundness matters

HCI Dhaka’s student visa checklist includes a recent bank statement showing sufficient funds or other accepted financial evidence, plus guarantor bank solvency papers.

Processing

Minimum three working days

The official Bangladesh visa portal says the Mission normally needs a minimum of three working days after receiving the application, excluding special cases.

Visa fees and service charges can depend on visa type, duration and submission arrangements. Use the amount shown by the official portal / IVAC at the time of application rather than an old fixed figure.

SPOUSE · FAMILY

Do not assume a dependant route without checking the Bangladesh-specific visa rule.

General Study in India guidance says accompanying spouse and dependent family members use an Entry Visa whose end date follows the principal student. Bangladesh-specific visa provisions have historically been more restrictive.

General guidance

Entry Visa, not Tourist Visa

The SII onboarding page directs accompanying spouse and dependent family members to apply for an Entry Visa.

Bangladesh caution

Postgraduate family rule needs confirmation

An official MHA Bangladesh visa provision states spouse and children of postgraduate students may receive dependent visas. Because this is nationality-specific and older guidance, verify the current rule with HCI Dhaka before planning family travel.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

India should not be marketed as a part-time-work destination.

The official Study in India material is built around full-time study and does not advertise a standard weekly part-time work entitlement for international students comparable with countries such as the UK, Australia or Canada.

Student status

Study is the visa purpose

Your Student Visa is issued for regular full-time academic study at a recognised institution.

Internship

Check before accepting work

Internships, training or paid activity can involve separate immigration or institutional rules. Confirm with the institution and FRRO before starting.

Budget

Do not finance the degree through assumed wages

Plan tuition and living costs without depending on an unverified part-time job entitlement.

If part-time employment is essential to your study-abroad strategy, India may be a weaker fit than destinations with an explicit student work allowance.
HEALTH INSURANCE

Your Student Visa does not automatically include medical insurance.

The Study in India FAQ says medical insurance is not provided with the Student Visa, although some institutions offer insurance to international students.

Visa

No automatic cover

Do not assume visa issuance means healthcare costs are insured.

Institution

Check campus insurance

Ask whether the university includes group medical insurance and what exclusions or limits apply.

Study in India FAQ · Official ↗
AFTER GRADUATION

There is no automatic post-study work visa.

This is the most important strategic difference for students comparing India with countries that have a Graduate Route, PGWP, PSWV or similar post-study permission.

Official answer

Study in India says “No”

The official SII FAQ directly states that students do not get a work visa in India after graduation.

If you later qualify

Employment is a separate immigration route

A foreign graduate would need to independently meet the requirements of an Employment Visa or another appropriate status. A degree itself does not create that right.

Choose India for the education, scholarship, cost and regional advantages. Do not choose it on the assumption that graduation automatically creates a long post-study stay.

WHERE TO STUDY

India’s city choice changes cost, climate, industry access and campus life.

Do not compare only university names. Compare the city around the university too.

Delhi NCR

National capital + major universities

Large academic market, government institutions and broad professional networks.

Bengaluru

Technology + research

Strong for engineering, computing, startups and science.

Mumbai

Finance + industry

Major business city with strong higher education, but housing can be expensive.

Pune

Large student ecosystem

Popular university city with technology, management and international student presence.

Chennai

Engineering + manufacturing

Strong technical institutions and industry links in South India.

Kolkata

Closer to Bangladesh

Geographic proximity can matter for travel and family access.

FIRST DAYS IN INDIA

Arrival is not the end of the immigration process.

International students on Student Visas requiring registration must complete FRRO / FRO registration within 14 days of first arrival.

01

Report to university

Complete enrolment, fee and accommodation formalities.

02

Confirm address

Your actual Indian residence matters for registration.

03

Use SII / e-FRRO

The SII system integrates foreign-student registration and immigration services.

04

Register within 14 days

Do not leave this until the end of the first month.

MAKE IT PERSONAL

Understand India. Then test the university, scholarship, budget and career fit.

India can be a strong study destination for the right student, but affordability alone is not enough. Check the actual institution, admission route, funding plan and what you want after graduation.
