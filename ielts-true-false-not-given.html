IELTS Reading True, False, Not Given: How to Decide

This IELTS Reading task checks whether a statement agrees with the passage, contradicts it, or cannot be established from the text.

True মানে passage statement-টি সমর্থন করে, False মানে বিপরীত তথ্য আছে, Not Given মানে নিশ্চিত করার মতো তথ্য নেই।

Step-by-step approach

  1. Read the statement and identify the precise claim, including qualifiers such as all, only, always or some.
  2. Find the relevant passage using names, numbers or distinctive ideas, then read its context.
  3. Choose TRUE only when the passage supports the claim; FALSE when it contradicts the claim; NOT GIVEN when there is insufficient information.
  4. Do not bring in your own knowledge or assumptions. Check the exact meaning rather than matching words.

Try an original example

Passage: The museum opens on Saturdays, but its guided tours are offered only on weekdays. Statement: Guided tours are available on Saturdays.

Show suggested answer and explanation

FALSE. The passage explicitly limits tours to weekdays, although the museum itself is open on Saturdays.

Common mistakes to avoid

Avoid memorized responses that ignore what the task asks. Focus on accurate comprehension, practice with varied materials and review feedback. Techniques support preparation; they do not guarantee a score or a particular answer.
