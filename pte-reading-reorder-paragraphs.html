Practical method

  1. Look for a possible opening sentence that introduces the topic without relying on prior references.
  2. Check how pronouns, articles and linking words refer back to earlier content.
  3. Arrange strong adjacent pairs before ordering the entire passage.
  4. Reread the completed paragraph to verify coherent meaning.

How to practise effectively

Try the task with several different examples. Record your answers or working process, review mistakes, and compare your performance over multiple attempts. One shortcut or template cannot guarantee a test score.
