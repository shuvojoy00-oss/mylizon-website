Important 2026 Student visa change

From 2 October 2026, most Student visa applicants can no longer include a partner or dependent child unless an exemption applies. Family members also cannot later apply as a subsequent entrant. Always check the current Home Affairs rules before making family, financial or travel plans.

START WITH YOUR PROFILE

Australia is not one eligibility number.

A Bangladeshi HSC student, a diploma holder, a 2.7 CGPA graduate, a mature applicant and a research student should not be assessed the same way. University admission and visa assessment are also two different decisions.

HSC Applicant

Direct Bachelor or pathway?

Some universities accept Bangladeshi HSC directly at specific GPA levels. Others may require foundation, diploma or prior tertiary study.

Low CGPA

Low CGPA does not mean “no Australia.”

Course level, institution, academic trend, subject match, work experience and pathway options can materially change the outcome.

Study Gap

The gap needs a story and evidence.

Employment, business, family responsibilities, training and a logical return to study can matter. An unexplained gap is more difficult than a documented one.

Masters

Your Bachelor background matters.

Universities can assess degree recognition, CGPA, prerequisites, subject relevance and professional experience differently.

Budget

Do not calculate tuition only.

Plan for tuition, visa charge, OSHC, living costs, travel, deposits, housing setup and currency movement.

Family

Old spouse advice can now be wrong.

Family inclusion changed on 2 October 2026. Most new Student visa applicants cannot include family unless they fit an exemption.

BANGLADESH ENTRY PATHWAYS

What can your current qualification lead to?

These are route explanations. Final admission always depends on the university, course and your complete academic record.

After HSC

Bangladeshi HSC can be accepted for direct undergraduate entry by some Australian universities, but the required GPA changes by institution and degree.

Example: Macquarie University currently lists Bangladeshi HSC with a minimum GPA of 4.5/5.0 as a recognised undergraduate qualification route. Griffith University's 2026 guide shows HSC 4.1 for some standard entry bands and 4.7 for higher entry bands.

If direct entry is not available, common alternatives include Foundation, Diploma, university college pathway or one year of recognised tertiary study.

After a Bangladesh Diploma

A diploma is not automatically equal to a specific Australian year level. Institutions assess awarding body, duration, subjects, results and course relevance. Credit transfer may be possible, but should never be promised before formal assessment.

After a Bachelor degree

For Masters admission, universities normally assess whether your degree is recognised and comparable to an Australian Bachelor, your CGPA or percentage, prerequisites, English level and sometimes work experience.

Course matching matters. A student changing from one academic field to another may need to explain the logic of the progression both to the institution and under the Genuine Student assessment.

Research Masters or PhD

Research applicants normally need stronger evidence of research capability, such as a thesis, research methods background, publications or a proposal, depending on the university. Supervisor availability can also matter.

Research degrees also have different Student visa work arrangements and Temporary Graduate visa age exceptions.

Visa English and university English are not the same thing.

The Department of Home Affairs sets visa-level English rules. Universities and professional courses can set higher admission requirements.

Why your course may require more

A university can require IELTS 6.5 or 7.0 even when the Student visa minimum is lower.

Teaching, nursing, psychology, law, health and registration-linked programs can carry higher English standards or specific component scores.

From 21 January 2026, TOEFL applicants must select “Taking TOEFL for Australia” when registering for the result to be valid for Australian visa purposes.

INTAKES & TIMING

Do not start with the visa. Start with the intake.

Course availability, scholarship deadlines, deposit deadlines, CoE timing and visa processing all work backwards from your intended start date.

February / Semester 1

The largest intake for many universities and usually the widest course selection. Strong applicants should often start research 8–12 months earlier.

July / Semester 2

Another major intake. Many programs are available, but not every course starts mid-year.

Trimester and other starts

Some institutions use trimester calendars or offer November, March, May, September or other starts. Always check the exact course page rather than assuming one national calendar.

STUDENT VISA · SUBCLASS 500

Admission gets you the offer. Visa assessment asks a different question.

Most Bangladeshi applicants apply from outside Australia. You generally need a valid CoE, appropriate health cover and evidence that satisfies the current visa criteria.

1. Confirmation of Enrolment

Applicants generally need a CoE for a CRICOS-registered course before lodging the Student visa.

2. Genuine Student

Your circumstances, course logic, previous study, immigration history and future value of the course are assessed.

3. Financial capacity

Home Affairs can require evidence for tuition, living costs, travel and other relevant costs, plus genuine access to funds.

4. English evidence

Whether you must provide English evidence depends on the rules and exemptions. Admission standards are separately set by the provider.

5. OSHC

Most international students and eligible dependants must maintain Overseas Student Health Cover for the required period.

6. Health & character

Medical examinations, identity, character and other requested evidence may form part of the process.

GENUINE STUDENT

Your application must make academic and financial sense.

Home Affairs asks GS questions in English with a 150-word limit for each response. Documents should support the story rather than contradict it.

Why this course?

Explain how it connects to your previous education, skills, employment and future. A completely unrelated course can require stronger reasoning.

Why Australia and this provider?

Home Affairs may consider why you are not studying a similar course in your home country and whether you understand the provider and course.

What is your situation in Bangladesh?

Employment, business, family ties, financial position and career prospects can all be relevant. Home Affairs specifically lists evidence such as employment/business records, income tax returns and bank statements in assessing economic circumstances.

What value will the course add?

A higher tuition fee only makes sense if the qualification has a coherent academic or career benefit for you.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Part-time work can help. It should not be your financial plan.

Current Student visa rules allow most students to work up to 48 hours a fortnight while their course is in session. Home Affairs explicitly warns that students should not rely on work to support themselves or family.

During study

48 hours per fortnight

For most Student visa holders while the course is in session.

Research degrees

Different work limit

Students studying a Masters by Research or Doctoral degree have no work-hour limit under the current Student visa rule.

Know your rights

Australian workplace law applies

International students have workplace rights. Pay, payslips, safety and employment conditions should be checked through Fair Work Ombudsman resources.

AFTER GRADUATION · SUBCLASS 485

Post-study work is possible — but qualification and age matter.

The Temporary Graduate visa changed substantially from July 2024. Do not rely on old “3–5 year PSW” advertisements.

Post-Higher Education Work stream

Bachelor including honours2 years
Masters coursework / extended2 years
Masters research3 years
Doctoral degree3 years
General age limit35 or under

Important exceptions and regional option

Masters by Research and PhD applicants can be under 50 for the 485 age criterion. A second Post-Higher Education Work stream may provide another 1–2 years for eligible graduates who studied and lived in designated regional areas.

A Student visa or university offer does not guarantee a 485 visa. The graduate must meet the 485 criteria that apply when they lodge.
PR & LONG-TERM PLANNING

Study is not a PR guarantee.

Australian permanent residence is governed by separate skilled, employer, state/territory and family migration rules. Occupation lists, points, state criteria and invitation settings can change.

Course ≠ PR

Choosing a course only because someone says it is a “PR course” is risky. Migration settings can change before you graduate.

Regional can matter

Regional study can create different graduate and migration possibilities, but eligibility must be checked under the rules in force at the relevant time.

Career value first

A stronger decision asks whether the course gives you useful skills and employment value even if migration policy changes.

6–9 months before

Prepare academic documents, passport, CV, English test, work evidence and university-specific statements or portfolios if needed.

4–6 months before

After grant

BANGLADESH FAQ

The questions students actually ask us.

Can I apply to Australia with low CGPA?

Possibly. There is no single national CGPA cut-off. Your study level, institution, field, academic history, work experience and pathway options determine what is realistic.

Can I apply after a long study gap?

Yes, a gap does not automatically make you ineligible. The key issues are what you did during the gap, why you are returning to study, why the course is logical now, and what evidence supports your explanation.

Is PTE accepted for Australia?

Yes. PTE Academic is accepted for Student visa purposes under the current rules, and many universities accept it. Course-specific score requirements can be higher.

Can my spouse go with me?

For applications lodged from 2 October 2026, most Student visa applicants can no longer include family members unless an exemption applies. This is a major rule change and older online advice may now be wrong.

Can I pay my living costs by working in Australia?

You should not plan that way. Work is limited during study periods and Home Affairs expects you to have sufficient financial capacity independently of future part-time income.

Does studying in regional Australia guarantee PR?

No. Regional study can affect some graduate and migration pathways, but PR remains a separate migration process with its own criteria.

Which university is best for me?

The answer depends on your subject, academic profile, budget, city preference, scholarship, English score, career goal and visa logic — not only ranking.

The city changes your budget, lifestyle and study experience.

Do not choose Australia as if it were one single market. Course availability, rent, transport, weather and regional classification can differ materially by location.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Scholarship marketing and scholarship reality are not the same thing.

Scholarships can come from universities, the Australian Government and other organisations. Always check the award conditions, duration and whether the amount is a tuition discount or a full funding package.

University awards

Merit-based tuition scholarships

Many universities offer automatic or competitive tuition reductions to international students. The amount and eligibility can differ by intake, faculty and country.

Government

Australia Awards

Prestigious Australian Government scholarships are available to eligible applicants from selected developing countries. Country eligibility and return-home obligations must be checked for the relevant round.

Research

Research Training Program

Eligible universities can award RTP support to international research Masters and PhD students, including fee offsets, stipends and/or allowances. Universities run their own competitive selection processes.

Never choose a university only because an advertisement says “up to 50% scholarship.” Ask: 50% of what, for how long, under what GPA condition, and is renewal automatic?

2026 VISA PROCESSING PRIORITY

Your provider can affect processing priority, not visa eligibility.

For offshore applications lodged on or after 14 November 2025, Ministerial Direction 115 uses three processing priorities. Priority affects when an application is processed, not whether it meets the visa criteria.

Priority 1

Includes categories such as school, non-award, standalone ELICOS, TAFE and recognised pilot-training applicants. Provider allocation settings can also affect higher-education priority.

Priority 2 and Priority 3

For higher education and VET, the education provider's progress against its indicative allocation can move an offshore application into a lower processing priority.

What Home Affairs aims for

Home Affairs currently says it aims to commence processing Priority 1 applications within 1–4 weeks, Priority 2 within 5–8 weeks and Priority 3 within 9–12 weeks. These are commencement targets, not visa-decision guarantees, and they can change.
