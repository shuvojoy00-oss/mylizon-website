BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

There is no single UK rule for Bangladesh HSC or CGPA.

Some universities offer direct undergraduate entry from HSC, others use Foundation or prior tertiary study. Postgraduate entry varies by university, degree length, awarding institution and programme.

HSC

Direct Bachelor is university-specific.

Some universities publish direct HSC routes, while others require Foundation or additional tertiary study. Always use the university’s Bangladesh page.

Foundation

Foundation is a major UK pathway.

For example, Manchester publishes HSC entry to integrated/INTO Foundation routes rather than treating HSC as one universal direct-Bachelor qualification.

Diploma

Bangladesh Diploma can be considered by some providers.

Oxford Brookes currently publishes consideration of the four-year Diploma in Engineering with around 65% for relevant undergraduate entry.

Masters

Four-year Bachelor is a common reference point.

Manchester currently says a four-year Bachelor from a reputable Bangladesh university may be considered, typically around CGPA 3.2/4, programme dependent.

Low CGPA

University choice changes the answer.

Pre-Masters, Graduate Diploma or more flexible universities may be realistic where a direct Masters route is not.

Study Gap

Gap is not one UKVI number.

Providers and UKVI care about academic progression, genuine study intention, work history and whether the proposed course makes sense.

UNIVERSITIES & SPONSORS

First check the university. Then check its sponsor status.

A UK Student visa requires a licensed Student sponsor. The Home Office register is updated frequently, so the live sponsor register matters more than an agency logo wall.

Russell Group & research universities

Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Sheffield, Nottingham, Southampton, Bristol and others can have more selective academic requirements.

Modern & career-focused universities

Many universities offer practical courses, placement options and broader entry routes. Requirements still vary by programme and intake.

Pathway colleges

Foundation and pre-Masters providers can create routes into partner universities, but the exact progression requirement must be checked.

ENGLISH · IELTS · PTE

Visa English is a CEFR requirement. University English can be higher.

Degree level or above

Student visa English must be equivalent to CEFR B2. A Higher Education Provider can assess English itself for degree-level study if it meets the rules.

Below degree level

The Student route uses CEFR B1. Course providers can still set their own English scores above immigration minimums.

Where financial evidence is required, funds normally must be held for at least 28 consecutive days, and the end of that 28-day period must fall within 31 days before the visa application.
SCHOLARSHIPS

Separate UK Government scholarships from university discounts.

Chevening

UK Government scholarship for eligible future leaders. Current eligibility includes post-degree work experience and a commitment to return home for at least two years after the award.

Commonwealth Scholarships

Separate UK-funded scholarship routes exist for eligible Commonwealth applicants and programmes.

University scholarships

Merit, country and course awards vary widely. Always check value, duration, tuition coverage and renewal conditions.

STUDENT VISA

From university application to UK visa decision.

01

University application

Submit academics, English, passport, CV/SOP and course-specific evidence.

02

Offer or conditional offer

Meet academic, English, deposit or document conditions required by the university.

03

Credibility / CAS checks

Some universities conduct pre-CAS interviews, financial checks or credibility reviews before assigning CAS.

04

Accept & pay deposit

Accept the offer and pay the university’s required tuition deposit.

05

CAS issued

CAS is the unique sponsorship reference needed for the Student visa. You cannot apply without it.

06

TB test & visa documents

Bangladesh applicants staying over 6 months generally need an approved TB certificate. Prepare maintenance and other required evidence.

07

Student visa application

Apply online, pay the £558 visa fee and Immigration Health Surcharge, and provide biometrics/identity as required.

08

Decision & eVisa

After approval, follow current UKVI digital-status/eVisa instructions and only travel within the permitted arrival window.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

UK documents should be grouped by admission, CAS and visa purpose.

Academic & university admission

Passport, SSC/HSC, Bachelor/Masters certificates and transcripts as relevant, English evidence, CV, SOP/personal statement where requested, references, portfolio/research proposal where programme-specific.

CAS / credibility

Offer conditions, tuition deposit evidence, academic progression explanation, study gap/employment evidence, sponsor/funds explanation and any university-specific pre-CAS interview or credibility documentation.

Financial evidence

Bank statements meeting the 28-day rule where required, qualifying student loan or official financial sponsorship, tuition payments shown on CAS, parental funds with relationship/consent evidence where permitted, and translations where required.

TB certificate

Bangladesh applicants coming for 6 months or more generally need a TB certificate from a Home Office-approved clinic. Certificates from non-approved clinics are not accepted.

ATAS

Some postgraduate science, engineering and technology programmes require an Academic Technology Approval Scheme certificate before visa application/enrolment.

Police clearance

A standard UK Student visa document list does not automatically require a police clearance certificate for every student. Character or programme-specific checks can arise separately.

VISA COSTS

Separate the visa fee from healthcare and medical costs.

Student visa

£558 for applications from outside or inside the UK from 8 April 2026.

Immigration Health Surcharge

£776 per year for students and student dependants, calculated according to visa length.

TB test in Bangladesh

Approved-clinic prices vary. Current Green Crescent guidance lists BDT 5,500 for applicants aged 11+ at the Chattogram clinic.

DEPENDANTS

Most taught Master’s students can no longer bring dependants.

Can qualify

Government-sponsored students

Government-sponsored students on qualifying full-time study longer than 6 months may be able to bring partner/children.

Can qualify

Doctoral / research-based higher degrees

For postgraduate courses starting on or after 1 January 2024, dependants generally require a PhD/doctorate or a research-based higher degree lasting 9 months or more.

Usually cannot

Taught Master’s

A standard taught MSc/MA is generally not enough for new dependant applications under the current rule.

Dependants' funds

Extra maintenance applies

Current figures are £845/month in London or £680/month outside London for each dependant, up to 9 months.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Degree-level students can usually work 20 hours per week in term time.

Degree level+

20 hours per week during term time when sponsored by an eligible higher education provider; full-time work can be permitted outside term time.

Below degree level

Eligible full-time below-degree study can be limited to 10 hours per week during term time.

Important restrictions

Student visa holders cannot generally be self-employed or work as a professional sportsperson/sports coach.

AFTER STUDY

The Graduate Route is changing on 1 January 2027.

Apply on or before 31 Dec 2026

Bachelor / Master2 years
PhD / doctorate3 years

Apply on or after 1 Jan 2027

Bachelor / Master18 months
PhD / doctorate3 years
The Graduate Route is unsponsored and is not itself a route to settlement. Dependants generally must already have been your Student dependants to continue as Graduate dependants.
SKILLED WORKER & SETTLEMENT

The long-term UK route usually requires switching into a settlement-capable work visa.

Skilled Worker

You need an eligible job with an approved employer, appropriate sponsorship and salary/occupation requirements under the rules in force at the time.

Settlement

Skilled Worker can potentially lead to Indefinite Leave to Remain after 5 qualifying years if all residence, salary and other requirements are met.

LONDON VS OUTSIDE LONDON

Location changes both visa maintenance and real living cost.

London

Largest university and employment market, but significantly higher rent and the higher Student visa maintenance benchmark.

Manchester / Birmingham / Leeds

Large student cities with broad university choice and generally lower living costs than central London.

Sheffield / Nottingham / Leicester / Coventry

Popular international-student destinations with varied university and accommodation choices.

Scotland

Edinburgh, Glasgow, Aberdeen, Dundee and others have their own course structures and four-year undergraduate norm in many cases.

Wales

Cardiff, Swansea and other providers can offer lower living-cost environments than London.

Northern Ireland

Belfast offers major university study with a distinct regional living-cost environment.

ARRIVAL

The UK process continues after visa approval.

Before departure

Passport, visa/eVisa status, CAS details, TB certificate records, university enrolment instructions, accommodation, funds/cards, medicines and travel plan.

Arrival

Border entry, baggage, transport, accommodation check-in, SIM/eSIM and university contact.

First week

Complete university enrolment/right-to-study checks, collect student ID, confirm timetable, bank/phone/transport setup and understand visa work conditions.

First month

Understand tenancy, attendance requirements, NHS access, academic support and employer right-to-work checks before taking a job.
