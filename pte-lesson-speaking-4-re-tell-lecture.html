How can I re-tell a lecture without trying to repeat everything?

Understand the task through an original practice example, then study the full demonstration in the LizOn classroom.

Understanding Re-Tell Lecture

Trying to transcribe every word makes it harder to identify relationships. Note the topic, two or three key points and examples only when relevant.

Try an original practice example

Original mini-lecture summary: “A speaker explains that cities grow around transport networks, which can improve access to jobs but also increase housing demand.”

Show the reasoning

Separate the broad topic from the main supporting points. Your response can say that the lecture discussed transport-led urban growth, job access and pressure on housing. Avoid adding unsupported statistics.

How to practise

Practise with short lectures: write three short content notes and build a coherent spoken summary from them.

Common questions

Can I rely on one memorised template?

Templates can help organise a response, but they should never replace listening, understanding and accurate task completion.
