What exactly are you learning?

The goal of How to Organize Your Writing is to understand the task and the method demonstrated in the video. Understand the specific technique, practise it independently and explain the reasoning.

This lesson is part of IELTS Writing Task 2, so the exact assessment requirements depend on the task format.

Step-by-step learning method

  1. Read the instructions. Identify what the task asks and what a complete answer requires.
  2. Observe the technique. Pause Joy Sir’s video and identify the specific reasoning or structure.
  3. Apply independently. Use a different example from suitable IELTS practice material.
  4. Review your mistakes. Identify exactly why a choice or response needs improvement and try again.

Common reasons Bangladesh students lose marks

  • Not fully answering the prompt.
  • Using memorised paragraphs without relevance.
  • Developing body paragraphs without supporting reasons.
  • Watching classes without practising the technique independently.

Questions students ask

How do I prepare for How to Organize Your Writing?

Start with Joy Sir’s classroom video, take brief notes, use a fresh task of the same type and review errors against reliable practice explanations.
