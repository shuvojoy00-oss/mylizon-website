BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Swiss Bachelor admission from Bangladesh is unusually demanding.

Swissuniversities publishes country-specific admission requirements for 2026/27. Bangladesh HSC is recognized as the upper-secondary credential, but individual universities can require entrance exams, prior university admission or even prior university study.

HSC

HSC alone does not mean direct entry everywhere.

For Bangladesh, Swissuniversities lists HSC as the school-leaving certificate, but university-specific requirements apply.

ETH Zurich

HSC + university admission + reduced entrance exam.

For 2026/27, ETH Zurich requires HSC plus proof of admission to the desired subject at a recognised Bangladeshi university, followed by the reduced ETH entrance examination. Otherwise the comprehensive exam may apply.

EPFL

Entrance examination route.

Swissuniversities currently lists Bangladesh HSC plus the EPFL entrance examination for Bachelor admission.

Other universities

Some can require previous university study.

Several Swiss universities use stricter equivalency rules for non-European school certificates. Always use the current Swissuniversities Bangladesh table plus the institution’s own rules.

Masters

Graduate admission is institution and program specific.

A recognised Bachelor degree, academic background, grades, prerequisites and language ability are assessed by the university.

Study Gap

Visa intention matters.

The Bangladesh student checklist asks for a motivation letter explaining your intentions in Switzerland and future plans after study.

HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

Switzerland has multiple institution types — and the language region matters.

Cantonal universities

Research universities such as Zurich, Basel, Bern, Geneva, Lausanne, Fribourg and St. Gallen.

Federal institutes

ETH Zurich and EPFL are Switzerland’s two federal institutes of technology with highly selective STEM and research profiles.

Universities of Applied Sciences

Practice-oriented institutions such as ZHAW, FHNW, HSLU and HES-SO. Admission and tuition differ from traditional universities.

Language regions

German dominates many institutions; French is central in western Switzerland; Italian is important in Ticino. Many Master’s programs are English-taught.

LANGUAGE

German, French, Italian or English can determine your real options.

Bachelor study

Many Bachelor programs are taught mainly in the local language. Language proficiency can therefore be a core admission requirement.

Master’s study

English-taught Master’s programs are common in STEM, business and research fields, especially at ETH, EPFL and major universities.

Bangladesh visa checklist

The Embassy’s 2026 student checklist says English test evidence such as TOEFL/IELTS is compulsory for English-taught study, or the relevant German/French/Italian certificate for other program languages.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Switzerland’s strongest funding is often graduate/research focused.

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships

Federal scholarships primarily support postgraduate researchers and artists, with eligibility and country-specific procedures.

ETH Excellence Scholarship

ETH’s ESOP is a highly competitive merit scholarship for excellent Master’s applicants.

University scholarships

Cantonal universities and foundations offer different merit/need/research funding. Do not assume one Swiss scholarship system.

NATIONAL VISA D · STUDY

Admission is only the first half. The canton makes the residence decision.

01

University admission

Obtain admission/acceptance from the Swiss institution and satisfy academic/language requirements.

02

Prepare study intention

Bangladesh applicants must provide CV and a motivation letter explaining the purpose of study and future plans after graduation.

03

Prepare finances

Provide sponsor responsibility, six-month bank movements, balance confirmation, sponsor ID/relationship and three years of tax-return acknowledgements under the current Dhaka checklist.

04

Police & language evidence

The Bangladesh checklist requires a police clearance certificate less than six months old plus relevant language-test evidence.

05

Submit at Swiss Embassy Dhaka

Bangladeshi nationals need a national visa for stays over 90 days. The application is submitted to the Swiss representation and forwarded to Switzerland.

06

Cantonal authorization

The migration authority of the canton where you will live decides the long-stay authorization. The Embassy cannot issue the visa until that approval arrives.

07

Visa issuance

Current Bangladesh guidance estimates about 8–12 weeks overall, but processing varies considerably by canton and case.

08

Travel & residence registration

After arrival, register with the competent local/cantonal authority within 14 days and complete the residence-permit process.

VISA & CANTONAL FEES

Do not confuse Embassy visa fees with cantonal residence costs.

Student visa fee · Bangladesh

The Swiss Embassy in Dhaka’s current 2026 student checklist lists the student visa processing fee as free of charge.

Cantonal authorization

Cantons can charge separate authorization fees. ETH currently gives Zurich as an example at CHF 95.

Residence registration

Local registration/residence-card fees vary by canton/municipality. ETH currently gives a Zurich example of CHF 182 after immigration.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

The Swiss Embassy’s Bangladesh checklist is very specific.

Academic & admission

Passport, Swiss acceptance/attestation, academic certificates/diplomas, CV, motivation letter and program/language evidence.

Language evidence

TOEFL/IELTS for English study, or the relevant German/French/Italian certification for programs in those languages. The Embassy may assess language ability.

Financial & sponsor evidence

Original sponsor confirmation, six-month bank-account movements, bank balance letter, sponsor passport/ID, proof of relationship and three years of tax-return acknowledgement receipts.

Police clearance

A recent police clearance certificate is explicitly required in the current Bangladesh student checklist and should be no more than six months old at submission.

Family-bond documents

The current checklist asks for proof of family bonds such as birth certificate, marriage certificate, NID and children’s birth certificates where applicable.

Translations / extra documents

The Embassy or canton can request additional documents. Use the current Bangladesh national-visa information sheet because requirements can change.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Family reunification for third-country students is not automatic.

Who may qualify?

For third-country nationals, eligible family members generally include a spouse/registered partner and unmarried children under 18.

B permit is discretionary

Swiss guidance says a B residence permit does not necessarily create an automatic right to family reunification; authorities may grant it depending on the circumstances.

Housing and finances

You need adequate accommodation and must not depend on social assistance. Family members also need health insurance.

Language evidence can matter

For a spouse, proof of enrolment in a local-language course can be required if they cannot already communicate at least orally at A1 level.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Non-EU students cannot simply start working from day one.

Wait six months

Third-country students may generally begin supplementary employment no earlier than six months after the start of the course.

15 hours per week

During semester, approved work is limited to a maximum of 15 hours per week. More work can be possible during semester breaks.

Employer application

The employer must apply to the cantonal authority, employment conditions must comply with local rules, and the university must confirm the job will not delay the studies.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Swiss health insurance is compulsory, but student exemptions can exist.

Non-EU students

Students generally need Swiss-compatible health cover. Some international-student packages can support an exemption from ordinary KVG insurance, subject to cantonal approval.

Canton matters

Exemption practice is not identical everywhere. Follow the health authority of the canton where you live and the university’s international-student guidance.

AFTER STUDY

Swiss higher-education graduates get six months to look for a suitable job.

Graduate job-search period

Length6 months
WhoThird-country graduates
Job targetMust match qualifications
PurposeFind qualifying employment

Swiss degree gives one important advantage.

For non-EU/EFTA graduates of Swiss higher-education institutions, the normal Swiss/EU labour-market precedence can be relaxed where the proposed work represents an overriding scientific or economic interest.

LONG-TERM EMPLOYMENT REALITY

Switzerland is one of Europe’s toughest labour markets for non-EU graduates.

Employer sponsorship still matters

After the graduate search period, a qualifying employer and cantonal/federal work authorization are normally needed.

Highly qualified profile

Third-country work permits focus heavily on qualified specialists and labour-market/economic-interest criteria.

No automatic PR route

A Swiss degree does not guarantee a work permit or permanent residence. Long-term residence depends on nationality, years of lawful residence, integration and permit history.

CITIES & CANTONS

The canton affects costs, language and immigration administration.

Zurich

ETH, University of Zurich and a large finance/technology market — also among the highest living costs.

Lausanne

EPFL and University of Lausanne in the French-speaking canton of Vaud.

Geneva

International organisations, finance and the University of Geneva; high accommodation costs.

Basel

Life sciences, pharmaceuticals and University of Basel.

Bern

Federal capital with University of Bern and a strong public-sector environment.

Lugano

Italian-speaking Switzerland with USI and a smaller international study environment.

ARRIVAL

Visa D gets you in. Local registration activates the residence process.

Before departure

Passport, Visa D, university admission, finances, accommodation, insurance and copies of the full visa file.

At the border

Carry supporting documents because border authorities can verify that the conditions for entry still exist even when you hold a valid visa.

Within 14 days

Register with the residents’ registration office / competent local authority and complete the residence-permit process.

First month

University enrolment, health-insurance compliance, bank/phone/transport setup and understanding your canton’s student-work rules.
