HOW WE VERIFY

Useful information needs a date and a source.

Study abroad rules change. So the platform separates stable planning guidance from immigration facts that need regular verification.

01

Official source first

Government, immigration, official university and recognised admissions sources are preferred for changing rules.

02

Verification dates

Dynamic rules in Radar and the decision datasets carry a review date so students know when the information was checked.

03

No fake certainty

Best Fit explains why a destination surfaced. It does not claim a visa percentage or guarantee admission.

04

Planning cost versus visa funds

The calculator keeps realistic spending separate from official financial evidence wherever the rules allow that distinction.

BEYOND ADMISSION

Getting the offer is not the finish line.

A strong study abroad plan also prepares you for arrival, day to day student life and the transition from study into your next career step.

01

Arrive Prepared

Plan accommodation, airport arrival, local registration, banking, communication, insurance and the first weeks before you fly.

02

Live Within the Rules

Understand the work conditions, attendance expectations, visa responsibilities, family restrictions and practical costs that continue after arrival.

03

Prepare for What Comes Next

Use your study period to build employability, local experience, professional networks and a realistic post study plan instead of waiting until graduation.
