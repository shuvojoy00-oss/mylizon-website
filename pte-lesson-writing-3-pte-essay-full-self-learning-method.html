How do I use self-review to improve my PTE essay?

Learn the reasoning through one worked example, then return to the original LizOn class for a complete demonstration.

Where students struggle

A single automated rating is not proof of an official Pearson score. Start with the exact task instructions and understand what information is needed.

Try an original example

Weak claim: “Education is important for all.” Revised: “Affordable online courses can expand training access for people who cannot commute to a campus.”

Show the suggested reasoning

The revision is more specific and can be developed with an example. Check topic relevance, clarity, grammar and mechanics rather than trusting an unverified score predictor.

A practical review routine

Attempt the task independently, compare your answer with the explanation, then record one specific mistake to address. Repeat the task type using a different passage or recording. Focus on comprehension and accuracy rather than shortcuts or an assumed score improvement.
