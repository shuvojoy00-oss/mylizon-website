QATAR · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Qatar
from Bangladesh.

Qatar combines Qatar University, Education City branch campuses, research-focused graduate study, competitive scholarships and direct access to a fast-growing Gulf economy. For Bangladeshi students, the key is to distinguish university-sponsored student residence, regulated part-time work, and the separate employer-sponsored route needed after graduation.

ADMISSION

Admission is institution-specific, with strong emphasis on school results, English and programme prerequisites.

Bangladeshi applicants should prepare attested academic records and check whether the chosen programme requires mathematics, science, portfolio, interview or standardised test evidence.

Bachelor

12 years of formal education

Qatar University requires international-school applicants to have completed twelve years of formal education and the required core subjects.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Graduate admission depends on programme relevance, GPA, English and department-specific criteria.

PhD

Graduate qualification + research fit

Research programmes commonly assess academic record, proposal, supervisor fit and English proficiency.

INTAKES

Fall is the main intake across Qatar’s universities.

Application dates differ sharply by institution. Qatar University’s Fall 2026 undergraduate cycle is already closed, while HBKU opened its next graduate cycle on 1 October 2026.

MAIN INTAKE

August / September

The broadest Bachelor, Master and PhD programme choice. Start planning well before the calendar year of entry.

NEXT LIVE EXAMPLE

HBKU 2027 cycle

International graduate applications are due 1 February 2027. HBKU undergraduate applications are due 1 March 2027.

Selected programmes may offer Spring entry, but this is not universal. International and scholarship deadlines can be earlier because institutions need time for admission, funding and residence sponsorship.

STUDENT VISA + RESIDENCE

Your university normally plays a central role in student sponsorship.

At Qatar University, international students without existing Qatar residence must apply either through the student-visa route or an eligible scholarship route. QU facilitates student visa issuance for admitted students who meet the requirements.

01

Apply to the university

Submit academic documents and complete the international admissions process.

02

Choose visa or scholarship route

At QU, international applications are not considered complete without the required visa or scholarship pathway.

03

Submit sponsorship documents

Provide passport, financial and university-requested immigration documents.

04

University facilitates student visa

The sponsoring institution coordinates the residence process for eligible admitted students.

05

Travel to Qatar

Use the approved student entry permission and follow the university’s arrival instructions.

06

Complete medical / identity formalities

Residence processes can include medical and identity requirements after arrival.

07

Receive Qatar ID / residence status

Your legal student residence remains linked to the sponsoring institution.

08

Maintain active enrolment

Keep university sponsorship and residence conditions valid throughout study.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

A student residence does not automatically let you sponsor dependants.

Qatar University states that its student visa is for the student only and does not give the student the right to sponsor family members. This is especially important for Bangladeshi applicants planning to move with a spouse or children.

Family housing

Available to limited graduate categories

QU has limited family housing for some married international graduate students, including eligible graduate assistants, research assistants and externally funded students. Housing eligibility is not the same as immigration sponsorship.

Family residence

Separate immigration eligibility applies

Ordinary family residence sponsorship is tied to separate residence and employment conditions. Do not budget for dependants until the university and Qatar immigration route are both confirmed.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Qatar now has approved part-time pathways for active university students.

Jusour’s Sahem programme is open to eligible residents and nationals actively enrolled at a Qatar-based university or higher education institution.

Academic term

Up to 20 hours / week

Current Sahem FAQ permits up to twenty hours weekly during the academic year.

Breaks

Up to 40 hours / week

Summer, winter and other term breaks can allow up to forty hours weekly.

University jobs

On-campus routes also exist

Qatar University and Education City institutions operate separate student employment programmes with their own eligibility rules.

Sahem describes the arrangement as a part-time training contract because Qatar does not have a general standalone student part-time-work legislation. Students should use approved channels rather than assuming unrestricted off-campus employment.
Jusour Sahem student work FAQ ↗
HEALTH + RESIDENCE

Health access follows residence and university arrangements.

Qatar University notes that Qatar residents can obtain an HMC health card, while international students should follow the sponsoring university’s medical and insurance instructions.

Student residence

University sponsorship

The institution can provide formal letters and sponsorship support required for residence-related services.

Health card

HMC access for residents

Residents can apply for a health card through the relevant Primary Health Care Center process.

AFTER GRADUATION

Eligible graduates can extend residence for one year to search for work.

Jusour’s Graduates Residency Extension is designed for recent international graduates of Qatar-based higher education institutions who remain under university sponsorship.

Residency extension

1 year

The current programme grants a one-year extension to eligible graduates while they search for employment.

Eligibility timing

Apply within 6 months of graduation

The applicant must still hold valid Qatar residence and remain in Qatar when applying. Current age eligibility is above 18 and up to 28.

This one-year extension is for job search. It is not an unrestricted work permit. Once hired, the graduate needs the proper employer-sponsored work and residence status.

WHERE TO STUDY

Qatar is essentially one connected Doha education market.

The most useful comparison is campus ecosystem rather than different cities.

Qatar University area

National university ecosystem

Large campus, student housing and direct university services.

Education City

International university cluster

Multiple branch campuses, shared student housing, research facilities and Qatar Foundation opportunities.

Doha

Employer and internship centre

Finance, aviation, energy, technology, media, health and public-sector opportunities are concentrated around the capital.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Let the sponsoring university guide the first residence steps.

Student sponsorship is institution-led, so arrival instructions can differ between Qatar University and Education City campuses.

01

Complete university check-in

Submit originals and activate enrolment.

02

Complete residence formalities

Follow medical, identity and Qatar ID instructions from the sponsoring institution.

03

Set up housing

Move into university or private accommodation.

04

Open local services

Complete banking, mobile and health-card arrangements when eligible.

05

Use approved work routes

If working while studying, use your university employment system or an eligible Sahem pathway.

MAKE IT PERSONAL

Choose Qatar with the university, scholarship, housing and work pathway together.

Qatar can be exceptionally attractive when scholarship or institutional funding is strong. For self-funded students, tuition and Doha housing should be calculated carefully before committing.
