SINGAPORE · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Singapore
from Bangladesh.

Singapore offers globally strong universities, English-medium education, close industry links and excellent graduate employability. It is also a high-cost destination with competitive admissions. For Bangladeshi students, the key immigration route is ICA's Student's Pass, with tightly defined work rights and no generic open post-study work visa.

Singapore in one view

Best suited to academically strong students who can manage higher tuition and living costs and who value university reputation, technology, finance, business and Asian employer access.

Singapore does not have one automatic open post-study work visa

Eligible graduates from recognised Institutes of Higher Learning may apply for an ICA Long-Term Visit Pass to seek employment. But the LTVP is not a work pass. Once employed, the employer must secure the appropriate Employment Pass, S Pass or other qualifying work authorisation.

ADMISSION

Singapore's public universities are highly competitive and qualification rules are institution-specific.

Bangladesh HSC alone is not automatically sufficient for every university. For example, NTU currently requires Bangladesh HSC applicants to have at least GPA 4.0 plus an additional recognised qualification.

Bachelor

HSC + university-specific additional evidence

Check each university carefully. Standardised tests, additional qualifications or subject prerequisites may be required.

Master

Strong recognised Bachelor degree

Competitive programmes may require relevant academic background, GMAT or GRE, work experience, portfolio or interviews.

PhD

Research strength + supervisor fit

Research statement, strong academics, references and funding fit are central.

For AY2027 admission, NTU currently lists the Bangladesh HSC application window from 15 October 2026 to 20 January 2027. Use university-specific dates rather than assuming one national deadline.

INTAKES

August is the dominant undergraduate intake.

Autonomous universities generally recruit well in advance. Selected postgraduate and private programmes can have January or multiple annual intakes.

MAIN INTAKE

August

Main undergraduate academic-year start at leading public universities.

OTHER STARTS

January / rolling, selected

More common for graduate, executive and private-institution programmes.

Apply much earlier than the start date. The university admission cycle can close many months before classes, and ICA requires the Student's Pass application around two to three months before the course begins.

LIVING COST

Singapore is expensive by Asian student-market standards.

NUS currently estimates a single student's monthly essentials at about S$1,200 to S$2,100 with on-campus housing and S$1,550 to S$3,400 off campus.

NUS monthly planning examples

On-campus accommodationS$500–900
Off-campus accommodationS$650–1,800
FoodS$300–800
Public transportS$100–300
Personal expensesS$300–500
NUS's total estimate excludes major discretionary spending, travel and electronic purchases. Housing availability is not guaranteed, especially for coursework graduate students.

TUITION

Singapore tuition changes dramatically depending on the MOE Tuition Grant.

International students can pay subsidised fees when awarded the Tuition Grant, but the grant carries service obligations. Students without the grant pay much higher non-subsidised fees.

NTU 2026 general programmes

S$21,400 subsidised

For non-ASEAN international students receiving the Tuition Grant. Comparable non-subsidised 2026 fees are around S$36,350 to S$40,600 depending on lab content.

SMU 2026 general degrees

S$26,200 subsidised

Other international students with Tuition Grant. Non-subsidised fees are S$47,700 for most listed undergraduate degrees.

Medicine

Much higher

NTU 2026 medicine tuition for other international students with subsidy is S$91,250 annually.

These are real 2026 university examples, not a national universal price. Always use your programme's exact fee and check the Tuition Grant bond before accepting subsidised tuition.

TUITION GRANT + SCHOLARSHIPS

Bangladeshi students have access to some international scholarships, but not every regional award.

The key distinction is between the MOE Tuition Grant, which subsidises tuition under its own terms, and competitive university scholarships.

MOE Tuition Grant

Subsidised tuition

Eligible international students can receive substantially lower tuition. Review the service obligation carefully before accepting.

NUS International Undergraduate Scholarship

Open to non-Singapore citizens

NUS currently considers eligible international applicants through the undergraduate admissions timeline.

Science & Technology Undergraduate Scholarship

Asian applicants

NUS lists this for citizens of Asian countries or regions outside Singapore, subject to the scholarship's programme and selection rules.

Bangladesh is not an ASEAN member, so do not advertise ASEAN-only scholarships to Bangladeshi applicants. Use international or Asia-wide scholarship routes instead.

ICA STUDENT'S PASS

Foreign full-time students at approved institutions use ICA's Student's Pass process.

You must first be accepted by an eligible institution. ICA then handles the immigration application online.

01

Receive full-time admission

Part-time, evening and weekend-only courses are not eligible for a Student's Pass.

02

Receive registration details

Your institution provides the Registration Acknowledgement Letter or application reference information.

03

Apply to ICA online

For Institutes of Higher Learning, apply at least two months and not more than three months before course commencement.

04

Pay processing fee

ICA currently charges a non-refundable S$45 Student's Pass application processing fee.

05

Receive approval / IPA

ICA states IHL applications are normally processed within one week, or within two weeks where a visa is required, though some take longer.

06

Travel to Singapore

Use the approval documentation and complete the entry requirements.

07

Complete formalities + biometrics

ICA or your school will schedule completion and biometric enrolment.

08

Receive digital Student's Pass

ICA currently charges S$60 for issuance and S$30 for a Multiple Journey Visa where applicable.

FAMILY

Singapore does not provide a broad automatic dependant route simply because an adult holds a Student's Pass.

Family immigration is tightly category-specific. Students should not assume a spouse or children can simply be added to the Student's Pass.

Adult university student

No automatic family entitlement

Check ICA eligibility separately if family accompaniment is essential to your study plan.

School-age child route

Parent or grandparent LTVP exists

ICA allows one parent or grandparent of a child studying on a Student's Pass to apply for LTVP with a local sponsor, but this is not the normal spouse route for university students.

ICA Long-Term Visit Pass categories ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Eligible students can work up to 16 hours per week during term without a separate work pass.

This work-pass exemption only applies to full-time students holding a valid Student's Pass and studying at institutions specifically listed by the Ministry of Manpower.

School term

Maximum 16 hours / week

Or an approved industrial attachment or internship that contributes to graduation requirements.

Vacation

Work allowed for eligible students

MOM permits qualifying students at listed institutions to work during vacation without a separate work pass.

Important limitation

Institution list matters

A Student's Pass by itself does not automatically give every foreign student the right to work.

MOM foreign student work exemption ↗
HEALTH + INSURANCE

University health fees and insurance arrangements vary by institution.

Singapore does not use one single international-student health premium across all universities. Check your institution's compulsory insurance, health service fees and exclusions before arrival.

University coverage

Institution-specific

Many universities bundle student insurance and health-service arrangements into compulsory student fees.

Private treatment

Can be expensive

Understand coverage limits before relying on the university policy alone.

AFTER GRADUATION

Eligible IHL graduates can apply for an LTVP to seek employment, but they still need a work pass to work.

This is a job-search residence option, not an open graduate work visa.

Graduate LTVP

Employment-seeking route

ICA allows graduates from listed Institutes of Higher Learning to apply for LTVP while seeking employment.

LTVP fee

S$45 application + S$60 issuance

A S$30 Multiple Journey Visa fee may also apply.

Once employed

Employer applies for work pass

After studies end, MOM requires the appropriate Employment Pass, S Pass or other work authorisation for employment.

Do not market the graduate LTVP as automatic work permission. ICA gives eligible graduates a lawful stay route to seek work, while MOM controls the employment pass.
ICA graduate LTVP ↗
WHERE TO LIVE

Singapore is one city-state, so housing location matters more than choosing a separate university city.

Commute time, rent and campus access should drive your housing decision.

Kent Ridge / west

NUS access

Convenient for NUS and nearby research or technology districts.

Jurong / Pioneer

NTU access

Best for reducing commute to NTU's western campus.

City centre

SMU + employers

Excellent access but generally more expensive housing.

Outer MRT areas

Budget trade-off

Longer commute can reduce private rental costs.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Complete ICA formalities before treating your stay as fully settled.

Your school may arrange offsite enrolment or ICA may require an appointment at the ICA Services Centre.

01

Complete university registration

Finish matriculation and any document verification.

02

Complete Student's Pass formalities

Attend your scheduled ICA or school-arranged appointment.

03

Biometrics

Provide fingerprints and photo where required.

04

Set up banking + phone

Useful for tuition, accommodation and employment administration.

05

Check work eligibility before accepting a job

Confirm both your institution and employment arrangement qualify under MOM's exemption.
