IELTS Reading Matching Information: Find the Right Paragraph

Matching Information asks which paragraph contains a particular detail, example, reason, comparison or explanation. It is different from Matching Headings, which asks for the main idea.

Matching Headings-এ main idea খুঁজবেন; Matching Information-এ নির্দিষ্ট তথ্য কোন paragraph-এ আছে তা খুঁজবেন।

Step-by-step approach

  1. Read the requested information and identify the specific kind of detail being asked for.
  2. Scan the paragraphs for relevant meaning, names, dates, examples and synonyms.
  3. Read around each possible match carefully; the same words alone are not enough.
  4. Check task instructions: a paragraph letter may be reusable when the instructions explicitly allow it.

Try an original example

Paragraph B explains why a bus route was changed after a bridge closed. Question: Which paragraph gives a reason for altering a transport service?

Show suggested answer and explanation

B. It explains the reason for the route change, rather than just mentioning buses.

Common mistakes to avoid

Avoid memorized responses that ignore what the task asks. Focus on accurate comprehension, practice with varied materials and review feedback. Techniques support preparation; they do not guarantee a score or a particular answer.
