How do I select more than one supported answer in PTE Reading?

A practical response to a common preparation difficulty, connected to the original free lesson.

Why this task is challenging

Choosing options that are generally believable instead of being supported by the passage. Before attempting the question, identify exactly what the instructions require.

Original practice example

Passage: “The programme reduced energy costs and improved equipment reliability, but staff training took longer than expected.”

Show explanation and answer

If the question asks for benefits, the supported answers are lower energy costs and better reliability. Lengthy staff training is a challenge, not a benefit.

How to practise the method

Try another item without looking at an answer first. Compare your response against the text or audio and note the specific word, relationship or instruction you misunderstood. Repeat with varied material rather than relying on one shortcut.
