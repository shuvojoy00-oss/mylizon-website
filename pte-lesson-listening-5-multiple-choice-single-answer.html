How do I choose the main answer in a PTE Listening MCQ?

A practical response to a common preparation difficulty, connected to the original free lesson.

Why this task is challenging

Selecting the first plausible option before hearing the speaker's conclusion. Before attempting the question, identify exactly what the instructions require.

Original practice example

A speaker says a local project improved bus connections, with the greatest benefit being shorter commute times.

Show explanation and answer

If the question asks for the main benefit, shorter commutes is the answer. Better connections are related, but not automatically the stated main benefit.

How to practise the method

Try another item without looking at an answer first. Compare your response against the text or audio and note the specific word, relationship or instruction you misunderstood. Repeat with varied material rather than relying on one shortcut.
