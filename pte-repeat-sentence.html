Improve your ability to listen to a sentence and repeat it accurately with clear pronunciation and natural rhythm.

Practical method

  1. Listen to the whole sentence instead of trying to translate each word.
  2. Notice meaningful word groups and sentence rhythm.
  3. Repeat what you heard clearly and at a natural pace.
  4. Practise with varied sentence lengths, then compare your recording to the source.

How to practise effectively

Try the task with several different examples. Record your answers or working process, review mistakes, and compare your performance over multiple attempts. One shortcut or template cannot guarantee a test score.
