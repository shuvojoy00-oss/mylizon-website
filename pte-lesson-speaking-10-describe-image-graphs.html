Understanding Describe Image: Graphs

Mentioning every visible number can make a response difficult to follow. Choose trends, highs/lows and a useful comparison.

Try an original practice example

Original practice graph: Product A rises from 20 to 45 units, while Product B falls from 50 to 30.

Show the reasoning

State the overall contrast and cite relevant endpoints. Do not list every intermediate figure or infer causes absent from the image.

How to practise

Practise a concise description, then listen for whether your figures and units are stated accurately.

Common questions

Can I rely on one memorised template?

Templates can help organise a response, but they should never replace listening, understanding and accurate task completion.
