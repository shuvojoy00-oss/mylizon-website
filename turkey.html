TÜRKİYE · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Türkiye
from Bangladesh.

Türkiye combines public and private universities, English and Turkish-medium degrees, strong government scholarships and access to Europe and Asia. But the route has its own structure: undergraduate applicants may need TR-YÖS or accepted equivalents, Bangladesh education visas now go through Mosaic Visa, and work rights require a separate work permit.

ADMISSION

International admission is university-led, but TR-YÖS is a major undergraduate route.

Universities decide which international exam results they accept. TR-YÖS is the national Exam for Foreign Students for Higher Education and is widely used for undergraduate admission, alongside accepted equivalents such as SAT, IB, GCE A Level or other credentials where the university allows them.

Bachelor

HSC + accepted exam route

Bangladeshi applicants normally apply with SSC and HSC records plus the university’s accepted international entrance result, often TR-YÖS for public universities.

Master

Bachelor degree + programme criteria

Graduate admissions are handled by universities and institutes. GPA, ALES or GRE, language proof, references, interview and research fit vary by programme.

PhD

Master degree + research fit

Doctoral applicants commonly need a relevant graduate degree, language proof, research alignment and programme-specific selection.

Ankara University’s 2026 international undergraduate call explicitly accepts TR-YÖS plus equivalent results including IB, ABITUR, TQDK, GCE A Level and SAT. Do not assume every university uses the same list.

Compare state, foundation and specialist universities separately.

Türkiye’s system includes large state universities, research-focused public institutions, private foundation universities and specialised technical or medical schools.

INTAKES + TR-YÖS

September is the main university start, but entrance-exam timing begins much earlier.

Undergraduate applicants should plan around TR-YÖS and university application windows rather than only the semester start.

MAIN INTAKE

September / October

The widest degree choice. Many international undergraduate application rounds run in late spring or summer.

SPRING

February, selected programmes

Some graduate and selected university programmes offer spring entry, but it is not as broad as autumn.

TURKISH · ENGLISH

Programme language changes the application and the first year.

Türkiye offers both Turkish-medium and English-medium degrees. Universities decide accepted English exams, Turkish proficiency levels and whether a preparatory language year is required.

English programmesUniversity-specific proofTOEFL or institutional proficiency tests are common.
Turkish programmesTÖMER / Turkish proofRequired level depends on institution and programme.
Prep yearCommonStudents below the required language level may complete preparatory study.
Scholarship students1 year Turkish courseIncluded under Türkiye Scholarships full-time programmes.
Do not assume IELTS is accepted everywhere. Some Turkish universities rely on TOEFL or their own English proficiency examination.
LIVING COST

City and housing choice matter more than a single national figure.

Türkiye does not provide one useful national living-cost number for every international student. Istanbul can be materially more expensive than Ankara, Eskişehir, Konya or other student cities, especially for private rent.

TUITION

State and foundation universities can sit in completely different price bands.

International tuition is set by each university and programme. Public tuition can be comparatively affordable, while private foundation university sticker prices may be much higher before scholarship discounts.

State universities

Programme-specific annual fees

2026 fee tables are published by universities such as METU and Ankara University. Engineering, medicine and English-medium programmes may cost more.

Foundation universities

Higher sticker tuition

Private universities commonly offer partial tuition scholarships or discounts, so compare net tuition rather than headline fee alone.

Medicine / dentistry

Higher-cost category

Health programmes often have both higher admission thresholds and substantially higher international tuition.

TÜRKİYE SCHOLARSHIPS

The government scholarship is one of Türkiye’s strongest advantages.

Türkiye Scholarships is a competitive government-funded programme open to international applicants at associate, Bachelor, Master and PhD levels and includes university placement.

2026 application10 Jan – 20 FebOne main annual window.
Undergraduate minimum70%90% for medicine, dentistry and pharmacy.
Graduate minimum75%Plus programme-specific selection.
PlacementIncludedThe scholarship also handles university and department placement.
Full-time coverage

More than tuition

Current programme coverage includes tuition, accommodation, health insurance, one year Turkish language course, monthly stipend and one-off flight ticket at the beginning and graduation.

Current monthly stipend

6,500 / 9,500 / 13,000 TL

The official full-time scholarship page lists undergraduate, Master and PhD stipends respectively at these current monthly levels.

Age limits also apply: under 21 for associate/Bachelor, under 30 for Master and under 35 for PhD under the current scholarship criteria.

EDUCATION VISA · BANGLADESH

A study visa is not a Turkish e-Visa.

The Turkish e-Visa is for tourism or commerce. Bangladeshi students need the regular education visa process and, from February 2026, submit through Mosaic Visa in Dhaka.

01

Receive university admission

Education visa applications begin after enrolment or acceptance from the Turkish institution.

02

Complete sticker-visa pre-application

Use Türkiye’s official visa pre-application process for study purposes.

03

Prepare academic + financial documents

Admission, passport, education records, sponsorship and financial evidence are central parts of the file.

04

Submit through Mosaic Visa

Mosaic Visa is the only authorised visa application centre in Bangladesh as of 1 February 2026.

05

Receive education visa

Travel only after the study-purpose visa is issued.

06

Register at university

Complete enrolment and address documentation after arrival.

07

Apply for student residence permit

Degree students need the residence permit for legal stay beyond the visa entry period.

08

Keep permit and address current

University, province and passport changes can trigger notification or new permit requirements.

Bangladesh-specific document requirements can change. Use Mosaic Visa and the Embassy of Türkiye in Dhaka for the current checklist instead of relying on old agency lists.
Embassy of Türkiye in Dhaka · Visa Applications 2026 ↗
SPOUSE · CHILDREN

A student can support spouse and children for a family residence permit.

The Presidency of Migration Management states that a student residence permit allows the student to be the supporter of a spouse and children in family residence permit applications, but does not create the same right for other relatives.

Who may be supported

Spouse + children

The student permit can support eligible family residence applications for spouse and children, subject to family residence conditions.

Work

Family residence is not automatic work permission

A dependant who wants to work must meet the appropriate Turkish work-authorisation rules.

Migration Management · Residence permit types ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Student residence does not automatically mean unrestricted work.

Foreign students can work if they obtain a work permit. Associate and undergraduate students gain the right to work only after completing the first year, and their weekly work is capped at 24 hours.

Bachelor / associate

After first year

The right to work begins after the first year of study.

Weekly limit

Maximum 24 hours

The Migration Management FAQ states a 24-hour weekly maximum for associate and undergraduate students.

Master / PhD

Work permit still required

Graduate and doctoral students may work if they obtain the required work permit.

Do not budget on the assumption that arriving with a student residence permit allows immediate work. Work permission is a separate legal step.
Migration Management · Student work rights ↗
HEALTH INSURANCE

Health insurance is part of the residence-permit plan.

International students can request Turkish General Health Insurance within three months of admission where eligible. If they do not use that route, valid private health insurance is required for the student residence permit.

General Health Insurance

Three-month window

Students who request GHI within the specified three-month period may use that coverage for residence purposes.

Private insurance

Required if GHI is not used

If the student loses the opportunity to enter GHI by missing the timeframe, private health insurance is required.

Migration Management · Student health insurance ↗
AFTER GRADUATION

Türkiye offers a limited post-graduation stay, not an automatic open work visa.

Graduates who apply within six months after completing a Turkish higher-education programme can receive a one-time short-term residence permit for up to one year.

Graduate residence

Up to one year

The short-term residence permit is available once to qualifying graduates who apply within six months after graduation.

Employment

Work permit is separate

To work after graduation, the graduate still needs the appropriate work permit. The graduate residence permit itself is not an open work visa.

For longer-term residence, Türkiye’s general long-term residence route normally requires eight years of continuous residence and other conditions. Student residence time may be treated differently in residence calculations, so do not market study alone as a direct settlement route.
Migration Management · Graduate short-term residence ↗
WHERE TO STUDY

City choice changes both cost and academic experience.

Compare the institution first, then housing, transport, language exposure, employment market and lifestyle around it.

Istanbul

Largest academic + business market

Huge university choice and professional network, but generally the highest housing pressure.

Ankara

Government + research

METU, Hacettepe and Ankara University with strong student infrastructure.

Izmir

Coastal + university city

Large student population and major public institutions on the Aegean coast.

Bursa

Industry + lower-cost potential

Manufacturing links and access to Istanbul without being in its central housing market.

Eskişehir

Student-focused city

Known for a strong student population and generally more manageable living costs.

Antalya

Tourism + international environment

Popular coastal city with hospitality and tourism exposure.

AFTER ARRIVAL

The education visa gets you in. The residence permit keeps you legal.

After university registration, international degree students should complete the student residence permit process and keep their address and education status current.

01

Register at university

Complete final enrolment and obtain current student documents.

02

Confirm address

Your Turkish address is part of the residence-permit process.

03

Arrange health insurance

Use GHI if eligible within the required period or purchase compliant private insurance.

04

Apply for student residence permit

Follow the Migration Management and university instructions for your province.

05

Notify changes

Moving province, changing institution or changing passport can require updates or a new residence process.
