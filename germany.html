Bangladesh HSC alone normally does not give direct Bachelor admission

DAAD Bangladesh states that after a Bangladeshi class-12/HSC school-leaving certificate, direct university admission is generally not available. Students normally use Studienkolleg + Feststellungsprüfung, or after at least one successful academic year at a recognised Bangladeshi university may qualify for direct subject-restricted admission in the same or a related field.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Your HSC route depends on whether you already completed university study.

HSC only

Usually Studienkolleg first.

For Bangladesh HSC applicants without prior university study, DAAD’s admission database generally points to subject-restricted admission to a preparatory course and Feststellungsprüfung rather than direct Bachelor entry.

1 year Bachelor

Can unlock direct subject-restricted admission.

At least one successful academic year at a recognised higher-education institution in Bangladesh can qualify you for direct study in the same or a related subject area, subject to the university’s final decision.

Studienkolleg

Normally one academic year.

You enter a subject-specific preparatory course and pass the Feststellungsprüfung. German around B1/B2 is commonly needed for Studienkolleg entry.

Masters

A recognised relevant Bachelor is assessed programme by programme.

German universities examine degree recognition, credits, subject fit, grades and language. There is no single national Bangladesh CGPA threshold.

Anabin / DAAD

Check recognition before applying.

Use the DAAD admission database and anabin for orientation, then confirm the final requirements with the university or uni-assist.

APS

Bangladesh applicants do not use APS India.

DAAD Bangladesh explicitly describes APS India for qualifications issued by Indian institutions. Do not copy the Indian APS process onto a Bangladeshi academic file.

STUDIENKOLLEG

The preparatory route is subject-specific.

T / TI course

Engineering, mathematics, natural sciences and technical subjects.

W / WW course

Economics, business and related social-science fields.

M course

Medical, biological and pharmaceutical subjects.

G / S / SW

Humanities, languages and selected social-science pathways depending on university/FH Studienkolleg.

UNI-ASSIST · VPD

Germany does not have one universal application portal.

uni-assist

Many universities use uni-assist to evaluate foreign qualifications and forward eligible applications.

VPD

Some universities ask for a preliminary review documentation (VPD) from uni-assist and then require a second application directly to the university.

Current fees

uni-assist charges €75 for the first chosen course in a semester and €30 for each additional course in the same semester.

GERMAN · IELTS · ENGLISH

German unlocks far more Bachelor options and improves the labour market.

German-taught degrees

TestDaF, DSH, telc C1 Hochschule and other recognised certificates are commonly used. Exact level depends on the programme.

Studienkolleg

German is essential. DAAD Bangladesh notes roughly B2 as a common preparatory-course benchmark, though the entrance test and institution decide.

English-taught Master’s

IELTS/TOEFL requirements are programme-specific. English-only study is much more common at Master’s than Bachelor level.

TUITION

“Germany is tuition-free” is only partly true.

Most public universities

Many public universities still charge no general tuition for standard Bachelor/Master study, but students pay semester contributions.

Baden-Württemberg

Most non-EU international students pay €1,500 per semester at public universities in the state, subject to exemptions.

TUM

New non-EU students at TUM generally pay programme tuition: usually €2,000–€3,000 per semester for Bachelor and €4,000–€6,000 for Master, plus semester fees.

Private universities

Private institutions can charge substantial tuition and should not be compared with public-university cost assumptions.

SCHOLARSHIPS

DAAD is the starting point, not a guarantee.

DAAD scholarship database

Search by country, degree level and subject. Many major DAAD schemes focus on Master, PhD and research rather than first-degree study.

Deutschlandstipendium

Participating universities can award merit-based funding, including to international students.

University / foundation funding

German foundations and individual universities offer separate awards with academic and social criteria.

NATIONAL STUDENT VISA · DHAKA

Bangladeshi students apply through the German Embassy in Dhaka.

01

Admission / Studienkolleg place

Obtain the appropriate university, preparatory-course or study-related admission documentation.

02

Blocked account / finance

Complete the €11,904 blocked account or use another accepted financing route.

03

Health insurance

Prepare travel/entry coverage and the German health-insurance evidence required for enrolment/residence.

04

Register online

The German Embassy Dhaka requires study applicants, including PhD applicants, to use its online appointment/application system.

05

Prepare complete documents

The Embassy only accepts complete applications. Follow the current Dhaka study-visa checklist exactly.

06

Embassy appointment

Attend the scheduled appointment in Dhaka with original documents and required copies.

07

Decision

Student national visas involve German immigration authorities and processing times can vary. Apply as early as possible after admission.

08

Travel & residence permit

After arrival, register your address and apply for the study residence permit at the local Ausländerbehörde.

VISA COSTS

The German national-visa fee is €75 for adults.

National visa

€75, paid in BDT at the Embassy’s current exchange rate.

Applicants under 18

€37.50 equivalent under the current fee table.

Document verification

If document verification is required, the German Embassy Dhaka currently lists an additional BDT 28,000 fee.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Admission and visa evidence need to tell the same story.

Academic admission

SSC/HSC, prior university transcripts where relevant, Bachelor/Master documents, language certificate, uni-assist/VPD documents and programme-specific evidence.

Visa file

Passport, admission, blocked-account evidence, academic records, language evidence, CV/motivation, insurance and the current Embassy checklist documents.

Blocked account

Use a provider that meets German blocked-account requirements and ensure the full required amount has actually been credited before visa assessment.

Document verification

The Embassy can require verification of Bangladeshi academic/civil documents, which can add both cost and processing time.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Family reunification is possible, but student-family cases have conditions.

Spouse / minor children

Family reunification can be possible for students with a valid residence permit, subject to adequate housing, finances and the family-reunification rules.

Marriage timing can matter

For ordinary student family reunification, authorities can consider whether the marriage existed before the student’s move and whether the expected stay exceeds one year.

Spouse language

Basic German can be required for a spouse unless an exemption applies.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Germany gives two practical ways to calculate student work.

140 full days / 280 half days

Third-country students can work within this annual allocation without Federal Employment Agency approval.

Or up to 20 hours/week

The newer framework also permits student employment up to 20 hours per week as an alternative calculation.

Student assistant jobs

Academic/student assistant activities can be treated more favourably and may not count against the ordinary annual allocation.

Semester breaks

Full-time work can be possible during semester breaks under the applicable student-employment calculation.

Official student work rules ↗
HEALTH INSURANCE

Health insurance is mandatory for enrolment and residence.

Students under the standard age limits

Most degree students enrol in German statutory student health insurance unless an exemption/private-insurance situation applies.

Before enrolment

Make sure the insurer sends the required digital insurance status to the university; travel insurance alone is not a long-term substitute for German student health cover.

AFTER STUDY

German university graduates can stay up to 18 months to find skilled employment.

Graduate job-search residence

Maximum duration18 months
PurposeFind qualified employment
Work while searchingAny occupation
Switch after jobSkilled worker / EU Blue Card

You do not need to leave Germany first.

After successfully completing a German degree, you can change directly into the graduate job-search residence permit or, if you already have a qualifying job offer, directly into skilled-worker or Blue Card status.

SKILLED WORK & SETTLEMENT

Germany offers a strong long-term route after graduation.

Skilled-worker permit

A recognised German degree and qualifying skilled employment can support a residence permit for qualified professionals.

EU Blue Card

Graduates in qualifying jobs can use the Blue Card where the current salary and employment conditions are met.

Special graduate settlement route

Graduates of German universities can become eligible for a settlement permit after more than 2 years of qualified employment, provided the statutory conditions are met.

CITIES

Housing pressure often matters more than tuition.

Berlin

Large international programme and start-up market, but severe housing competition.

Munich

Top universities and employers, but among Germany’s highest living costs.

Aachen

Engineering-focused student city centred around RWTH, generally cheaper than Munich.

Stuttgart

Strong automotive and engineering labour market; Baden-Württemberg tuition rule applies to many non-EU students.

Hamburg

Large port, logistics, aviation and media economy with major universities.

Leipzig / Dresden

Lower-cost eastern German study options with growing technology and research ecosystems.

ARRIVAL

Address registration and residence permit should happen early.

Before departure

Passport, national visa, admission, blocked-account confirmation, insurance and accommodation.

Anmeldung

Register your address with the local registration office after moving into permanent accommodation.

University enrolment

Complete enrolment, semester contribution and health-insurance verification.

Residence permit

Apply with the local Ausländerbehörde before the national visa expires and understand your work conditions.
