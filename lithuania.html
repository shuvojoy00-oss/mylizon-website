Major 2026 change: first and second year Bachelor work is now limited

For non-EU students whose Lithuanian study residence permit was issued under the rules effective from 22 May 2026, first and second year Bachelor students can generally work no more than 20 hours per week. Third/fourth year Bachelor students, Master students, doctoral students and residents can work up to 40 hours per week. Holidays and study placements are treated separately.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Lithuania requires academic recognition of foreign qualifications.

The SKVC framework recognises Bangladesh HSC and Bachelor qualifications, but recognition and university admission are separate decisions.

HSC

SKVC lists Bangladesh HSC as the secondary qualification.

For recognition, the country-specific document list requires the Higher Secondary Certificate, HSC transcript, Secondary School Certificate and SSC transcript.

Bachelor

Bangladesh Bachelor documents are explicitly listed.

For higher-education recognition, SKVC lists the Bachelor diploma and diploma supplement/transcript as the core documents.

Recognition

Recognition does not equal admission.

SKVC can grant full, limited or conditional recognition. The university still decides selection, grades, subjects and programme-specific eligibility.

University recognition

Some Lithuanian institutions perform recognition themselves.

Authorised universities can assess foreign qualifications directly, while others use SKVC. Always follow the chosen institution’s instructions before filing separately.

Masters

A recognised relevant Bachelor is the starting point.

Master admission depends on degree level, subject match, grades, credits and language requirements set by the institution.

English

No one national IELTS score applies.

English-taught programmes commonly ask for B2-equivalent evidence, but IELTS/TOEFL/PTE rules and exemptions are institution-specific.

UNIVERSITY VS COLLEGE

Lithuania has two major higher-education sectors.

Universitetas

University studies

Academic and research-oriented Bachelor, Master and doctoral education. Universities can also offer professionally oriented programmes.

Kolegija

College / professional Bachelor

Professionally oriented higher education focused on applied skills and labour-market preparation. Graduates receive a Professional Bachelor degree.

IELTS · TOEFL · ENGLISH

English requirements belong to the programme, not one national immigration score.

English-taught degrees

Many universities accept IELTS, TOEFL and equivalent B2-level evidence. Exact minimums and exemptions vary by institution.

Lithuanian-taught degrees

Programmes taught in Lithuanian require the relevant Lithuanian-language competence set by the institution.

MONEY & SUFFICIENT MEANS

The 2026 student living-funds benchmark is approximately €576.50 per month.

2026 immigration benchmark

Student monthly amount€576.50
12-month equivalent€6,918
Return funds benchmark€1,153
Tuition / accommodationAdditional

Why €576.50?

Lithuania’s student sufficient-means rule is linked to 0.5 of the national minimum monthly wage. The 2026 minimum wage is €1,153, making the student benchmark €576.50/month. Return funds use one minimum monthly wage.

Immigration formulas can change when Lithuania changes the minimum wage. Always verify the amount in MIGRIS when you submit the actual residence application.
TUITION

Lithuania is one of the more affordable EU study destinations.

Bachelor

Study in Lithuania currently gives a national planning figure starting from about €1,300/year, though many English-taught programmes cost more.

Master

National guidance starts from about €2,300/year. Programme examples commonly fall in the €4,000–€7,000 range.

PhD

Study in Lithuania gives an average starting point of about €8,400/year, with state-funded doctoral places available to some international candidates.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Do not assume Bangladesh is eligible for every Lithuanian state scholarship.

2026 state Master scholarships

The current 2026 full-time Master state scholarship country list does not include Bangladesh, so this should not be marketed as a Bangladesh-wide option.

University scholarships

Vilnius University, VILNIUS TECH and other institutions offer tuition waivers or merit scholarships open to wider international applicant groups.

VILNIUS TECH example

For 2026/27, VILNIUS TECH offered tuition-free and partial scholarships for high-achieving international Bachelor applicants.

MIGRIS TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMIT

Full-time degree students use a residence permit, not the ordinary Lithuanian D student visa.

01

Admission & recognition

Secure admission to an approved full-time Lithuanian higher-education programme and complete recognition requirements.

02

University mediation letter

The Lithuanian institution submits the electronic mediation/invitation information needed for the student residence-permit case.

03

Create MIGRIS application

Complete the temporary residence permit application through the Lithuanian Migration Information System.

04

Finance & insurance

Prepare sufficient means, return funds and valid health-insurance evidence for the requested permit period.

05

External-service appointment

Book the permitted VFS/external-service visit through MIGRIS after the system allows booking.

06

Bangladesh / India route

Lithuania’s Embassy in New Delhi is accredited to Bangladesh. Bangladesh has no Lithuanian residence-permit centre of its own; study TRP applicants can use the permitted external-service network, with India among the study-eligible routes under current rules.

07

Migration Department decision

The Lithuanian Migration Department — not the university — decides whether the residence permit is issued.

08

Receive card & travel

Once approved and produced, the residence card can be delivered through the external service process, allowing the student to enter Lithuania with the valid residence permit.

RESIDENCE PERMIT COST

Students receive a reduced state fee.

Study TRP · general procedure

€80 for a foreigner admitted to a Lithuanian research/study institution under a study or doctoral programme.

External-service charges

VFS/external-service and card-delivery charges are separate from the Lithuanian state fee.

Graduate permit

The Migration Department states that one general-procedure temporary residence permit after completing Lithuanian studies can be issued without the normal state fee.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Recognition and immigration documents are different files.

SKVC secondary recognition

Higher Secondary Certificate, HSC academic transcript, Secondary School Certificate and SSC academic transcript.

SKVC higher-education recognition

Bachelor diploma plus diploma supplement/transcript, together with the general identification and document-form requirements.

University admission

Recognised qualification, academic records, English evidence, motivation/CV/references where required and programme-specific documents.

Residence permit

Passport, admission/mediation information, sufficient means, return funds, insurance and other MIGRIS attachments.

Police / legalisation

If a police clearance or legalised document is required, follow current Migration Department and institution instructions. The Lithuanian Embassy in India notes that student PCC legalisation is coordinated through the Lithuanian educational institution and Lithuanian MFA.

SPOUSE & FAMILY · 2026 RULES

Master and doctoral students now have stronger immediate family rights than early-stage Bachelor students.

Master / equivalent / resident / PhD

From the 2026 changes, second-cycle students, residents and doctoral students can bring qualifying family members immediately under the applicable family-reunification rules.

Bachelor students

Other foreign residents generally face the ordinary family-reunification condition of having lived in Lithuania for two years, holding a permit valid for at least another year and having reasonable prospects of permanent residence, unless another exception applies.

Graduate job-search holders

Foreigners who completed studies in Lithuania and are applying for or already hold the graduate job-search permit are also included among those who can bring family immediately under the 2026 changes.

WORK WHILE STUDYING · 2026 CHANGE

Your study year now determines the ordinary weekly work limit.

Bachelor year 1–2

For permits issued under the rules effective from 22 May 2026, first and second year Bachelor students can work up to 20 hours per week.

Bachelor year 3–4

Third and fourth year foreign Bachelor students can work up to 40 hours per week under the current rules.

Master / PhD / residency

Master students, doctoral candidates and residents can work up to 40 hours per week.

Holidays and practice

The 20-hour restriction for first/second year Bachelor students does not include holidays or practice carried out as part of the studies.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Valid health coverage is part of the residence-permit setup.

Before arrival

Non-EU students need health-insurance coverage acceptable for the residence-permit period unless they are covered through another legally recognised basis.

After employment

Students who become employed can enter Lithuania’s compulsory health-insurance/social-security system through employment under the applicable rules.

WORK & LONG-TERM STAY

The graduate permit is a bridge into a proper work residence basis.

Employment residence

Graduates can move into the relevant Lithuanian employment residence category once they have a qualifying job and meet the current legal conditions.

EU Blue Card

Highly qualified graduates can use Lithuania’s EU Blue Card route when salary, qualification and job conditions are met.

Graduate advantage

2026 labour-migration quota rules include important exemptions for foreigners who completed studies in Lithuania and move into work within five years of graduation.

Permanent residence

Long-term/permanent status is a separate legal route based on qualifying residence history and other requirements. A Lithuanian degree does not automatically guarantee it.

CITIES

Lithuania’s main study hubs are compact and comparatively affordable.

Vilnius

Largest international university and employer market, especially for technology, fintech, business and shared services.

Kaunas

Major student city with KTU, VMU and LSMU, plus engineering, health and manufacturing employers.

Klaipėda

Port city with maritime, logistics, engineering and coastal-living advantages.

Šiauliai / Panevėžys

Smaller-city study options can reduce housing costs but have narrower English-speaking job markets.

ARRIVAL

Your residence card simplifies the entry stage, but local setup still matters.

Before departure

Passport, temporary residence permit card/decision, admission, insurance, finances and accommodation.

University registration

Complete final enrolment, student account, orientation and any missing recognition paperwork.

Declare residence

Complete the local residence declaration and personal-code/banking/phone setup required for your situation.

Working

Know whether your permit falls under the 20-hour first/second year Bachelor rule or the 40-hour higher-level rule before accepting a job.
