Payment & Enrollment Terms & Conditions

1. About LizOn Education

These terms govern payments, admission, course participation, educational support, and associated services provided by LizOn Education in Bangladesh. The course quotation, published package description, enrollment confirmation and any expressly agreed written arrangements form part of the student’s enrollment agreement.

2. Read before making payment

Please carefully review the course, package, number of classes, delivery mode, instructor arrangements, fees, schedule, validity, required equipment and policies before sending money. Contact the office if any detail is unclear. Making a payment and requesting enrollment signifies acknowledgment of the disclosed terms, subject to applicable law.

3. Payment details and recipient verification

Only use the payment details shown on this page or directly verified through LizOn Education’s official channels. Check the recipient, account type, number and amount in the payment application before authorizing a transfer. For a merchant account select Make Payment; for designated personal wallets select Send Money. Different transaction types may behave differently or attract provider charges.

4. Wrong number and mistaken transfers

Students must check all payment information before confirming a transfer. LizOn cannot automatically receive or reverse funds sent to an unrelated or incorrectly entered account. Contact the payment provider immediately for assistance and notify LizOn. LizOn will reasonably cooperate where practical and legally required, but is not liable for a transfer it never receives solely because a payer entered an incorrect recipient, except where an error is attributable to LizOn or applicable law provides otherwise.

5. Amounts, charges and confirmation

Pay the fee agreed for the particular course or service. Any wallet, bank, transfer or service charge levied by a payment provider is separate unless specifically included in the offer. Sending money does not automatically mark enrollment confirmed. Share your name, course, amount and transaction ID or screenshot through official WhatsApp for manual review. Do not share PINs, passwords, verification codes or confidential banking credentials.

6. Receipts and payment evidence

Keep the original transfer confirmation, transaction reference and any written receipt. Screenshots and references are reviewed against actual funds received. Altered, inaccurate or duplicate payment claims may lead to suspension of enrollment while verified. A receipt may be issued after the payment has been reconciled.

7. Non-refundable course fees

Once a student purchases or enrolls in a course and the agreed service is made available, course fees are generally non-refundable for change of mind, lack of preparation, unused classes or personal scheduling conflicts. This rule does not exclude refunds, remedies or cancellation rights that must be provided under applicable Bangladesh law or expressly agreed written terms.

8. Missed classes, late arrival and absence

Students are responsible for attending confirmed classes on time. Unattended classes, late arrivals and missed sessions are not automatically refundable or replaceable. A make-up or rescheduled lesson may be offered only under the confirmed package’s scheduling rules and teacher availability. Students should inform the office as early as possible before a foreseeable absence.

9. Rescheduling, pauses and validity

Requests to reschedule, defer, transfer to another session or pause study are subject to reasonable advance notice, available slots, package validity and written approval. Pauses and transfers are not automatic and do not extend package validity unless confirmed in writing. LizOn will make reasonable adjustments when a provider-caused change affects a booked service.

10. Batch classes and one-to-one sessions

Batch enrollment reserves participation in a group learning schedule, while one-to-one enrollment is based on the agreed number and duration of individual classes. Delivery may be online or offline as purchased. Joining a course does not give unlimited classes, lifetime access, or unrestricted private coaching unless specifically stated in a written offer.

11. Teacher and timetable changes

LizOn may reasonably reassign instructors or adjust venues, routine, learning materials or teaching format where educational or operational requirements justify it. Students will be informed of material changes where reasonably possible. If LizOn cannot provide a material part of the purchased service, appropriate remedies will be considered in accordance with the agreement and applicable law.

12. Learning outcomes and score disclaimer

IELTS, PTE, English proficiency and other educational outcomes depend on starting ability, attendance, independent practice, personal circumstances and examination performance. LizOn does not guarantee a specific score, pass, admission or result unless an explicit written guarantee defines the conditions and remedy. Promotional aspirations or target scores are not unconditional promises.

13. Examination and third-party charges

External examination registrations, test booking fees, university applications, immigration charges, courier fees and third-party services are separate unless the package explicitly includes them. Third-party pricing and rules can change. Students are responsible for checking spelling, identity documents, dates, required identification and eligibility before registration.

14. Study abroad information

Counseling and study abroad support do not guarantee a university offer, scholarship, visa or immigration outcome. Institutions and authorities make independent decisions. Students must supply genuine information, accurate documents and required funds. False, incomplete or misleading materials may cause refusal or termination of assistance.

15. Student responsibility and respectful conduct

Students must participate respectfully, follow reasonable classroom rules and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, disruption, academic dishonesty or misuse of other students’ information. Serious misconduct may lead to disciplinary action, with a fair review of the circumstances and without overriding applicable consumer rights.
