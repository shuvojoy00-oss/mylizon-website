THAILAND · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Thailand
from Bangladesh.

Thailand offers a wide range of English-taught international degrees, relatively moderate living costs, strong regional universities, postgraduate scholarships and a dedicated Non-ED Plus route for Bachelor level and above. For Bangladeshi students, the strongest fit is usually international programs in Bangkok and Pathum Thani, research-led postgraduate study, or scholarship-backed Master and PhD pathways.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Thailand accepts international students across Bachelor, Master and PhD levels.

There is no single national academic score. The university and program decide equivalency, subject prerequisites and English requirements.

Bachelor

HSC or equivalent

International programs normally require completion of upper-secondary education plus program-specific English, mathematics, SAT, portfolio or entrance-test criteria.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Applicants normally need a relevant undergraduate degree, transcript, English evidence and any faculty-specific GPA, work experience or statement requirements.

PhD

Research-ready profile

Doctoral admission normally requires a relevant graduate qualification, strong academic record, research fit and English evidence.

Bangladesh documents

English translations matter

Prepare passport, certificates, transcripts and certified English translations where originals are not already in English.

ADMISSION

Applications are university-led rather than handled through one national portal.

Each university publishes its own international program requirements and intake calendar.

01

Choose the program

Confirm language, academic prerequisites, tuition and intake.

02

Check equivalency and tests

Some programs require SAT, mathematics tests, portfolios or interviews in addition to English.

03

Submit international application

Upload academic records, passport, English score and supporting documents.

04

Scholarship review

Apply separately where required or indicate scholarship interest through the university system.

05

Accept offer

Complete deposits or confirmation steps and obtain the university visa support documents.

06

Apply for Non-ED / Non-ED Plus

Use the Royal Thai Embassy or e-Visa route applicable to Bangladesh and your degree level.

INTAKES

August is the broadest intake, with January available in many postgraduate routes.

Thailand does not use one national international-student calendar, so deadlines vary substantially.

MAIN INTAKE

August

Broadest availability across international undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

NEXT PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

AIT January 2027

AIT lists 20 December as the deadline for overseas applicants requiring an educational visa for the January semester. January 2027 orientation is scheduled for 5 January.

Chulalongkorn and MUIC use program-specific rounds. For 2027 entry, shortlist programs early and work backward from each faculty deadline rather than relying on one national intake date.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Thailand has both national and university scholarships for international students.

Funding is especially active at Master and PhD level.

Thailand Scholarships 2026

174+ awards

MHESI announced at least 174 scholarship awards across 211 programs for international students of all nationalities, mainly Master, Doctoral, exchange and training routes.

AIT Scholarships

Full or partial tuition support

Available competitively to all nationalities for Master and Doctoral study. Students still cover uncovered tuition, registration and living expenses.

Bangladesh-specific AIT option

GWSC scholarship

The 2026 Global Water and Sanitation Center Master scholarship explicitly included Bangladesh among eligible nationalities.

Chulalongkorn

ASEAN and Non-ASEAN funding

Chula lists undergraduate program scholarships plus graduate funding including the Graduate Scholarship Program for ASEAN and Non-ASEAN Countries.

STUDENT VISA + RESIDENCE

Bangladeshi degree students should plan around the Thai educational visa process.

The Royal Thai Embassy in Dhaka now lists Non-ED Plus information, and Thailand introduced e-Visa service in Bangladesh from 2025.

01

Receive university admission

Obtain the official acceptance and visa-support documents.

02

Use the Bangladesh e-Visa route

Follow the Royal Thai Embassy in Dhaka’s current e-Visa instructions and upload the required documents.

03

Apply under the correct education category

Bachelor degree level and above fall within the Non-ED Plus framework introduced in July 2024.

04

Enter Thailand

Carry admission and university documents and follow the institution’s reporting instructions.

05

University handles stay extension

Under Non-ED Plus, universities or institutions submit extension-of-stay applications on behalf of eligible students.

06

Maintain active student status

Keep enrolment, immigration reporting and university conditions valid throughout study.

Royal Thai Embassy Dhaka · Visa information ↗
FAMILY

Family options are limited and depend on the dependant relationship and student circumstances.

Thailand’s official student-visa guidance specifically notes a Non-Immigrant O follow route for parent or parents of an international student under 20 studying a long-term course, subject to Immigration Office discretion.

Parents of under-20 student

Possible Non-O follow route

This is discretionary and applies to long-term study rather than short courses.

Spouse / children of adult student

Verify individually

Do not assume a university student visa automatically grants dependant residence. Confirm with the university and Thai immigration before planning family relocation.

Thailand Government · Student visa guidance ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Do not budget on unrestricted part-time work.

Thailand’s Ministry of Labour requires foreign workers to be legally authorized and to work only within the permission granted. Non-ED Plus itself is not published as an open part-time work authorization.

Student status

No blanket open-work right

Being enrolled at a Thai university does not by itself mean you may freely take any paid job.

Work authorization

Thai labour rules still apply

Foreign workers need the legally required work permission and must comply with employer, occupation and visa conditions.

Planning advice

Fund study without job income

For Bangladesh applicants, treat student employment as optional only after the university and labour authorities confirm the legal route.

Thailand Ministry of Labour ↗
AFTER GRADUATION

Non-ED Plus gives eligible graduates one year in Thailand after completing the degree.

The extension is designed for job seeking, travel or other activities and allows a graduate who becomes employed to apply for a new visa type from inside Thailand.

Graduate extension

1 year

Available to eligible foreign graduates from Bachelor level or higher under the Non-ED Plus framework.

When employed

Change to the correct work status

The graduate pathway does not remove work-permit requirements. Ordinary employment requires the appropriate employment visa and work authorization.

This is materially better than leaving immediately after graduation, but it is not permanent residence and it is not an unrestricted open work permit.
Thai MFA · Non-ED Plus ↗
WHERE TO STUDY

Thailand’s student experience changes significantly by location.

The best city depends on university, budget and industry exposure.

Bangkok

Largest international education market

Chulalongkorn, Mahidol-linked programs, Thammasat facilities and the widest employer network.

Pathum Thani

AIT and major university corridor

Strong for postgraduate research, engineering, sustainability and policy with generally lower housing pressure than central Bangkok.

Nakhon Pathom

Mahidol Salaya ecosystem

Large international-campus environment west of Bangkok with student-focused facilities.

Chiang Mai

Northern university city

Lower-cost lifestyle and strong research, health, science and regional-development environment.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Complete university and immigration steps immediately.

Your institution is the main source for stay-extension and reporting documents.

01

University registration

Present original academic documents and complete enrolment.

02

Confirm immigration reporting

Follow the university’s instructions for stay extension and required reporting.

03

Set up accommodation

Campus housing can materially reduce monthly costs.

04

Open local services

Arrange mobile, banking, insurance and transport after registration.

05

Check work legality first

Do not accept paid employment until the correct authorization is confirmed.

AIT’s current arrival guidance estimates airport taxi costs around THB 800–1,000 from Suvarnabhumi to AIT and THB 300–500 from Don Mueang.
