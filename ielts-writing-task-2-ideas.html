Practical method

  1. Identify the exact question type and every instruction.
  2. Choose a clear response that addresses every part of the prompt.
  3. Brainstorm two relevant ideas and develop each with a reason and example.
  4. Leave time to check coherence, vocabulary accuracy and grammatical clarity.

How to practise effectively

Try the task with several different examples. Record your answers or working process, review mistakes, and compare your performance over multiple attempts. One shortcut or template cannot guarantee a test score.
