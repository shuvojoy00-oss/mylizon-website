BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Can your Bangladesh qualification lead to New Zealand study?

New Zealand admission is more directly provider and qualification based. HSC can be accepted by several universities, while postgraduate admission depends on degree equivalence, subject fit and programme rules.

HSC

Direct Bachelor is possible at several universities.

AUT, Waikato and Otago currently publish Bangladesh HSC entry at GPA 4.0/5.0 for general undergraduate admission, subject to programme requirements.

Alim

Some universities publish Alim equivalency.

Waikato and AUT currently note HSC or Alim with GPA 4.0/5.0, while individual providers may determine equivalency differently.

Bachelor

Masters entry is provider and programme specific.

Recognition of the degree, grade average, prerequisites, relevant subject background and sometimes work experience can matter.

Low CGPA

Pathways can matter more than one cut-off.

Graduate certificates, postgraduate certificates, diplomas or a different provider may be more realistic than forcing direct Masters entry.

Study Gap

Explain the gap through genuine intentions.

Employment, business, family circumstances and why this study makes sense now should be supported by evidence.

Research

Research Masters and PhD are a separate route.

Proposal, supervisor fit, prior research and academic evidence become more important. PhD tuition and visa conditions also differ from taught study.

Examples are not national guarantees. Otago currently publishes HSC GPA 4.0/5.0 for Bangladesh; AUT and Waikato publish the same general benchmark. University of Auckland can assess overseas secondary qualifications through its own programme-specific framework and prior tertiary/foundation routes.

NZQCF LEVELS

In New Zealand, qualification level changes the immigration outcome.

Students need to understand the level before choosing the course because partner-work rights and post-study options can depend on it.

Level 5–6Certificates / DiplomasPost-study eligibility only for specified qualifications; new 6-month graduate visa may apply from 16 Nov 2026 if PSWV is unavailable.
Level 7Bachelor / Graduate DiplomaDegree-level 7 can support standard PSWV rules. Graduate Diploma rules expand from 16 Nov 2026.
Level 8PGCert / PGDip / HonoursCan support PSWV if study-duration rules are met. Partner-work eligibility depends on the specific qualification unless other criteria apply.
Level 9Master’sStrong post-study position. Eligible study can support partner work and up to 3-year PSWV.
Level 10DoctoralDoctoral study has distinctive tuition, work, family and residence implications.

Code of Practice matters

A Fee Paying Student Visa offer must come from an approved provider, and the provider must meet New Zealand’s international learner pastoral-care requirements.

IELTS · PTE · ENGLISH

New Zealand does not use one universal Student visa English score.

The education provider sets course admission English requirements. Immigration New Zealand says English-test evidence is not mandatory for every Fee Paying Student Visa, but it can help show that your study intention and academic capability are genuine.

Typical university pattern

Many Bachelor programmes use around IELTS 6.0 with minimum components, while many postgraduate programmes use around 6.5. Specific professional courses can require more.

Visa assessment

INZ can consider English evidence alongside the provider’s assessment of your ability to pass the course. Do not confuse course entry rules with a single immigration minimum.

BANGLADESH FINANCIAL EVIDENCE

South Asia sponsorship has a special rule.

For citizens of Bangladesh,only immediate family can sponsor the student or provide a financial undertaking.

Who counts as immediate family?

Partner, parents, siblings, grandparents and parents-in-law under the current INZ South Asia rule.

Common accepted evidence

Bank statements, scholarships, education loans, fixed-term deposits, sponsor sharing ID and Financial Undertaking for a Student where applicable.

Bangladesh-specific caution

New Zealand’s Funds Transfer Scheme currently lists several South Asian/Asian nationalities but not Bangladesh. Do not assume an India-style FTS route applies.

LIVING COSTS & ACCOMMODATION

Auckland is not Dunedin. City choice changes the real budget.

Auckland

Large job and provider market, but generally one of the higher-cost housing environments. Official university planning estimates can reach NZD 20k–27k/year.

Wellington

Capital-city environment with public-sector and professional strengths. University estimates can also reach NZD 20k–27k/year.

Christchurch

Major South Island city with university and applied-study options; University of Canterbury uses around NZD 20k as a planning figure.

Dunedin

Traditional student city. Otago currently suggests roughly NZD 18k–21k for annual living costs.

Hamilton / Palmerston North

Smaller-city options can reduce some housing and transport costs, though exact rent depends on suburb and housing type.

Accommodation types

Halls of residence, homestay/private board and flatting are the main student options. Shared accommodation can start lower than managed residences.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Use provider scholarships as real numbers, not marketing headlines.

Study with New Zealand provides an official scholarship search by nationality, while universities publish their own international awards.

University scholarships

Many providers offer international tuition scholarships based on merit, country, subject, leadership or other criteria.

Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships

Government-funded scholarships are available for eligible countries/programmes and have their own rules and obligations.

Research funding

PhD and research applicants should compare university doctoral scholarships, stipends and supervisor/project funding separately.

FEE PAYING STUDENT VISA

From offer to visa decision — New Zealand’s actual flow.

01

Choose an approved provider & course

Verify provider status and programme suitability. The provider must be approved and meet international learner Code requirements.

02

Offer or conditional offer

Offshore applicants can apply with a conditional offer. INZ may approve the visa in principle, but an unconditional offer is needed before final approval.

03

Tuition & financial plan

Show tuition is paid or fundable, living funds are genuine and accessible, and outward travel is covered.

04

Genuine intentions & documents

Explain why this course/provider makes sense, your previous study/work, finances and future plans.

05

Submit Student visa

The Fee Paying Student Visa currently costs from NZD 850 and is normally granted for the paid study period, up to 4 years.

06

Police / health evidence if required

Required police certificates must now be included upfront. Medical/chest X-ray requirements depend on stay length and health/TB criteria.

07

Insurance

You must agree to hold acceptable medical and travel insurance for the required period, subject to limited exceptions such as PhD students.

08

Decision & travel

INZ currently shows 80% of Fee Paying Student Visa applications within 8 weeks and recommends applying at least 3 months before travel.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH APPLICANTS

New Zealand’s document logic is different from Australia’s.

Academic & admission

Passport, photo, offer of place, academic certificates/transcripts, English evidence if required by the provider, CV, SOP/provider statement where requested, course-specific portfolio/research material.

Financial & sponsor

Tuition evidence, recent bank statements, fixed deposits, education loan documentation, scholarship evidence, sponsor sharing ID, Financial Undertaking for a Student where applicable, income/source-of-funds evidence and a credible plan for later years.

Employment & genuine intentions

Job certificate, experience letter, salary slips, salary bank statements, business documents, tax evidence and other records that explain your career path and why the new study is logical.

Police clearance

If required, it must be uploaded upfront. Generally, applicants aged 17+ whose total intended time in New Zealand is 24 months or more need police certificates from citizenship countries and countries where they have spent over 5 years since age 17. Certificates must generally be less than 6 months old.

Medical / chest X-ray

A chest X-ray can be required for study of 6 months or more if the applicant is from, or has spent relevant time in, a country that is not low-TB-incidence. Full medical rules depend on visa type and length of stay.

Family / relationship evidence

Marriage/partnership evidence, proof of living together, financial/social evidence and child relationship documents are relevant where partner or child visas are being considered.

PARTNER & CHILDREN

In New Zealand, the student’s qualification can determine the partner’s work rights.

Any qualification

Partner visitor visa may be possible.

A student can support a visitor visa for a partner and children, subject to the relevant criteria.

Eligible Level 7 / 8

Partner work visa only for qualifying study.

Eligible Level 7 or 8 study must connect to the specified Green List / PSWV qualification rules.

Level 9 / 10

Master’s and doctoral study can support partner work.

The partner work visa currently costs from NZD 1,630, generally matches the student visa duration and requires at least NZD 4,200 for living expenses.

Children

Dependent child visas can be possible.

Eligibility depends on the parent’s visa pathway. A dependent child student visa can allow school study and may be treated as domestic in qualifying cases.

WORK & LIFE

Student work rights increased to 25 hours a week.

25 hours / week

Eligible students can work up to 25 hours a week while studying, depending on visa conditions.

Full-time in scheduled holidays

Eligible tertiary students can have full-time work rights during qualifying scheduled breaks when course/visa conditions allow.

Practical work is separate

Required practical work experience that forms part of the course can be additional to ordinary work-hour limits.

EARNINGS CALCULATOR

Use the legal minimum as a floor, not a promise.

From 1 April 2026, New Zealand’s adult minimum wage is NZD 23.95/hour before tax. Actual student jobs may pay more or use different starting/training rates.

IRD · TAX

Working students need to understand New Zealand tax basics.

IRD number

An IRD number is the tax identifier generally needed when earning income in New Zealand so the correct tax rate can be applied.

Tax residency

Tax residency is separate from immigration status. New Zealand tax residents are generally taxed on worldwide income, subject to applicable exemptions and rules.

FROM GRADUATE WORK TO EMPLOYMENT

AEWV can be the bridge after study — if you secure the right job.

Accredited Employer Work Visa

Requires a full-time job offer from an accredited employer, normally at least 30 hours per week, plus job/skill requirements. Current cost starts from NZD 1,540.

Why the new 6-month visa matters

Immigration New Zealand explicitly describes it as time for eligible graduates to find work and, where appropriate, transition to an AEWV.

RESIDENCE REALITY

Do not market “PR courses.” Understand the actual pathways.

Green List Tier 1

Straight to Residence

For qualifying Tier 1 jobs where the applicant meets the specific registration, qualification and pay requirements.

Green List Tier 2

Work to Residence

For qualifying Tier 2 jobs after meeting the required New Zealand work and role conditions.

SMC

Skilled Migrant Category

The current points-based pathway requires 6 skilled resident points, built from qualification, occupational registration or income plus eligible New Zealand skilled work where needed.

SMC 6-POINT EXPLORER

New Zealand’s skilled residence points are not Australia-style points.

Qualification points are concentrated: for example, a qualifying New Zealand Master’s studied full-time for at least 30 weeks can produce 6 points, while a New Zealand Bachelor can produce 5.

NZ Level 10 Doctoral6 points
NZ Level 9 Master’s · 30 weeks full-time6 points
NZ Level 8 Honours / PGDip / PGCert5 points
NZ Level 7 Bachelor5 points
NZ skilled work experienceUp to 3 additional points
ARRIVAL

The plan should continue after the visa grant.

Before departure

Passport, visa, offer/enrolment, tuition evidence, insurance, accommodation, outward travel evidence, important documents and medication.

Arrival day

Border entry, baggage/biosecurity declarations, transport, SIM/eSIM, accommodation check-in and emergency contacts.

First week

Orientation, student ID, timetable, bank account, IRD number if working, transport, health/insurance details and provider support services.

First month

Understand work conditions, tenancy rights, academic expectations, attendance/progress requirements and local support services.
