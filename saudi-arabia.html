SAUDI ARABIA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Saudi Arabia
from Bangladesh.

Saudi Arabia combines major public universities, fully funded international scholarships, specialist science and technology institutions, and a national Study in Saudi platform with a dedicated educational visa. For Bangladeshi students, the strongest opportunities are often scholarship-led, while self-funded routes, programme language and university-specific admission rules must be checked carefully.

Saudi Arabia now has a dedicated educational visa route

WHY SAUDI ARABIA

The country is no longer only a scholarship destination.

Saudi Arabia is building a broader international education route around Study in Saudi, educational visas, research universities and expanding graduate and industry pathways.

Funding

Full scholarships remain a major advantage

Public universities can offer full, partial or paid scholarship routes. Some institutions waive tuition and include housing, stipends, healthcare and travel.

Research

Strong STEM and graduate options

KFUPM and KAUST are particularly strong for engineering, computing, energy, science and research-led graduate study.

Visa reform

Educational visa with student work features

The national educational visa explicitly includes part-time work during study and full-time work during holidays.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility depends on the university, degree level and funding route.

Bangladeshi students can apply from outside Saudi Arabia through Study in Saudi and through institution-specific international admissions where available.

Bachelor

HSC or equivalent

Applicants need an accepted secondary qualification, authenticated documents and any programme-specific academic or aptitude requirement. Some scholarship rules also use age limits.

Master

Recognised Bachelor degree

Graduate programmes normally require a relevant degree, transcripts, recommendations and programme-specific English or Arabic requirements.

PhD

Relevant graduate preparation

Research programmes can require a relevant Master degree, strong GPA, references, English scores, research fit and interviews.

Educational visa

Age 16+

The long-term educational visa FAQ requires age 16 or above, valid passport, admission from a licensed institution, financial proof or guarantee, medical report, insurance and proof of the study language.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Start with Study in Saudi, then follow the university’s own academic rules.

The Ministry of Education directs international applicants to the Study in Saudi platform. Individual universities still control programme criteria and admission decisions.

01

Choose degree and university

Compare programme language, eligibility, funding and campus location.

02

Create Study in Saudi profile

Use the national platform for international opportunities and application windows.

03

Prepare authenticated documents

Academic certificates and supporting records must follow the institution’s authentication rules.

04

Meet programme tests

English, SAT, aptitude tests, interviews or Arabic proficiency may apply depending on the programme.

05

Funding decision

Scholarship and self-funded routes may have different conditions and timelines.

06

Accept admission and start visa

After admission, follow the institution and educational visa instructions for travel.

UNIVERSITIES

Choose by academic strength, funding model and city.

Saudi Arabia has public comprehensive universities, specialist STEM institutions and research-intensive graduate universities.

King Saud University

Major Riyadh public university with external scholarships for international students and selected paid routes.

King Abdulaziz University

Large Jeddah university with undergraduate and postgraduate international admissions and fully funded external scholarships.

Official ↗

Dhahran-based science, engineering, computing and business university with dedicated international admission and scholarship pathways.

Graduate-only research university near Jeddah. All admitted students receive KAUST Fellowship support unless covered by another funding arrangement.

Islamic University of Madinah

Public university with international scholarship admissions through Study in Saudi, including Arabic, Islamic and selected scientific fields.

Official ↗

Imam Abdulrahman Bin Faisal University

Eastern Province public university with broad undergraduate and postgraduate study across sciences, engineering, business and other fields.

INTAKES

Fall is the main intake, but international windows are not standardized.

Application dates are controlled by Study in Saudi and individual universities. Do not assume one national deadline.

MAIN START

August / September

The broadest Bachelor, Master and PhD availability is tied to the main academic year.

CURRENT 2026 EXAMPLE

KAU · 16 Sep to 6 Oct 2026

King Abdulaziz University opened non-Saudi undergraduate and postgraduate applications through Study in Saudi for this period. That window has now closed.

For 2027 entry, start preparing documents now and monitor Study in Saudi plus each target university. Scholarship windows can open many months before enrolment.

Many programmes are in Arabic, while STEM and graduate programmes can be taught in English. Always check the programme, not only the university.

REALISTIC LIVING COST

Scholarship students and self-funded students can have completely different budgets.

University housing and subsidized campus services can make Saudi study far cheaper than private-city living. Do not use one national number for everyone.

Useful official cost anchors

KSU standard student housingSAR 1,320 / semester
KSU selected single roomsSAR 1,500–1,800 / semester
KSU international food initiativeSAR 150 / month
KAUST student survey averageUSD 1,320 / month
LizOn self-funded planning rangeSAR 2,500–5,000+ / month
The LizOn range is a planning estimate for a single self-funded student and is not a government minimum. Actual cost varies strongly by Riyadh, Jeddah, Dhahran, campus housing, transport and lifestyle. Fully funded scholarship students may have housing, healthcare, travel and stipends covered.

TUITION

Saudi Arabia is unusual because many strong international routes are scholarship-first.

Self-funded tuition exists, but public scholarship offers can reduce tuition to zero and add substantial living support.

KFUPM international Bachelor

Tuition waived

KFUPM says international undergraduates living outside Saudi Arabia receive a full scholarship, with possible housing, books, stipend and medical services.

KAUST graduate study

Full tuition support

The KAUST Fellowship covers tuition and bench fees valued up to USD 35,000 annually plus stipend, housing, insurance and relocation support.

Other universities

Programme specific

Paid programmes and partial scholarships also exist. Use the exact Study in Saudi listing and university offer before budgeting.

KFUPM tuition and scholarships ↗
SCHOLARSHIPS

Saudi scholarships can cover far more than tuition.

The Ministry classifies public-university international scholarships as full, partial or paid. Benefits vary by institution and offer.

Public university full scholarship

Tuition + living support

Official Ministry guidance lists possible healthcare, arrival allowance, housing and annual travel tickets for external scholarship students.

King Abdulaziz University

Fully funded international scholarships

KAU lists monthly stipend, arrival allowance, healthcare, housing subject to availability, subsidized meals and annual round-trip airfare.

KFUPM

Full international undergraduate scholarship

International undergraduates living outside Saudi Arabia have tuition waived, with additional scholarship support.

KAUST

Fellowship for admitted graduate students

Full tuition, stipend, base accommodation, health insurance and relocation support are core benefits.

Saudi Ministry · Public university scholarships ↗
EDUCATIONAL VISA + RESIDENCE

Degree students use the long-term educational visa route.

The official platform says it is suitable for Bachelor, Master and PhD study lasting more than one year and does not require a sponsor.

01

Get admission

Admission must come from a licensed and accredited Saudi educational institution.

02

Check passport

Passport validity should be at least six months from the planned entry date.

03

Financial proof

Show ability to cover tuition and living costs or provide an acceptable financial guarantee or scholarship.

04

Medical + insurance

Provide a medical report on communicable diseases and medical insurance for the student and accompanying dependants.

05

Language evidence

Prove ability in the language of study unless entering an Arabic-language study route covered by the exemption.

06

Travel and complete university formalities

Follow the institution’s arrival, identity, residence and registration instructions.

Educational visa requirements ↗
SPOUSE · CHILDREN

The educational visa is unusually family-friendly on paper.

Study in Saudi lists the ability to bring dependants as a benefit of the educational visa. It also states that the student’s family can enter on visitor visas and that family members may work.

Dependants

Permitted under the educational visa framework

Applicants with accompanying dependants should include insurance and any documents required by the university and immigration process.

Budget reality

Scholarship family coverage varies

Do not assume a student scholarship automatically pays dependant housing, insurance or living costs. KAUST, for example, notes that charges may apply to dependant housing and coverage.

Study in Saudi · Visa benefits ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

The educational visa explicitly includes student work rights.

The national platform says educational-visa students may work part time during study and full time during holidays.

During study

Part-time work allowed

The official educational visa page confirms the right in principle but does not publish a universal weekly-hour cap on that page.

Holidays

Full-time work allowed

The visa benefit specifically distinguishes full-time work during academic holidays.

Important

Use approved legal employment

Saudi labour rules generally require non-Saudis to be legally authorized for work. Follow the educational-visa and employer procedures rather than assuming unrestricted employment.

Saudi HRSD · Employment of non-Saudis ↗
AFTER GRADUATION

The educational visa lists up to two years of practical training after graduation.

This is a meaningful post-study feature, but it should not be described as an automatic open work visa or permanent residence route.

Practical training

Up to 2 years

The official educational visa benefits include practical training for the student for two years after graduation.

Long-term employment

Separate work authorization applies

For ordinary employment beyond the study or training framework, non-Saudi workers must meet Saudi work authorization and employer requirements.

Treat this as a graduate practical-training opportunity, not as a guaranteed settlement pathway. The exact transition from educational status to employment should be verified at graduation because labour and immigration rules can change.
Educational visa post-study benefit ↗
WHERE TO STUDY

Your city changes both the academic ecosystem and daily budget.

Saudi Arabia is geographically large, so university location matters more than it does in compact Gulf destinations.

Riyadh

Government, business and technology

Home to King Saud University and a large concentration of employers, government institutions and private-sector headquarters.

Jeddah

Commercial and coastal hub

Home to King Abdulaziz University, with strong business, logistics, health and services exposure.

Dhahran

Energy and engineering

KFUPM sits in the Eastern Province near the centre of Saudi Arabia’s energy and industrial ecosystem.

Thuwal

Research campus

KAUST is a self-contained graduate research community on the Red Sea, north of Jeddah.

Madinah

Islamic and Arabic study

The Islamic University of Madinah attracts international scholarship students alongside selected scientific study.

Dammam / Eastern Province

Industry and health ecosystem

Imam Abdulrahman Bin Faisal University connects students to the Eastern Province’s health, engineering and business sectors.

AFTER ARRIVAL

University onboarding comes before everything else.

Scholarship and self-funded students may have very different arrival support, so follow the exact institution instructions.

01

University check-in

Present originals and complete admission verification.

02

Complete residence and identity steps

Follow the educational visa and university process for your legal student status.

03

Move into housing

Scholarship housing can be assigned directly, while self-funded students may need private accommodation.

04

Activate banking, mobile and health services

Use university international-student support where available.

05

Confirm work rules before taking a job

Use only legal work permitted by your visa and institutional rules.
