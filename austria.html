Do not confuse Austria’s D visa with the Residence Permit Student

Bangladeshi students planning to study in Austria for more than six months generally need the Residence Permit Student first. The initial residence-permit application must be submitted personally at the Austrian Embassy in New Delhi. A Visa D is then used for entry after the Austrian residence authority approves the permit.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Austria does not publish one national Bangladesh HSC or CGPA cut-off.

Admission is decided by the individual Austrian higher-education institution.

Bachelor

An equivalent secondary-school qualification is the baseline.

For non-EU applicants, Austrian universities generally require a secondary-school leaving certificate considered equivalent and sufficient for university entrance. The institution decides equivalency.

Home-country access

The certificate should support higher-education access.

Universities may examine whether your Bangladesh qualification gives access to the intended type of study and can prescribe supplementary examinations where substantial differences exist.

Masters

A relevant Bachelor of at least 180 ECTS-equivalent is normally required.

The university assesses degree level, subject relevance, credits, grades and language. There is no Austria-wide Bangladesh CGPA threshold.

Fachhochschule

UAS admission can be more practice-oriented.

Universities of applied sciences accept general university entrance qualifications or relevant professional qualifications and normally use their own entrance process.

Language

German dominates Bachelor study.

Most Austrian programmes are German-taught, while the number of English-taught programmes is growing, especially at Master level.

Entrance exams

Competitive programmes can close much earlier.

Medicine, dentistry, psychology, architecture, business, computer science and arts programmes can use entrance examinations or selection procedures.

UNIVERSITY VS FACHHOCHSCHULE

Austria has several distinct higher-education sectors.

Universität

Research universities

Academic and research-oriented Bachelor, Master, diploma and doctoral study.

Fachhochschule

Universities of Applied Sciences

Professionally oriented programmes with structured curricula, industry links and compulsory entrance procedures.

Private

Private universities and higher-education institutions

Institution-specific tuition and admissions, often with more specialised or English-taught programmes.

Arts

Universities of the arts

Music, performing arts, fine arts and applied arts with portfolio/audition-heavy admission.

GERMAN · IELTS · ENGLISH

German is still the main language of Austrian higher education.

German-taught programmes

Institutions accept recognised German certificates and decide the exact level. Many degree programmes expect B2 or C1 before full admission.

English-taught programmes

Around 580 programmes are currently listed as English-taught across Austrian higher education. IELTS/TOEFL requirements are programme-specific.

Preparatory study

Conditional admission and university preparation programmes can be relevant where supplementary examinations or additional German study are prescribed.

TUITION · 2026

Public-university tuition is predictable for most third-country students.

Public universities

Regular degree students from third countries with a student residence permit generally pay €726.72 per semester from the first semester.

ÖH contribution

All students pay the compulsory Austrian Students’ Union contribution. The current amount is €26.20 per semester.

Fachhochschulen

Universities of applied sciences may charge their own tuition. Check the exact programme.

Private universities

Private tuition varies substantially and can be many thousands of euros per semester or year.

Official tuition guidance ↗
SCHOLARSHIPS

Austria has a searchable funding system rather than one universal Bangladesh scholarship.

grants.at

Austria’s official scholarship database lets students filter grants by nationality, degree level and field.

OeAD programmes

OeAD administers government-funded and mobility scholarships, many of which target Master, doctoral, research or exchange applicants.

Institutional scholarships

Universities and FH institutions can offer separate tuition support or merit funding. Eligibility varies by programme.

RESIDENCE PERMIT STUDENT · BANGLADESH

For study over six months, the residence application comes before the entry visa.

01

Admission

Obtain admission or conditional admission from the Austrian institution.

02

Prepare finance

Prove the age-based funds for 12 months, plus any extra housing and insurance amount.

03

Accommodation

Show a legal claim to suitable accommodation, such as a rental or student-housing agreement.

04

Health insurance

Provide insurance covering all risks in Austria for the residence-permit process.

05

Apply in New Delhi

Bangladesh falls under the Austrian Embassy New Delhi. Initial residence-permit applications for stays over six months are submitted there personally.

06

Austrian authority decides

The Embassy forwards the application to the competent residence authority in Austria. Applicants normally wait abroad for the decision.

07

Visa D for entry

After residence approval, apply for the Visa D needed to enter Austria and collect the residence card. Austria’s Bangladesh VFS service handles D-visa submissions.

08

Collect residence card

Travel to Austria, register your address and collect the Residence Permit Student from the competent Austrian authority.

RESIDENCE & VISA COSTS · 2026

The residence-permit fee changed on 1 January 2026.

Residence Permit Student

€218 for applications submitted from 1 January 2026. The full application fee is paid at submission and is not refunded if the application is refused or withdrawn.

Visa D

Visa D is a separate entry step after residence approval for long-term students. The Bangladesh VFS fee schedule and local BDT equivalent should be checked at the time of submission.

Additional costs

Translations, legalisation, police certificates, insurance, VFS/service fees and document handling can add to the official permit fee.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Austria expects originals, legalisation where required and consistent academic/residence evidence.

Academic admission

SSC/HSC or previous degree certificates and transcripts, passport, language evidence and programme-specific documents. The university can request supplementary evidence or entrance examinations.

Residence permit

Passport, biometric photo, admission, financial proof, accommodation, all-risk health insurance and other documents requested by the New Delhi Embassy/Austrian residence authority.

Funds

The applicant’s account should normally be accessible from Austria and the authority can ask for proof of origin, including recent bank history, sponsor bank statements, employment, scholarship, loan or sale/inheritance documentation.

Police clearance

A recent police clearance, generally not older than three months at application, can be required for first residence applications.

Legalisation / translation

The New Delhi Embassy states that required documents must be legalised and documents not in German need certified German translations for the residence process.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Student family reunification is possible through a separate Family Community permit.

Eligible family

Spouse, registered partner and unmarried minor children can qualify. Spouses/registered partners must generally be at least 21 when applying.

Family finances

For 2026, couples need €2,064.12/month together, plus €201.88/month for each child, with extra housing costs above the threshold added.

Work is not automatic

A family member who wants salaried employment needs the appropriate labour-market authorisation. The relevant permit is employer-specific and may involve an AMS labour-market check.

Austria temporary residence / family rules ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

Students can work up to 20 hours/week, but the employer needs the permit.

20 hours/week

Third-country students can receive an employment permit for up to 20 hours per week without a labour-market test.

Employer applies to AMS

The prospective employer, not the student alone, applies for the Beschäftigungsbewilligung through Austria’s Public Employment Service.

Study remains primary

Employment must not jeopardise the main residence purpose: successful study.

Study progress matters at renewal

Residence-permit extensions require proof of study success or academic progress.

Official student work rules ↗
HEALTH INSURANCE

All-risk health insurance is required for the residence permit.

Before / during residence application

You need coverage valid in Austria and covering all risks. A short travel-only policy may not satisfy the long-term requirement.

Student self-insurance

Eligible students can use the preferential ÖGK student self-insurance. The 2026 contribution is €78.84 per month.

AFTER STUDY

Austrian graduates can remain for 12 months to find qualified work or start a business.

Graduate extension

Length12 months
PurposeJob search / business
Work while searchingUp to 20 hrs / week with permit
Next routeRed-White-Red Card

No points test for Austrian graduates.

If you find employment matching your qualification level during the 12-month period and are paid the locally customary graduate salary, you can apply for a Red-White-Red Card without the ordinary points system or labour-market test.

Official graduate pathway ↗
RED WHITE RED CARD & LONG-TERM STAY

The graduate route can lead into Austria’s skilled-worker settlement system.

Red-White-Red Card for graduates

Qualifying Austrian graduates with a matching job offer can obtain the employer-specific Red-White-Red Card without the ordinary graduate points test.

Red-White-Red Card Plus

After qualifying employment, graduates may later move into Red-White-Red Card Plus with unrestricted labour-market access when the statutory conditions are met.

EU Blue Card

Highly qualified graduates can also use Austria’s EU Blue Card route where the job and salary requirements are met.

Permanent residence

Long-term residence is a separate legal pathway based on qualifying settlement history and integration requirements. A degree alone does not guarantee it.

CITIES

Vienna has the biggest market, but Austria’s regional university cities can reduce living costs.

Vienna

Largest concentration of universities, English-taught programmes and graduate employers, with higher housing costs.

Graz

Strong engineering, technology, research and student ecosystem with TU Graz, University of Graz and FH Joanneum.

Innsbruck

Research university city with tourism, life science and alpine-region strengths; housing can still be expensive.

Linz

Industrial, technology and digital-economy centre around Johannes Kepler University and applied institutions.

Salzburg

Humanities, music, tourism and selected science programmes in a smaller high-cost city.

Klagenfurt

Smaller university environment with technology, business and lower-scale city living.

ARRIVAL

Address registration is one of your first Austrian legal steps.

Before departure

Passport, Visa D, residence approval, admission, accommodation and insurance documents.

Within 3 working days

Register your Austrian address with the local registration office using the Meldezettel process.

Collect residence card

Collect the Residence Permit Student from the competent Austrian residence authority within the permitted timeframe.

University & work

Complete enrolment, ÖH contribution and insurance setup. Do not begin ordinary employment until the employer has the required AMS authorisation.
