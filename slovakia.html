SLOVAKIA · BANGLADESH STUDENT GUIDE

Study in Slovakia
from Bangladesh.

Slovakia offers tuition-free full-time study in Slovak at public universities, comparatively affordable living costs, growing English-taught options, legal student work and a 9 month graduate job-search route. Bangladeshi applicants should plan carefully around New Delhi because documents require legalization and superlegalisation before the residence process.

Slovakia in one view

Strong fit for students who want a lower-cost EU destination, especially if they are open to Slovak-language study or selected English programmes.

!

Bangladesh-issued documents need legalization and superlegalisation before the Slovak residence process

The Slovak Embassy in New Delhi states that documents from Bangladesh must first be legalised by the competent Bangladeshi authorities and then superlegalised by the Embassy before further copying or translation for the Slovak residence file.

ADMISSION

Admission is university-led, while foreign qualifications must be recognised in Slovakia.

International admission standards are generally the same as those applied to Slovak students, with programme-specific entrance exams, aptitude tests or subject requirements.

Bachelor

Completed secondary education

Bangladeshi applicants normally use SSC and HSC documents, subject to Slovak recognition and university requirements.

Master

Relevant Bachelor degree

A follow-up Master normally requires a recognised relevant Bachelor or equivalent qualification.

PhD

Second-cycle degree

Doctoral admission requires completion of a second-level degree and faculty-specific selection.

Application deadlines are set by each institution and are commonly around the end of February or March, with entrance examinations usually between June and September.

Slovakia has public, state and private higher education institutions.

Most international students compare Bratislava, Košice, Žilina, Nitra and Banská Bystrica depending on field and cost.

INTAKES

September is the main academic start.

Most programmes follow the standard Slovak academic year, while application and entrance dates vary by university and faculty.

MAIN INTAKE

September

Broadest Bachelor, Master and PhD programme choice.

SECONDARY

Programme-specific

Some institutions may offer other starts, but they should not be treated as a national standard.

Because Bangladesh applicants need document legalization and visa coordination through New Delhi, apply earlier than the final university deadline whenever possible.
SLOVAK · ENGLISH

Language choice has a major impact on tuition.

Full-time programmes taught in Slovak are generally tuition-free within the standard programme length, while foreign-language programmes set separate fees.

LIVING COST

Slovakia can be affordable, especially outside Bratislava.

Official Study in Slovakia examples show low dormitory and food costs compared with many Western European capitals.

Official cost examples

Dormitory bedfrom ~€60 / month
1 bedroom flatfrom ~€400 / month incl. utilities
2 bedroom flatfrom ~€550 / month incl. utilities
Student canteen lunchfrom ~€2
Local transport ticket~€1.00–1.40
Bratislava is generally more expensive than regional cities. Build a realistic annual budget instead of assuming the cheapest dormitory rate will always be available.

TUITION

Foreign-language tuition can range from zero to €13,000 per academic year.

Each university and faculty sets its own foreign-language fees. Slovak-language full-time study within standard length is generally tuition-free.

Slovak language

€0 tuition in standard full-time study

The strongest cost advantage for students able to study in Slovak.

Foreign-language programmes

€0–13,000 / year

Official national range across institutions and fields.

Medicine / premium programmes

Often upper end

English-taught medical and selected specialist programmes can be among the most expensive.

SCHOLARSHIPS FOR TALENTED STUDENTS

Bangladeshi applicants can compete in Slovakia’s worldwide talented-student scholarship scheme.

The 2026 to 27 programme is open to international students worldwide for selected full-time Bachelor and Master programmes at participating public or state universities.

Maximum supportUp to €15,000Across the eligible support period.
Annual support€5,000 / academic year€500 monthly for ten months.
BachelorUp to 3 yearsSelected supported programmes.
MasterUp to 2 yearsSelected supported programmes.
2026 requirement

SAT or SCIO threshold

The current scheme uses SAT 1100 minimum or an average SCIO percentile of 63 in Mathematics and General Academic Prerequisites.

Current status

2026 to 27 applications closed

The extended 2026 deadline was 8 September 2026. Future applicants should monitor the next cycle rather than treating the scheme as currently open.

NATIONAL D VISA + STUDY RESIDENCE

Bangladeshi students should plan the New Delhi process with their Slovak university.

For the current National D study route in New Delhi, the admitted Slovak higher education institution requests the appointment. Students then attend the visa submission and mandatory Embassy interview.

01

Secure admission

Receive official confirmation from the Slovak university.

02

Legalise Bangladesh documents

Complete Bangladesh legalization and Slovak Embassy superlegalisation before translation and residence use.

03

University requests appointment

The current New Delhi study system requires the Slovak university to submit the appointment request during the published registration periods.

04

Submit National D visa

Use the designated visa-centre process once your university-coordinated appointment is assigned.

05

Attend mandatory Embassy interview

The New Delhi process requires a personal interview after submission.

06

Travel to Slovakia

A national D visa can support entry and the subsequent temporary-residence filing.

07

Apply for temporary residence for study

Study residence can be granted for the period needed for study, up to the statutory maximum.

08

Keep study status active

The residence purpose is tied to active study and should be renewed before expiry.

The Slovak Embassy in New Delhi currently lists a €33 National D visa fee for the purpose of submitting a residence application, converted into INR at the monthly Embassy rate. Use the current Embassy instructions at submission time.

SPOUSE · CHILDREN

Family reunification exists, but family members use their own residence route.

Qualifying relatives can apply for temporary residence for family reunion, generally for the duration of the principal resident’s permit up to the statutory limit.

Separate residence

Family does not simply inherit student status

Relationship, funds, criminal-record documents and other residence requirements apply separately.

Family work rights

Often restricted during first 9 months

For most family-reunion holders linked to an ordinary study permit, a work permit may be required during the first nine months. After nine months of continuous family residence, free labour-market access can apply.

IOM Slovakia family reunion guidance ↗
WORK WHILE STUDYING

University students can work up to 80 hours per month without changing the purpose of residence.

The current official Study in Slovakia guidance and IOM residence guidance both use an 80-hour monthly limit for third-country university students.

University student

80 hours / month

You can work under an employment contract or certain agreements without a separate work permit within the student rule.

Weekly equivalent

Roughly 20 hours / week

Official law is expressed as 80 hours per month, so the page keeps that wording rather than pretending it is a strict weekly cap.

Business

Possible under study residence

IOM guidance states that a person with temporary residence for study may also do business without changing the residence purpose, subject to other legal requirements.

Study in Slovakia work rights ↗
HEALTH INSURANCE

Insurance treatment depends on your scholarship and residence situation.

Students should not assume they are automatically part of Slovak public health insurance. Some government-approved scholarship holders can qualify, while other international students need appropriate private or other valid coverage.

Scholarship holders

Some public-insurance eligibility

The talented-student scholarship page notes public health insurance can apply where the statutory conditions are met.

Other students

Verify before travel

Confirm the exact insurance requirement for visa, residence and university enrolment.

AFTER GRADUATION

Graduates can extend study residence for 9 months to seek employment or start a business.

The current 2026 IOM guidance applies this opportunity to successful graduates of Slovak universities, including Bachelor graduates.

Graduate extension

9 months

Renew the temporary study residence for job search or business preparation before your current residence is cancelled or expires.

Employment after graduation

Work-permit exemption

IOM states that graduates of Slovak universities can be employed without a work permit or Labour Office approval, while residence still needs to be changed or maintained under the appropriate basis.

The purpose of study residence formally ends when you graduate. IOM advises acting quickly because Foreign Police may start cancellation after 30 days if you do not renew or change status.

WHERE TO STUDY

City choice changes both cost and career access.

Bratislava has the largest employer market, while Košice, Žilina and other cities can offer lower accommodation costs.

Bratislava

Largest employer + university market

Comenius University, STU, economics and international companies, with the highest housing costs.

Košice

IT + engineering + medicine

Strong technical and medical institutions with lower housing pressure than Bratislava.

Žilina

Transport + engineering

Strong industry links and a smaller-city environment.

Nitra

Agriculture + regional study

Specialist universities and comparatively manageable living costs.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Complete the temporary residence process before the National D period ends.

Your university and the Foreign Police process become central after arrival.

01

Complete university enrolment

Activate full student status and submit original documents.

02

Obtain Slovak translations

New Delhi guidance notes that official Slovak translations can be completed in Slovakia before the residence application.

03

Apply at Foreign Police

Submit the temporary residence application within the lawful National D stay.

04

Residence card

Complete biometrics and card issuance as instructed.

05

Track renewal and graduation dates

Residence rights change when study ends, so prepare the next route early.
