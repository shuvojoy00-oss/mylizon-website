IELTS Reading True, False, Not Given: Complete Guide

Confused between FALSE and NOT GIVEN? This task asks whether a statement agrees with the information in the passage, contradicts it, or cannot be established. Learn how to prove each answer using the text rather than guessing from keywords.

বাংলায় বুঝুন

TRUE: প্রশ্নের বক্তব্য passage-এর তথ্যের সঙ্গে মিলে যায়। FALSE: passage-এ বিপরীত তথ্য আছে। NOT GIVEN: বক্তব্যটি সত্য না মিথ্যা, passage থেকে নিশ্চিত হওয়া যায় না।

Banglish: IELTS true false not given kivabe korbo? False ar not given er difference ki? Passage e evidence na thakle nijer knowledge diye answer diben na.

What exactly are you being tested on?

TRUE

The passage supports the whole statement.

FALSE

The passage directly disagrees with the statement.

NOT GIVEN

The passage neither confirms nor contradicts the statement.

This is an information question. Yes/No/Not Given assesses the writer’s views or claims, which is a different task.

Step-by-step solving method

  1. Read the entire statement. Identify exactly what must be proved, including qualifiers such as all, some, only, before, more than.
  2. Locate the relevant part of the passage. Names, dates and distinctive nouns may help you scan. Expect paraphrases rather than identical vocabulary.
  3. Compare the meaning. Does the passage support the entire statement, contradict it, or leave it unresolved?
  4. Choose NOT GIVEN only after checking the relevant context. Missing evidence is not the same as evidence of the opposite.
  5. Review the proof. Explain which sentence supports your choice. Move on rather than rereading the entire passage repeatedly.

Original practice: test all three answers

Practice passage (original): “The city library opened its new study hall in March. Visitors can use the hall until 8 p.m. on weekdays. The library also introduced a booking system for group rooms. The report did not evaluate visitors’ satisfaction.”

1. The new study hall opened in March. — See answer

TRUE. The passage explicitly states the new study hall opened in March.

2. The study hall closes at 6 p.m. on weekdays. — See answer

FALSE. The passage says 8 p.m. on weekdays, directly contradicting the statement.

3. Most visitors were pleased with the new booking system. — See answer

NOT GIVEN. The passage mentions the booking system but supplies no satisfaction data.

These are original educational examples, not official IELTS questions.

Common reasons students lose marks

  • Choosing TRUE because a repeated keyword appears without checking the claim.
  • Choosing FALSE when the passage is simply silent about a detail.
  • Ignoring important qualifiers such as “always” or “only”.
  • Making a decision from outside knowledge instead of passage evidence.
  • Reading the entire passage repeatedly instead of locating and understanding relevant evidence.

Questions students ask

False আর Not Given এর পার্থক্য কী?

False means the passage contradicts the statement. Not Given means the passage does not establish whether it is true or false.

IELTS true false not given kivabe solve korbo?

Age statement er exact claim bujhun, name/date diye relevant line locate korun, tarpor meaning compare korun. Same word paoa answer er proof noy.

If the passage says nothing about popularity, is 'very popular' false?

Not Given. The passage has not made a contradictory claim about popularity; it has provided no evidence for that statement.

Should I use general knowledge?

No. IELTS requires you to decide based on the passage, not information you happen to know.
