Finland does not let you use future part-time work to prove your first-year finances

For a first student residence permit, Migri requires at least €800 per month. If your studies last one year or more, you need €9,600 available for living costs, plus enough to cover unpaid tuition. Future work in Finland cannot be used to satisfy the initial income requirement.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Finland does not use one national Bangladesh HSC or CGPA cut-off.

Universities and universities of applied sciences decide academic eligibility. The Finnish National Agency for Education handles formal recognition in specific professional/public-sector contexts, not ordinary admission decisions.

HSC

HSC can support Bachelor admission.

English-taught Bachelor programmes generally accept foreign upper-secondary qualifications that give eligibility to higher education in the awarding country, but each programme sets its own rules.

UAS Bachelor

Universities of Applied Sciences often use entrance exams or certificate-based selection.

International UAS programmes can use the International UAS Exam, SAT or programme-specific selection methods.

University Bachelor

Research universities use their own admission criteria.

Some programmes use SAT, entrance exams, previous grades or other selection methods. Do not apply a UAS admission rule to a research university.

Masters

A relevant Bachelor is the starting point.

University Master programmes assess prior degree level, subject match, grades and language. UAS Master programmes often also require relevant work experience after the Bachelor degree.

Low CGPA

Programme selection can change the answer.

Because Finland has no one national CGPA threshold, a different university, UAS or subject match can create a more realistic route.

Recognition

Formal recognition is separate from university admission.

The Finnish National Agency for Education can recognise the level of a completed foreign higher-education degree where a formal recognition decision is legally needed.

UNIVERSITY VS UAS

Finland has two major higher-education sectors.

University

Research-oriented higher education

Universities focus on scientific research and academic degrees. They offer Bachelor, Master and doctoral education.

UAS

Professionally oriented higher education

Universities of Applied Sciences emphasise practical skills, industry cooperation and applied research. Their Bachelor programmes are strongly career-focused.

Master

The Migri student income requirement is €800 per month.

Future work cannot fund the first permit.

Migri states that you cannot secure the initial student income requirement with future employment in Finland. The living funds must already be available or covered by a qualifying grant/scholarship.

SCHOLARSHIPS

There is no general Finland Government scholarship for Bachelor or Master tuition.

University scholarships

Individual universities and UAS institutions offer tuition waivers and merit scholarships to non-EU students.

Living costs are usually your responsibility

Study in Finland warns that scholarships typically cover tuition partly or fully, not ordinary living expenses.

Beware misleading “fully funded Finland Government” ads

The official Study in Finland site explicitly says there is no governmental Bachelor/Master scholarship programme covering tuition, travel and living costs.

MIGRI RESIDENCE PERMIT FOR STUDIES

Finland grants higher-education degree students a continuous residence permit.

01

Receive admission

Obtain a place in a Finnish educational institution leading to a degree or other qualifying study.

02

Tuition & scholarship

Pay tuition or document scholarship/waiver and make sure any unpaid tuition is covered in your finances.

03

Prepare €9,600 living funds

For studies lasting at least one year, have at least €9,600 available for living costs.

04

Insurance

Arrange private insurance meeting Migri requirements unless your situation provides access to equivalent coverage.

05

Apply through Enter Finland

Complete the online residence-permit application and pay the 2026 online processing fee.

06

Prove identity / biometrics

Visit the Finnish mission or application centre instructed for Bangladesh to verify identity and provide biometrics.

07

Migri decision

Migri checks admission, finances, tuition, insurance, passport and other legal requirements.

08

Travel & registration

After approval, travel with the residence permit card and complete university plus local registration steps.

PERMIT COSTS · 2026

Finland increased study-permit fees from 1 January 2026.

Online first study permit

€600 from 1 January 2026.

Paper first study permit

€750 under the 2026 Migri fee schedule.

Application fee for higher education

€100 for applicants outside the EU/EEA/Switzerland applying to Finnish higher education.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Admission documents and Migri documents should be prepared separately.

Academic admission

SSC/HSC or prior degree certificates and transcripts, English evidence, entrance exam/SAT where required, CV, motivation, references and programme-specific documents.

Residence permit

Passport, admission, proof of tuition payment or scholarship, €9,600 living-fund evidence for one year, insurance and other attachments requested in Enter Finland.

Financial evidence

Funds should be genuinely available to you. If you use scholarship or grant funding for living costs, include official evidence stating the amount and period.

Translations / legalisation

Follow Studyinfo/university and Migri instructions for translations, certified copies and legalisation where applicable.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Family members of higher-education degree students can receive continuous residence permits.

Who can qualify?

Spouse, registered partner and children under 18 are among the family members who can apply under Finland’s family-ties rules.

Family needs additional money

Migri applies a separate family income requirement. In the Helsinki metropolitan area, Migri’s example for a student, another adult and two children totals €2,500 per month.

Family members can work

A residence permit issued on the basis of family ties gives an unrestricted right to work and study in Finland.

A permit for higher-education families

Family members of degree students in higher education are issued a continuous A permit, though the first family permit may be granted for up to one year.

WORK WHILE STUDYING

Students can work an average of 30 hours per week.

30 hours/week average

The limit is calculated as an average over the year. Some weeks can be higher if the annual average remains within the rule.

120 hours/month average

Migri also explains the rule as about 120 hours per month or 1,560 hours per year.

Full-time holidays can be possible

You may work full-time during holidays if the overall annual average still remains within the 30-hour rule.

Degree internships and thesis work

Work that is formally part of the degree, such as credited internship or diploma work, is not subject to the ordinary 30-hour limitation.

HEALTHCARE & REGISTRATION

Your residence status and municipality registration affect healthcare access.

Private insurance before arrival

International students commonly need qualifying private insurance for the residence permit. Coverage requirements depend on the expected duration of study.

Municipality of residence

After arrival, register with the Digital and Population Data Services Agency where eligible. A municipality of residence can give access to local public healthcare services.

PERMANENT RESIDENCE · 2026

Finland changed permanent-residence rules on 8 January 2026.

Several application paths now exist

Permanent residence depends on the type and length of continuous residence plus integration, income, work-history or Finland-degree conditions depending on the pathway.

Finnish degree can matter

Migri now includes a specific pathway for certain higher-education degrees completed in Finland, subject to the other permanent-residence requirements.

Do not market this as automatic PR

A Finnish degree or job-search permit does not automatically give permanent residence. The applicant must meet the pathway requirements in force at the time of application.

ARRIVAL

Residence permit approval is only the beginning of Finnish setup.

Before departure

Passport, residence-permit card, admission, tuition and scholarship evidence, insurance, housing and university arrival instructions.

University registration

Complete enrolment, student account, orientation and student-union/UAS services.

Personal identity & municipality

Complete Digital and Population Data Services Agency registration where needed and apply for a municipality of residence if eligible.

Working

Keep track of your annual average hours and Finnish tax setup before taking employment.
