Can Australia realistically fit your profile?

Australia does not use one national academic cut-off. Your route depends on study level, institution, subject, English, academic history, budget and visa logic.

HSC

Direct Bachelor or pathway?

Some universities accept Bangladesh HSC directly at specified GPA levels. Others ask for foundation, diploma or prior tertiary study.

See pathways

Credit is case by case.

Duration, awarding body, subjects and relevance matter. Credit transfer should never be promised before formal assessment.

Universities can look at degree recognition, CGPA, prerequisites, subject match and sometimes professional experience.

BANGLADESH ACADEMIC PATHWAYS

Your current qualification can lead to more than one route.

Final admission is always university and course specific.

After HSC

Possible routes include direct Bachelor, Foundation, Diploma/college pathway, or prior tertiary study depending on the institution.

Examples: Macquarie currently recognises Bangladesh HSC with GPA 4.5/5.0 as one undergraduate route. UOW publishes HSC 5.0 for some direct Bachelor bands, with lower results potentially using pathway options. These are examples, not national rules.
After a Bangladesh Diploma

Universities assess qualification duration, awarding body, academic content and relevance. Some applicants may enter Bachelor programs with credit; others may start earlier in the degree.

After a Bachelor

Masters admission commonly assesses whether the degree is recognised, CGPA/percentage, prerequisites, course relevance, English and sometimes work experience.

Research Masters or PhD

Expect more emphasis on proposal, thesis/research background, publications, research methods and supervisor fit. Research applicants also have different work-rule and 485-age considerations.

BANGLADESH DEGREE RECOGNITION

“Section 1, 2, 3” exists in some provider frameworks — not as one universal Australia ranking.

Some Australian providers distinguish Bangladeshi qualifications by institution section or qualification length. We should use those classifications only where the provider publishes or applies them, not invent a national LizOn ranking.

What can change by provider

Institution category, degree length, honours status, result, discipline and the qualification level being applied for.

What LizOn should show

Provider-specific examples and formal university assessment. The future university matcher can store recognition logic provider by provider.

LOW CGPA · GAP · SUBJECT CHANGE

The explanation has to make academic and career sense.

Low CGPA

Helpful factors can include stronger recent study, relevant work experience, a realistic university choice and a coherent progression.

Study Gap

Useful evidence can include employment letters, salary records, business documents, training, family circumstances or other evidence explaining the period.

Changing Subject

A field change is not automatically disqualifying, but it needs a credible reason for why the new course now makes sense for your skills and career.

STUDY

Choose the field, then the university.

Course structure, accreditation, English, tuition, campus and intake can matter more than a general university ranking.

Technology

IT · Cybersecurity · Data · AI

Compare prerequisites, specialisation depth, placement opportunities and professional recognition where relevant.

Business

Business · Accounting · Finance · Analytics

Compare accreditation, majors, internships and quantitative prerequisites.

Engineering

Civil · Mechanical · Electrical

Check mathematics/science prerequisites and Engineers Australia accreditation where relevant.

Health

Nursing · Public Health · Allied Health

Registration-linked courses may carry higher English, placement and compliance requirements.

Education

Teaching · Education

Teacher-registration linked programs can have higher English and placement requirements.

Built Environment

Construction · Project · Architecture

Accreditation, portfolios and practical industry components vary by course.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Understand what the scholarship actually pays for.

University

Merit tuition scholarships

Can be automatic or competitive. Amount, duration, renewal and faculty restrictions vary.

Government

Australia Awards

Country-specific government scholarships with eligibility and return-home obligations for relevant rounds.

Research

Research Training Program

Universities can award fee offsets, stipends and allowances to eligible research Masters and PhD candidates.

Never stop at “up to 50% scholarship.” Check percentage, duration, renewal conditions, eligible courses and whether it is automatic.

ACCOMMODATION

Rent depends on city and accommodation type.

Use current local listings or the official Study Australia cost tools before committing. Static Australia-wide rent claims become outdated quickly.

Shared room / share house

Usually the lowest-cost common student option, with rent varying heavily by suburb and city.

Student residence

Purpose-built student accommodation can bundle utilities and facilities but may cost more than a shared private rental.

Private studio / apartment

More privacy, generally a substantially higher weekly budget plus bond and setup costs.

Official cost-of-living calculator ↗
OSHC

Health cover is a visa requirement, not travel insurance.

Approved insurer groups

Current government material identifies Allianz Care Australia, Bupa Australia, CBHS International Health, Medibank/ahm and nib.

What basic OSHC does

It contributes to eligible medical and hospital services under the policy rules. Exact benefits, waiting periods and exclusions vary.

Compare before accepting

You do not have to assume the university-recommended policy is automatically the best value for your needs.

GENUINE STUDENT

GS is evidence-backed reasoning, not a decorative SOP.

Home Affairs looks at your circumstances, course logic, understanding of the provider and the value of the study to your future.

Why this course?

Show how it connects to your previous education, skills, experience and future direction.

Why this provider and Australia?

Understand the course, provider, location and why this option makes sense compared with realistic alternatives.

What is your situation in Bangladesh?

Employment, business, income, family and economic circumstances may be relevant. Evidence should support the story.

What value does the course add?

The cost and academic progression should make sense in relation to your future career and circumstances.

VISA COSTS

Do not call AUD 2,500 your “total visa cost.”

Student visa base charge

AUD 2,500 from 1 July 2026 for the base application charge.

Biometrics

If Home Affairs requests biometrics, Bangladesh collection centres include Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet. Service fees are separate and can change.

Medical

Medical examination costs depend on the approved panel clinic and required examinations. Follow HAP/request instructions.

Other costs

OSHC, English testing, police evidence where required, translation and other profile-specific expenses sit outside the base visa charge.

FAMILY / SPOUSE

Old family advice is now one of the biggest sources of misinformation.

Going alone

The standard pathway for most new applicants

Most new Student visa applicants from 2 October 2026 cannot include partner or dependent child unless an exemption applies.

Eligible exceptions

Check the current exemption categories

Examples include specified PhD, government scholarship and sponsored categories. Home Affairs rules must be checked at lodgement.

Current Student visa holders generally cannot later bring family through the Student visa subsequent entrant route under the October 2026 changes.

WORK & LIFE

Understand the rules before calculating income.

During study

48 hours per fortnight

Most Student visa holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session.

Research degrees

Different work rule

Masters by Research and Doctoral students have different work-hour treatment under current Student visa conditions.

Rights

Fair Work protections apply

International students have workplace rights, including pay and payslip protections.

EARNINGS CALCULATOR

Estimate gross earnings without pretending every student earns the same.

Current National Minimum Wage from 1 July 2026 is AUD 26.44/hour for an adult award-free employee, with a national casual minimum of AUD 33.05/hour. Many jobs are award-covered and may pay differently.

Weekly gross

COMMON STUDENT JOBS

The job can require more than a resume.

Hospitality

Café, restaurant, hotel and bar roles. RSA may be required for alcohol-related work depending on state. Barista skills can help.

Retail & customer service

Shops, supermarkets and service roles. Award rates and penalty rates may apply.

Warehouse & logistics

Picking, packing and distribution roles. Shift availability and physical requirements vary.

Construction support

A White Card is generally required to enter construction work environments.

Child-related work

WWCC / Blue Card or equivalent can be required depending on state and role.

Care / support roles

Qualifications, checks, training and role-specific compliance can apply. Never assume immediate eligibility.

TFN · TAX · SUPER

These rules are national, not “Sydney tax vs Perth tax.”

TFN

Apply for a Tax File Number so employers and the ATO can correctly handle your tax affairs.

ATO ↗

Income tax

Tax residency is separate from visa status. Overseas students on courses longer than six months are generally treated as Australian residents for tax, subject to the ATO residency test.

Superannuation

Eligible employees receive employer super contributions. Temporary residents may be able to claim DASP after leaving Australia and meeting the conditions.

Graduation can open options. It does not guarantee migration.

Age exceptions & regional option

Masters by Research and PhD applicants retain an under-50 age exception for the relevant 485 criterion. Eligible regional graduates may access a second Post-Higher Education Work stream.

REGIONAL AUSTRALIA

“Regional” does not mean a remote town.

Not regional

Sydney · Melbourne · Brisbane

These three major metropolitan areas are the main exclusions for migration regional classification.

Regional examples

Perth · Adelaide · Gold Coast · Canberra · Hobart

These and other designated locations can count as regional for relevant migration settings.

PR REALITY

Study is not permanent residence.

Skilled migration is a separate system involving occupation eligibility, skills assessment, points, state/employer options and invitation/application rules.

A safer way to think

Choose a course for skills and career value first. Then separately understand the migration pathways that may exist under the rules in force when you graduate.

65 points can be an eligibility threshold for points-tested skilled migration, but 65 does not guarantee an invitation.

STUDY AREAS & SKILLED OCCUPATIONS

Do not call them “PR courses.”

Some fields can connect to occupations used in skilled migration frameworks, but occupation lists, skills-assessment standards, points and state criteria can change before graduation.

Nursing & health

Can connect to regulated occupations. Registration, English and skills requirements are critical.

Engineering

Can connect to engineering occupations, with skills assessment and professional requirements relevant.

ICT

Can connect to ICT occupations, but degree content, skills assessment and work history can matter.

Teaching & social work

Professional registration/assessment and higher English thresholds can be significant.

Construction fields

Occupation relevance depends on the exact qualification and skills-assessment pathway.

Career first

A course should still make sense even if migration policy changes before graduation.

ARRIVAL

The guide should not stop when the visa is granted.

Before you fly

Passport, visa grant, CoE, OSHC, accommodation, airport plan, money/cards, medication, important documents, luggage and Australian border declarations.

First 3 hours

Immigration, baggage, customs, airport transport, SIM/eSIM if needed and accommodation check-in plan.

First 24 hours

Settle into accommodation, contact family, arrange local phone/data, groceries and local transport.

First 7 days

University orientation, student ID, timetable, bank, TFN, USI where needed, OSHC details and update your education provider with your Australian residential address within the required period.

FLIGHT PLANNING

Use live prices, not a permanent “Dhaka to Australia” number.

Airfares change by destination, airline, transit, baggage, season and booking time.

Routes worth comparing

Dhaka → Brisbane
Dhaka → Perth
Dhaka → Adelaide

Book only after you are comfortable with the visa and university timing. Always compare baggage allowance and transit conditions, not just the headline fare.
