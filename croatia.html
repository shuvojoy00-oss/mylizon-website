Major 2026 change: the student work ceiling is now 25 hours per week

Amendments to Croatia’s Aliens Act entered into force in June 2026. A third-country national holding temporary stay for university study can now perform student work, employment or self-employment for up to 25 hours per week without a separate stay-and-work permit. Older official English pages can still show 20 hours.

BANGLADESH ELIGIBILITY

Foreign qualifications must be recognised for continued study in Croatia.

Croatia separates secondary-school recognition for Bachelor entry from higher-education recognition for Master entry.

HSC

Bangladesh school credentials enter Croatia’s foreign secondary recognition process.

Applicants who completed secondary education abroad must have the qualification recognised as part of the Croatian undergraduate admission process.

Central admissions

Undergraduate recognition is linked to the national admissions system.

AZVO states that recognition of foreign secondary qualifications for undergraduate study is processed through the central admission procedure after registration on Postani Student.

Masters

The Croatian higher-education institution assesses the foreign Bachelor.

For graduate study, academic recognition of a foreign higher-education qualification is normally handled by the university, polytechnic or competent admissions office.

No national CGPA

The institution still decides selection.

Recognition confirms the academic value of the qualification. It does not create one Croatia-wide HSC percentage or Bachelor CGPA requirement.

Language

Croatian or English depends on the programme.

English-taught programmes set their own accepted IELTS, TOEFL or other proof. Croatian-taught programmes require the institution’s Croatian-language standard.

Regulated professions

Professional recognition is a separate process.

Medicine, teaching and other regulated professions can require recognition by the relevant professional body in addition to academic admission.

MONEY · 2026 FORMULA

The student residence minimum is €362.25 per month.

2026 student residence funds

Monthly student minimum€362.25
12-month equivalent€4,347
Return funds€869.40
Tuition / housingAdditional

The formula is tied to Croatia’s previous-year average salary.

The 2026 reference salary is €1,449. Students need 25% of that amount each month, while return funds are 60% of the same reference. Scholarship evidence can also be used under the rules.

This immigration minimum is much lower than a realistic student budget. Study in Croatia estimates average living expenses around €500–€800 per month.

TUITION

Third-country tuition is set by each Croatian institution.

Humanities / social sciences

Official Study in Croatia guidance gives a broad international range of roughly €1,100–€10,400 per year.

Natural / technical sciences

Typical published international ranges are about €1,000–€6,000 per year.

Medicine

International medical programmes commonly fall around €10,000–€12,000 per year.

Application fees

Institutions can also charge application fees, commonly around €60–€300.

Official tuition guidance ↗
SCHOLARSHIPS

Croatia has mobility and institutional funding, but not one universal Bangladesh scholarship.

Bilateral scholarships

Croatia offers bilateral government scholarships under agreements with selected countries. Eligibility changes by annual call.

Erasmus+ / CEEPUS

Mobility and exchange programmes can support eligible students, depending on the institution and programme.

University support

Some Croatian institutions offer tuition reductions, grants or merit support to international students.

TEMPORARY RESIDENCE FOR STUDY

Residence approval and the long-stay D visa are connected but different steps.

01

Admission & recognition

Complete the institution’s admission and the required recognition of your Bangladesh qualification.

02

Temporary residence application

Apply for temporary stay for the purpose of university study through the competent Croatian diplomatic/consular authority. Students who are legally in Croatia can also fall under the student exception allowing in-country filing.

03

Finance

Show at least €362.25/month under the 2026 formula plus €869.40 for return travel, or qualifying scholarship support.

04

Health insurance

Prepare comprehensive health cover acceptable for the residence process.

05

Bangladesh route

The Embassy of Croatia in New Delhi represents Bangladesh. Croatia also operates an official VFS Croatia Visa Application Centre in Dhaka for visa submissions.

06

Visa D where required

After the residence basis is approved, visa-required nationals use the Croatian long-stay visa D for entry and residence-card formalities.

07

Biometrics / residence card

After arrival, complete the police/residence-card steps and provide biometrics.

08

Address & HZZO

Register the Croatian address within the legal deadline and complete health-insurance formalities with HZZO where required.

RESIDENCE & VISA COSTS

Temporary stay, residence-card production and Visa D are separate charges.

Temporary stay decision

When the application is submitted at a Croatian police administration/station, the published temporary-stay administrative fee is €46.45.

Biometric residence card

Regular production costs €31.85, plus the applicable administrative charge. Accelerated card production is higher.

Long-stay Visa D

The current Croatian consular tariff is €93. VFS and courier charges in Bangladesh are additional and can change with exchange rates.

DOCUMENTS · BANGLADESH

Prepare recognition, admission and immigration documents as separate layers.

Bachelor recognition

SSC/HSC certificates and transcripts plus the documents requested by the Croatian central admissions/recognition system for applicants educated abroad.

Master recognition

Bachelor degree certificate, transcripts/diploma supplement and the university’s academic-recognition application where required.

Temporary residence

Passport, enrolment/admission, proof of purpose, funds, return funds, health insurance and other general temporary-stay documents.

First residence application

Under the general rules, a police/criminal-record document from the home country or a recent country of residence can be required for a first temporary-stay application.

Translation / authentication

Follow the institution, AZVO and Croatian authority instructions for certified translations and legalisation/apostille of Bangladesh documents.

SPOUSE & FAMILY

Family reunification can be possible with a student’s temporary stay.

Eligible relationship

Close family members of a third-country national holding Croatian temporary stay can potentially apply for temporary stay for family reunification.

Separate application

The spouse or child does not automatically receive residence from the student’s approval. Relationship, accommodation, health insurance and financial conditions must be documented under the family route.

Work rights depend on the family category

Do not assume an ordinary family permit attached to a student gives unrestricted employment. Check the exact work basis with MUP before planning family income.

WORK WHILE STUDYING · 2026 AMENDMENT

Students can now work up to 25 hours per week.

25 hours/week

The June 2026 Aliens Act amendment increased the maximum for third-country students from 20 to 25 hours per week.

Student Service

International students can use authorised Student Service intermediaries for temporary student jobs under Croatian student-work rules.

Employment or self-employment

The amended law also covers employment and self-employment within the 25-hour limit without a separate stay-and-work permit.

2026 minimum student rate

Study in Croatia lists the minimum net Student Service remuneration for 2026 at €6.56 per hour.

2026 Aliens Act amendment ↗
HEALTH INSURANCE

Third-country students need valid health coverage.

Residence application

Comprehensive private health insurance can be used by third-country students as proof of coverage for temporary residence where acceptable.

HZZO after residence

Croatian MUP instructs students to contact the competent Croatian Health Insurance Fund office after residence approval to determine their insurance status and obligations.

AFTER STUDY

Croatian graduates can receive up to one year for job search or starting a business.

Graduate temporary stay

Maximum length1 year
PurposeJob search / company / trade
QualificationCroatian higher-education qualification
ExtendableNo

This is a separate “other purposes” residence route.

Article 76 of Croatia’s Aliens Act allows a third-country national who completed a higher-education qualification in Croatia to obtain temporary stay for up to one year to look for employment, establish a company or register a trade.

WORK & LONG-TERM STAY

The graduate year is a bridge into Croatia’s employment residence system.

Residence and work permit

Once you obtain qualifying employment, the employer/graduate can move into the relevant Croatian stay-and-work permit procedure.

EU Blue Card

Highly qualified employment can use Croatia’s EU Blue Card when the qualification, contract and salary conditions are met.

Labour-market rules

Many ordinary work permits involve a Croatian Employment Service labour-market test or opinion, while specific categories and occupations can be exempt.

Long-term residence

Long-term or permanent status is separate and depends on qualifying residence history and other statutory conditions. A Croatian degree alone does not guarantee it.

CITIES

Tourism changes Croatia’s housing market dramatically by city and season.

Zagreb

Largest university and year-round employer market, especially for business, technology, healthcare and administration.

Split

Large coastal university city with medicine, engineering, tourism and maritime study, but strong seasonal housing pressure.

Rijeka

Port, engineering, medicine, logistics and university environment with generally less tourism pressure than Split/Dubrovnik.

Osijek

Lower-cost eastern Croatian student city with a broad public university and growing IT sector.

Dubrovnik

Tourism, maritime and international study opportunities, but among the most challenging cities for affordable housing.

Zadar

Smaller coastal university setting with humanities, maritime and tourism-related strengths.

ARRIVAL

Residence registration and health formalities should happen quickly.

Before departure

Passport, Visa D where required, temporary-stay approval, admission, insurance, finance and accommodation evidence.

Register address

Follow the current MUP deadline for reporting your Croatian residence/address after entry and any later change of accommodation.

Residence card

Provide biometrics and complete biometric residence-permit production with the competent police authority.

University / health / work

Complete enrolment, HZZO insurance formalities and Student Service registration before taking student work.
